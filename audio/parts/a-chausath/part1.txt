Mitauli ke jungle me ek khula mandir hai.  Chhat nahi hai.  Chaaron or 64 chhote-chhote kaksh hain, aur unme deviyan thin.  Ek kaksh hamesha khaali hai.  Gaon kehta hai wahan ek yogini ab bhi baithti hai, isliye us jagah par koi ghaas nahi ugti.  Khaali kaksh.   Main wahan do baar gaya.  Doosri baar maine dekha ki us kaksh me koi nahi, bas ek patthar ki chabootara hai.  Chhat nahi hone ka karan simple hai: mandir ki deewarein hi khaari chhat ka kaam karti hain, aur asli chhat kabhi bani hi nahi ya Sikh aakraman me gir gayi.   “Khaali kaksh” ki kahani us jagah ke ek asli fact se aati hai — maurtiyan aaj kahan hain, koi nahi janta.  Unme se kuch museum me hain, kuch nahi hain.  Jab koi cheez kho jaati hai, to uski jagah ka khali aasra kahani bana jaata hai.   Chausath Yogini parampara purani hai — 9th-sadi ke mandir, tantra aur yogini upasana se jude.  Gaon ki os ki kahani aur mandir ki khali jagaho me kahaniyan bhar deti hain.  Par yahan ka sabse bada sach yahi hai: ye imarat bachane layak hai, aur usse pehle use bhoot ki kahani ban jaana padega.
