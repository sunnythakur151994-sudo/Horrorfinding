Nizamabad ke ek gaon me kahani thi: raat ko ek aurat aati hai, jo aapke haath par mehndi lagati hai.  Mehndi lagwane ke baad haath kaaton wali baat kaafi heavy thi.  Gaon ke tel mein bachche darr ke maare mehndi lagane se hichakte the.  Mehndi kahan lagi.   Meri cousin ki shaadi thi.  Raat ko mehndi lag rahi thi.  Ek budhiya aayi, jise kisi ne bulaya tha — woh gaon ki mehndi lagane wali thi, aur unke haathon ki mehndi alag hi sooli thi.  Ek bachche ne usse dar ke haath kheench liye.   Baat yahi thi: “raat ki mehndi” wali kahani gaon ki us aurat ke khilaf thi.  Woh vidhwa thi, akeli rehti thi, aur shaadi ke mausam me gaon ki auraton se zyada mehnat karti thi.  Kahani ne uske haath ko darr bana diya, kyunki us aurat ko koi bulata to tha — par izzat nahi deta tha.   Us shaadi me maine uske haath dekhe.  Un par mehndi se zyada nishan the — sujan, dard, purani chot.  Aaj gaon me koi usse mehndi nahi lagwata.  Kahani jeet gayi, aur ek hunar-mand aurat bhookh ke liye taras gayi.
