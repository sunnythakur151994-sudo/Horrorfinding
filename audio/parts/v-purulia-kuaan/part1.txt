Purulia ke ek gaon ka kuaan saal bhar khaali rehta tha.  Log kehte the usme se raat ko paani tapakne ki awaaz aati hai, aur ek aurat ka chehra bhi dikhta hai.  Gaon ki auratein wahan se paani lena bandh kar chuki thin.  Kudai.   Panchayat ne kudai karwai.  18 foot par ek pipe mila — purani pipeline ka toota hua hissa, jisme raat ko paani ki boondein girti thin.  Awaaz wahi thi.  Kuein ke andar ki deewar par ek purana nishaan tha, jise log chehra samajhte the.   “Aurat ka chehra” wali baat us gaon ki ek aurat se judi thi, jo kai saal pehle wahan se paani late hue gir gayi thi.  Wo sach tha.  Par uske baad kuein ko band nahi kiya gaya, aur woh khula chhoda gaya — jahan bachche khelte the.   Us din ke baad kuein ke kinare par ek chhota board laga: “Dhyan rahein”.  Gaon ki auraton ne paani wapas lena shuru kiya.  Bhoot nahi gaya — bas uski jagah ek toota pipe aur ek khula kuaan aa gaya, jise kisi ne 20 saal tak theek nahi kiya.
