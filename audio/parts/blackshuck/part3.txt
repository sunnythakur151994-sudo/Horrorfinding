Uske ghar ka saadhaaran kutta uthkar uske paas aata hai, geele jooton ko soonghta hai aur phir apni jagah let jaata hai.  Aadmi uske paas baith jaata hai.  Kaafi der tak kuch nahi kehta.   Saalon baad bhi toofan aaye toh use church yaad aata hai.  Lekin ab woh ek baat apni kahani mein zaroor rakhta hai: “Us waqt meri aankhein band thin.”  Shayad isi chhoti imaandaari ki wajah se baaki kahani aur gehri lagti hai.  Hum uske saath andhere mein khade hote hain, bina yeh daawa kiye ki humein sab dikh raha hai.
