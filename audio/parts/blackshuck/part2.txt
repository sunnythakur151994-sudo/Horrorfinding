Us ek pal mein main jaanwar ko kho deta hoon.  Jab haath hataata hoon, mujhe apni saans ki awaaz bahut tez sunaai deti hai.  Jo ek pal mein nahi dekha gaya.   Baad mein sabke paas us raat ka ek alag hissa hai.  Kisi ne aankhein dekhin, kisi ne panje.  Kisi ko yaad hai ki woh darwaze tak gaya.  Kisi ko lagta hai woh achanak gaayab hua.  Main jab bolne lagta hoon toh mujhe apne hi bayaan mein khaali jagah milti hai.  Kadak ke waqt maine aankhein band ki thin.  Uske pehle bhi mujhe farsh saaf nahi dikh raha tha.   Lekin doosre log jab apni baat jodte hain, toh meri yaad bhi badalne lagti hai.  Main sochta hoon, shayad maine bhi woh dekha tha.  Yahin kahani dheere se meri aankhon se nikal kar sabki zubaan par chali jaati hai.  Ab woh ek aadmi ki yaad nahi, poore toofan ki shakal hai.  Darwaze par reh gaye nishaan.   Black Shuck ke baare mein alag riwaayatein hain: kabhi vinaash ka sanket, kabhi sunsaan raaste ka saathi, kabhi door se dikhkar gaayab hone waala praani.  Kisi puraane darwaze par nishaan ko uske panje keh dene se unka kaaran siddh nahi hota.  Puraana likhit bayaan bhi humein batata hai ki kya likha gaya; har vyakhya ko apne aap sach nahi banaata.   Mere kalpanik drashta ke liye raat tab khatam hoti hai jab woh ghar ka darwaza khatkhatata hai.  Andar se roshni aati hai.
