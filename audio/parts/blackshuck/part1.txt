Toofan shuru hua toh humne socha tha church ke andar surakshit rahenge.  Darwaza band hua, geele kapdon ki gandh patthar ki thandak mein mil gayi.  Bahar bijli chamakti toh khidki ek pal ke liye safed ho jaati.  Phir andhera pehle se gehra lagta.  Main bench par baitha apne haath ragad raha tha.  Tab darwaze ki taraf sabke chehre ek saath mud gaye.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Andar aaya andhera.   Black Shuck ki riwaayat se juda 1577 ka Suffolk toofan aur uska puraana likhit bayaan is kahani ki prishthabhoomi hain.  Neeche ka “main” ek kalpanik shrota-drashta hai.  Woh aitihasik gawahi dene nahi, us bhay ki kalpana karne ke liye hai jo logon ne ek kaale kutte ka roop dekar sunaaya.   Darwaza hawa se hila ya kisi ne dhakela, mujhe is kisse mein bhi theek se nahi dikhta.  Jo dikhta hai woh farsh ke paas ek kaali aakriti hai.  Uska aakaar roshni ke saath badalta hai.  Pehle lagta hai koi geela jaanwar ghus aaya.  Phir woh beech ke raaste mein rukta hai, aur mujhe uski aankhein dikhne ka bharam hota hai.  Koi mere paas prarthana ke shabd bol raha hai, baar-baar ek hi jagah se shuru karke.   Bijli ki kadak itni paas hoti hai ki main kaan dhak leta hoon.  Kuch girta hai.  Log hilte hain.
