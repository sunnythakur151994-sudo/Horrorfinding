Chhatarpur ke ek gaon ka talaab teen saal me sookh gaya.  Uske beech me ek chhota mandir nikla — aadha dhasa hua, jiske darwaze par patriyan ug aayi thin.  Gaon kehte the ki usme se raat ko ghanti aur rone ki awaaz aati hai.  Ghanti.   Maine wahan ja kar dekha.  Mandir ke andar ek lakeer me ek ghanti latki thi, jo hawa se hil kar deewar se takrati thi.  Bas.  Rone ki awaaz wahan ke shrign ke beech se aati hawa thi.   Par gaon ne mandir ko “bhoot ka mandir” kehna pasand kiya.  Ek purana “murti ka darr” bhi tha — mandir me rakhhi murti pani me gir gayi thi, uski shakal kharab ho gayi thi.  Us murti ko “bhoot” kehna aasan tha, use dobara sthapit karna nahi.   Humne gaon ke logo se baat ki.  Ek budhe ne kaha, “Hum to bas mandir ko bacha rahe the, kahin koi tod na de.”  Unki kahani ek suraksha kavach thi.  Aur unki baat sahi thi — uske baad wahan ASI ka board laga, aur mandir ko saaf kiya gaya.  Bhoot gayab nahi hue; sirf logon ne kaam karna shuru kiya.
