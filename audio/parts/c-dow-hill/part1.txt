Kurseong ke Dow Hill me ek purana school aur ek khaali building hai.  Yahan ki sabse mashhoor kahani: ek ladke ka sar seedhiyon par dikhta hai, aur ek “headmaster” ki aatma.  Iske saath ek asli hissa jodta hai — 1968 ke baad yahan kuch laashein mili thin, aur ek murder case darj hua tha.  Jaanch.   Hum wahan school ke caretaker se mile.  Unhone bataya ki raat me unhe bhi kuch mehsoos hota hai, par unhone kabhi kuch dekha nahi.  “Sar wali” kahani 1968 ki laashon ke baad bani.  Us waqt ka case aaj bhi khula hai — kisi ko sazaa nahi hui.   Building ka sach ye hai: purani lakdi ki seedhiyan, jo raat me thandi hone par karakti hain; ek chhat jisme barish ka paani jama hota hai; aur ek aisa corridor jahan bijli nahi hoti.  School ke bachche in sab ko “headmaster” ki kahani se jodte hain, kyunki school ki har imarat me koi na koi aisi kahani hoti hai.   Par ek cheez ko humein bhoot ki kahani ke naam par bhoolna nahi chahiye: 1968 me yahan kuch bachche mare the, aur unki maut ka koi jawab aaj tak nahi mila.  Us file ko “haunted school” ke naam se yaad rakhna unke liye insaaf nahi hai.  Woh ek khuli jaanch hai.
