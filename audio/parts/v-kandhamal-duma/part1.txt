Odisha ke Kandhamal me ek purani baat hai: ek chhota-sa jeev, jo raat ko aadmi ki chhati par baith jaata hai, jisse aadmi jag nahi paata.  Log use duma kehte hain.  Chhoti si baat hai, par gaon ki har aurat apne bachche ko seene se laga kar soti hai.  Woh raat.   Main solaa saal ka tha.  Graam me chacha ki maut ke baad unka ghar khaali tha.  Main wahan do raat sone gaya.  Pehli raat theek rahi.  Doosri raat main 3 baje uta aur mujhe laga koi upar baitha hai.  Main hil nahi paaya, saans tez, aur aawazein — apne ghar ke logon ki.  Maine aankh khuli rakhkar dekha.  Kuch nahi tha.   Main baad me darr kar bhaaga.  Chachi ne mujhe paani diya aur apne paas sula liya.  Chunki unhe maloom tha.  Unhone kaha ki wo duma nahi hoti, wo “raat ka dabaav” hai — jab dimaag aadhe jaage, aadhe soye ho, to jism hak jaata hai aur darr ke saath apni hi aawaz me bole huee baatein sunai deti hain.  Unki beti ko hota tha, aur unke papa ko bhi.   Pehli baar mujhe kisi gaon ki aurat ne bhooton ke baare me aisa seedha jawaab diya.  Kahaniyan chhup jati hain, par asli baat ghar ke andar chal rahi hoti hai.  Aaj bhi mujhe wo dabaav hota hai.  Main ab darr me chhat par nahi bhagta.  Main karwat leta hoon, ginnta hoon, aur apni maa ka naam leta hoon.
