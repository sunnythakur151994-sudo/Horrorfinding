Sikkim me ek chhota gompa pahadi par hai.  Raat ko cheekhne wali hawa, aur gompa ki ghanti bajti hai — hamesha ek hi waqt par.  Bhikshu mushkil se Hindi bolte the, par unhone ek line saaf boli: “Awaaz ban jaati hai.”  Teen baje.   Main wahan raat bhar ruka.  Baraf pighalti rahi.  Ek baje tak thand se kaanp gaya.  Bees minute pehle ghanti bajana shuru hua — pehle dheemi, phir saaf.  Maine neeche dekha.  Ek buddha bhikshu wahan baitha tha aur woh lambe sibre se khud ghanti baja raha tha.   Subah maine poocha.  Unhone kaha ki sadiyon se parampara hai: raat ke teen baje dhyan ke baad ghanti bajai jaati hai, taki pahad par bhatke hue logon ko raasta mile.  Har raat ek bhi bhikshu baithta hai.  “Khud baji” wali baat maine kisi bahane se kuch sune hue logon se suni thi — kyunki gompa ki ghanti ki awaaz pahad me sadiyon tak ghoomti hai.   Mujhe us raat joh darr laga tha, woh us ghanti se nahi tha.  Woh us pahad se tha jo itna bada tha ki laga ki koi poochh hi nahi sakta ki main yahan kyun hoon.  Subah maine unhe dhanyavaad kaha.  Unhone has kar kaha: “Yahan koi khud nahi bajti.  Yahan koi hai jo bajata hai.”  Aur woh sabse zyada human answer tha.
