Rohtasgarh qila pahadi par hai, aur usse jodne wali ek purani baat hai: rani ka khazana.  1576 me jaala ke baad qila tabah hua, aur kehte hain ek rani apne gehne lekar bhag gayi.  Raat ko wahan ek aurat khadi dikhti hai, jo kuch dhoondhti hai.  Jaanch.   Gaon ke log batate hain ki pichhle kuch saalon me logon ne raat me khudai ki.  Humein wahan koi khazana nahi mila, par humein un gaddhon ke nishaan milе — aur ek hai jo police ne bharaa tha.  Unhone ek aadmi ko pakda tha jisne qile ki deewar ka patthar ukhaad diya tha.   “Rani ka bhoot” ki kahani purani hai, par uska asli kaam aaj kuch aur hai: woh lootera se bachati hai.  Gaon ke buzurg kehte hain ki jo khazana dhoondhne raat me jaata hai, woh qile ko todta hai.  Jin nishaano ke bare me hum baat kar rahe hain, woh un logon ke hain jinhe patthar bechna tha.
