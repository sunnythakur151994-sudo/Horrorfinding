Kisse ke kuch versions mein kaha jaata hai ki pukaar ginne se dhokha pehchaana ja sakta hai; doosron mein niyam badal jaata hai.  Is kahani ke ghar mein bas itna sikhaaya gaya tha: pehle dekho ki tumhe bulaane waala hai kaun.  Kundi tak ka faasla.   Bahar se ek halki khurach aayi.  Shayad geeli daali darwaze se lagi thi.  Maine socha, agar koi sach mein madad maang raha ho?  Baba ghar par nahi the.  Kya woh laut aaye hain?  Dimaag ko ek theek wajah chahiye thi, aur woh jaldi-jaldi wajah bana raha tha.   Main uthkar darwaze tak gaya.  Neeche ki daraar se bas andhera dikh raha tha.  Kundi thandi thi.  Use chhoone se pehle maa ne meri kalai pakad li.  Zor se nahi.  Bas itna ki mera haath ruk jaaye.  Main uski taraf muda.  Usne mere paas aakar kaha, “Pehle yahin se poochho.”     Maine bahar awaaz di.  Jawaab nahi aaya.  Phir maine poochha ki kaun hai.  Sirf baalti mein boond giri.  Jo pukaar itni apni lagi thi, uske paas mere sawaal ka ek bhi jawaab nahi tha.  Hum darwaze se hat gaye.  Maa ne diya thoda paas kar liya.  Bachi hui raat humne baat karte hue bitaayi—khet ki, rishtedaaron ki, agle din ke kaam ki.  Woh chhoti baatein us waqt ghar ki deewaron jaisi lag rahi thi.  Subah ka aangan.   Ujale mein aangan chhota nikla.  Darwaze ke paas koi nishaan nahi tha.  Ek daali neeche jhuki hui thi; hawa mein hilne par lakdi se lagti thi.
