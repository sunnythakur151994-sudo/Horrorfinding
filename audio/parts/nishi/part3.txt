Maa ne use hata diya.  Main sochta raha ki aadhi neend, baarish aur pehle se suni kahani milkar kitna kuch bana sakti hain.  Mujhe aaj bhi nahi pata ki us raat maine kya suna hota—kyunki yeh Nishi ki lok-katha ka kalpanik anubhav hai, kisi gawah ki report nahi.   Lekin kahani ka woh pal aasaani se nahi jaata.  Andhere se koi tumhara naam le, toh tum awaaz se pehle rishta pehchaante ho.  Nishi ka darr usi chhote-se bharose mein rehta hai.  Kundi abhi band hai.  Tumhara haath us tak pahunch chuka hai.
