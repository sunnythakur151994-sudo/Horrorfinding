Maa ne mujhe pehli baar bulaaya toh main neend mein hi “haan” kehne waala tha.  Awaaz aangan se aayi thi—bilkul usi tarah, jaise woh subah doodh lene bhejte waqt bulaati thi.  Mera haath chadar se bahar nikla.  Phir bagal ki charpai ki rassi hili.  Maa mere paas so rahi thi.  Uski saans ki awaaz mujhe saaf sunaai de rahi thi.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Do baar mera naam.   Main kuch der seedha leta raha.  Neend ka ek hissa abhi bhi mujhse keh raha tha ki galat suna hai.  Bahar baarish band ho chuki thi.  Chhat se girti boondein aangan mein rakhi baalti par pad rahi thi.  Uske beech dobara mera naam aaya.  Is baar thoda dheere, jaise bulaane waali nahi chahti ki ghar mein koi aur jaag jaaye.   Maine maa ka kandha chhua.  Usne aankhein khole bina poochha, “Kya hua?”  Main jawaab nahi de paaya.  Mujhe uski awaaz abhi bahar bhi sunaai di thi.  Usne mera chehra dekha toh uthkar baith gayi.  Maine darwaze ki taraf ishaara kiya.  Hum dono chup ho gaye.   Bengal ki Nishi Daak ki lok-katha isi pehchaan se darr banaati hai.  Raat ki pukaar kisi ajnabi ki nahi hoti.  Woh kisi apne ki awaaz leti hai.
