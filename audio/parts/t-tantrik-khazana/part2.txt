Indian Treasure-Trove Act, 1878 kehta hai ki zameen mein chhupa hua maal milne par jise mile, use Collector ko likhit soochna deni hogi, khazana sarkari khazane mein jama karwana hoga, aur chhe mahine tak daavedaar bulaye jaate hain.  Jo log raat mein chupke se khazana nikaal le jaate hain, wo chor nahi, khazana chhupane wale mulzim hote hain.   Aur purani jagah par khudai?  Uske liye puratatva vibhag ki ijazat chahiye.  Zameen mein padha ek puraana sikka history hai, milkiyat nahi.
