Bundelkhand ke ek ghar mein ek saadhu aaye.  Unhone aangan mein khadi hokar aankhein band ki, phir kaha: “Neeche saat foot par ek tijori hai.  Do sau saal purani.  Is ghar ka purwaj ne dabai thi.”  Ghar wale ruk gaye.  Ghar ki chhat tapak rahi thi, beti ki shaadi aane wali thi, aur ek saadhu ne thoda sa aasmaan khol diya.  Dhandhe ka dhaancha.   Khazana-wali baat ka ek pattern hota hai, aur wahi pattern ise puraane dhokhon ki list mein rakhta hai.  Pehle “gyaan” muft milta hai — jagah, gehrai, tint.  Phir ek chhoti rukaawat aati hai: “khazana band hai, bandhan kholna hoga”.  Bandhan kholne ke liye pooja chahiye, pooja ke liye saamagri chahiye, saamagri ke liye paisa chahiye.  Paanch sau se shuru hokar pandrah hazaar, phir pachaas hazaar.  Har martaba “bas ek aur cheez” aati hai.   Is ghar mein wahi hua.  Khudai shuru hui, do martaba raat ka “muhurat” badla, ek “nishan” ke liye bakri chahiye thi.  Aadhe raste par aangan ki zameen kamzor padi, deewar mein daraar aayi.  Saadhu ne kaha “bandhan takleef de raha hai, humein agla step lena hoga”.  Ghar wale wahi ruk gaye — kyunki agla step tab shuru hota hai jab pehla paisa doob chuka hota hai.  Khazana mila to kya hota.   Aur ek baat jo koi nahi batata: agar khazana sach mein milta, to bhi wo poora unka nahi hota.
