Mahabalipuram ke kinare samundar ke paas ek mandir aadha doob kar khada hai.  Gaon kehte the ki us shehar me ek doosra shehar paani ke andar hai, jise kisi ne nahi dekha.  Phir 2004 ki tsunami ne lehar ko pichhe hataya, aur ek minute ke liye kuch patthar dikhe.  Lehar ke baad.   Us din gaon ke log samundar tak bhag gaye.  Unhone chhote se patharon ki deewarein dekhi — ek mandir ka base, aur kuch chhote gopuram jaise dhanche.  Lehar laut aayi aur sab wapas doob gaya.  Ek puratan mandir jo paani me tha, woh asli tha.   Pallava zamaane me Mahabalipuram ek port tha.  Samundar 1300 saal me unchi ho gaya, aur kai imarat doob gayi.  “Paani me shehar” wali kahani ek dam invention nahi thi.  Iske alaawa vahin ke ASI/archaeology survey ne paani ke neeche dhanche darj kiye hain.   Gaon wale ab bhi kehte hain ki tsunаmi wale din “purana Mahabalipuram” ek baar dikha tha.  Main us par viswas kar sakta hoon, kyunki sach abhi bhi paani ke andar hai.  Ye bhoot ki kahani nahi — ek doobi hui zameen ki kahani hai, aur ek samundar ka hisaab.
