Pandav van me so rahe the.  Unke paas hathiyaar nahi the, sirf thakan thi.  Us raat Hidimba naam ka ek rakshas unhe dekh kar apni behen Hidimbi ke paas gaya: wahan sote hue log hain, tu jaake unhe jagaa, main khaa loonga.  Do bhai, ek raat.   Hidimbi gayi.  Usne unhe dekha, aur usne unhe jagaaya nahi.  Usne Bhima ko uthaya aur kaha — bhaag jao.  Bhima nahi bhaaga.  Us raat Hidimba aaya, aur dono bhaiyon ke beech yudh hua.  Bhima ne use maara.  Aur us rakshas ki behen ne unka haath pakad liya.   Mahabharat yahan ajeeb ho jaata hai.  Hidimbi ka Bhima se ek putra hua — Ghatotkacha — jo aage Kurukshetra me Pandavon ke liye lada.  Jo raat ek daanav ki kahani se shuru hui thi, woh ek putra ki kahani ban gayi.  Rakshas ko sirf rakshas samajhna kahani ko chhota kar dena hai.   Mere ek dost ne ise padhkar kaha: “Yahan sabse zyada insaan Hidimbi hai.”  Main usse sehmat hoon.  Bhookh wale ghar me ek ladki thi jisne hunger wale bhai se zyada insaaf dikha diya.  Ye sirf ek purani kahani nahi — ye aaj bhi ghar-gali me hoti hai.
