Kochi ke ek purane chawl ki teesri manzil khaali thi.  Seedhiyan lambi thin, chhat gili.  Gaon wale kehte the wahan ek budhiya har raat phool rakhti hai — kisi ke liye, jise koi nahi jaanta.  Main chadha, kyunki mujhe phool nahi dekhne the, malba dekhna tha.  Phool aur chhat.   Wahan ek kamre me khuli khidki thi, aur uske paas ek martban me taaza genda rakhe the.  Kamra saaf tha.  Main darr gaya.  Neeche utar kar maine chawkidaar ko poocha.  Woh hausa — uski maa pichhle saal wahi rehti thi, aur woh falle hue kamre me roz phool rakhta tha.   Us chawl ki chhat 2019 me gir gayi thi.  Wahan rehne wale logon ko nikaal diya gaya tha.  Kuch log nahi gaye.  Ek budhiya rehti thi, par gaon ne uske bare me kuch nahi socha, sirf “wo log” keh diya.   Aaj main samajhta hoon ki chawl ka bhoot koi nahi tha.  Ek beta tha jo apni maa ke kamre me phool rakhta tha, aur ek gaon tha jo usse darr ke naam se bulata tha.  Jahan imarat girti hai, wahan kahaniyan pehle aa jaati hain — aur asli log chhup jaate hain.
