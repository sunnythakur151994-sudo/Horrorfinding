Alipore Jail ab ek museum hai.  Kohinoor jail ke cell ki deewarein, phansi ka kamra, aur kai azadi ke senaniyon ki yaadein.  Staff kehta hai raat ko ek cell me kuch awaaz hoti hai — ek aadmi ke chalne ki, sandiyon wali.  Ek cell me aaj bhi ek kursi rakhi hai.  Jaanch.   Hum us cell me gaye.  Andar thanda sa tha.  Wahan ek purana fan tha jo tej chal raha tha.  Staff ne humein bataya ki cell me raat ko awaaz hoti hai — par usne khud bataya ki woh awaaz purane fan se aati hai, jo cell ke darwaze tak chal raha hota hai.   Jail ki asli kahani awaazon se badi hai.  Yahan 1908 me ek political case chala, aur yahan kai logon ko phansi hui.  Yahan ki diwarein un logon ke naam rakhti hain.  Museum ke ek guard ne (jo 30 saal se wahan tha) mujhse kaha: “Beta, yahan koi bhoot nahi.  Yahan sirf history hai.  History kaafi hai.”   Aaj Alipore Jail ek museum hai — log aate hain, tasveerein lete hain, aur “cell 13” ki kahani sunte hain.  Wahin ke record me cell 13 kisi death se judi nahi hai.  Jo hai woh ek purana fan, ek lamba corridor, aur un logon ki yaad hai jinhone apna sab kuch diya.
