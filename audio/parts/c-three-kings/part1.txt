Cansaulim ke Three Kings Church pahadi par hai, aur wahan se poora Goa dikhta hai.  Kahani kehti hai ki yahan teen padri rehte the, aur ek ne baaki dono ko zeher de diya kyunki unhe ek khazana chahiye tha.  Uske baad uski aatma wahan bhatakti hai, aur ghanti raat me bajti hai.  Jaanch.   Hum shaam ko chadhe.  Church band thi.  Ek chowkidaar wahan tha.  Unhone bataya ki ghanti hawa se bajti hai, kyunki uska ek hissa dhila hai.  Us raat humne ghanti suni — hawa tez thi.  Usne ye bhi bataya ki log raat me church ke andar ghuskar “proof” dhoondhne aate hain, aur ek baar kisi ne darwaza tod diya.   Teen padri wali kahani gaon ke logon me hai, par church ke record me aisi koi hatya nahi hai.  Jo hai woh ye: church 1600s me bana, pahadi par bijli girती hai, aur raat me wahan koi nahi rehta.  Khazane ki kahani barso se chalti hai, kyunki ek baar kisi ne wahan khudai ki aur kuch nahi mila.   Us pahadi par sabse bada khatra church ya bhoot nahi hai — rasta hai.  Raat me utarte hue humne ek doosre ko pakad kar chalna pada.  Goa ke pahadi churchon par roz log phisalte hain.  “Bhoot” wali kahani kaafi logon ko raat me wahan jaane se rokti hai.  Shayad wahi uski sabse achhi duty hai.
