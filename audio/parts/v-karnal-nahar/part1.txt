Karnal ke paas nahar ke kinare raat ko ek roshni chalti dikhti thi.  Log kehte the woh ek mazdoor ki aatma hai jo nahar me doob gaya tha, aur apna theka dhoondhta rehta hai.  Bachche wahan nahi jaate the.  Roshni ka raasta.   Humne do raat dekha.  Pehli raat kuch nahi.  Doosri raat roshni aayi — dheemi, chalti hui.  Hum paas gaye.  Woh ek budha aadmi tha, haath me torch, jo raat me kinare ka kachra uthata tha.  Uska theka sach me tha — nagar nigam ka safai ka kaam, raat ki shift.   “Doobne wala mazdoor” wali baat us nahar me sach me hui thi — 1990s me ek mazdoor doob kar mara, aur uska koi case darj nahi hua.  Uske baad nahar ke kinare roshni ki kahani chali.  Jo roshni main dekh raha tha woh ek aur mazdoor thi, jo usi jagah par kaam kar rahi thi.   Us raat humne uske saath chai pi.  Unhone kaha ki unhe nahar ka woh kissa yaad hai, aur woh usi din se kaam par jaate waqt apna naam likh kar jaate hain.  “Kahani se darr nahi lagta.  Lagta hai ki koi mera naam bhi nahi jaanta.”  Bhoot ki kahani ne ek mazdoor ko yaad rakha, aur ek doosre ko bhula diya.
