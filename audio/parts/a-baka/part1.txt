Ek gaon me rehna hi ek shart ke saath tha: har hafte ek niwala paas jaata tha — ek insaan, bhel ke saath, rakshas ke liye.  Us hafte ek haath se doodh maangne wali maa ki baari thi.  Uska beta chhota tha.  Woh sirf apne bachche ke liye maafi maang rahi thi.  Ek aadmi khud chala.   Bhima bhookhe panda ban kar gaya.  Woh gaon ke un logon ke paas gaya jo rakshas se darte the, aur unhone use bhel diya.  Woh rakshas ke paas gaya, aur wahan ek chhota bachcha mila jo apna bhel kha raha tha.  Bhima ne use kaha: ghar ja, main bhookh noch loonga.   Rakshas aaya.  Uska bhoj khali tha.  Bhima ne usse lada, usse maara, aur gaon ko us shraap se aazad kiya.  Gaon ne use jaa kar dekha, aur us saal koi niwala nahi gaya.  Yeh katha ek asli sawaal chhodti hai: gaon ne kisi ko bhejna band nahi kiya tha.  Unne sirf ek badal chuna tha.   Mujhe ye katha sunate hue ek budhi ne kaha: “Ye kissa kahta hai ki jo gaon apna darr bujhaane ke liye kisi ek ko bhejta hai, woh gaon nahi — woh bhediya hai.”  Main ye line bhoolta nahi.  Kahani purani hai, sawaal naya hai.
