Mahishasura ke paas var tha: koi bhi purush usse nahi maar sakta.  Woh bhainsa ban jaata, insaan ban jaata, aur jab bhi marne ke kareeb aata, bhainsa ban jaata.  Devta har yudh har gaye.  Mandiron me ghantiyaan shuru ho gayi — aur uske baad ek aawaz aayi jo unhone kabhi nahi suni.  Mandir ke dwar par.   Devi ki katha kehti hai ki Brahma, Vishnu aur Mahesh ke tejas se ek aandhi uthi, jo ek aurat ke roop me khadi ho gayi.  Uske das haath, ek sinha, aur ek aisi shakal jise dekhkar asur ki sena ke kuch log hi bhaag gaye.  Woh dwar par khadi thi, aur usne kaha: Main hoon.   Yudh nau din chala.  Kahani kehta hai ki aakhri din Mahishasura bhainsa ban kar aaya, Devi ne usse patak diya, aur uske baad ek insaan ke roop me aaya — Devi ne uska sir kaat diya.  Aaj Durga Puja ka nau-raat usi yudh ka hain, aur Dashami me Doorga pratima visharjit hoti hai.   Meri dadi ise sunate hue kehti thi: “Bhaiyya, bhainsa nahi, sabse bada dushman hamesha woh hota hai jise koi maar nahi sakta.”  Yahan Devi ek aurat thi, jo us var ko todi jise koi purush nahi tod sakta tha.
