Delhi ke Ridge forest me Malcha Mahal ek toota hua shikar-gah hai.  1984 me yahan ek khandaan aaya — aur unhone dawa kiya ki woh Awadh ke shahi khandaan ke waris hain.  Woh andar rehte the, bahar nahi jaate the, aur unke ghar me bijli nahi thi.  2017 me aakhri waris ki maut hui.  Jaanch.   Yahan “bhoot” ki kahaniyan 2017 ke baad shuru hui.  Aas-paas rehne wale kehte hain raat ko wahan roshni dikhti hai.  Humne forest department ke guard se baat ki.  Unhone bataya ki jungle me raat ko do-chaar log aate hain — shikar, ya kuch aur.  Aur kuch roshniyan unki torch hoti hain.   Asli kahani us khandaan ki thi: ek maa, ek beti aur ek beta, jinke paas paisa nahi tha, bijli nahi thi, aur koi unse milne nahi aata tha.  Woh logon se darte the, isliye jungle me rehte the.  Unke marne ke baad ek kamre me unke kapde, kuch tasveerein aur ek bister mila.   Uss jagah par bhoot ki kahani sunane se pehle ye samajhna zaroori hai: woh teen log asli the.  Unka akelapan asli tha.  Aaj jo log wahan raat ko jaate hain, unhe jo dikhta hai woh kisi ki yaad hai — aur us yaad ka izzat karna bhoot se darrne se zyada zaroori hai.
