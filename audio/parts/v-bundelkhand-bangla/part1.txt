Bundelkhand ke ek chhote station ke peeche aath railway bangale the.  Saat me station ke mazdoor rehte the.  Aathva khaali tha — parda latka, darwaza band, aangan mein neem.  Bachche kehte the wahan se aawazein nikalte hain.  Bade kehte the wahan koi nahi rehta kyunki chhat girne wali hai.  Raat ka gana.   Main baara saal ka tha.  Ek raat maine apni jhalak me dekha ki chat par koi kuch ga raha hai.  Awaaz dheemi, dhekhi hui.  Subah maine apne papa se poocha.  Woh chup rahe.  Dopahar ko woh mujhe us bangale ke saamne le gaye, darwaza khola, aur andar ek puraana pen chhodkar wapas laaye.   Us bangale me pehle ek signalman rehte the jo 1983 me widhaan hua tha.  Uske baad koi nahi aaya.  Par “aawazein” ki wajah mujhe papa ne dikhayi: chhat ke veem me tirchhi ratkadi lagi hui thi jo hawa me ek sarange jaisi aawaz karti thi.  Kisi ne use kabhi theek nahi karaya.   Ghar me gungunaahat aur darwaza khatkhataana — sabhi wahi ek tukda loha tha.  Par us me yakeen karne wali baat ye hai: ek pen chod dena hi wo kaam tha jo kisi ne kiya.  Bangla khaali nahi tha, par usme koi nahi tha — aur yahi asli baat har gaon ki khaali imaraton ke bare me sach hoti hai.
