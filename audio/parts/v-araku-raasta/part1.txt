Araku jaane wale driver ek baat zyada bolte hain: raat me us mod par ek aurat khadi hoti hai, haath hilati hai.  Gaadi rok kar wo gaayab ho jaati hai.  Humne roka.  Aur woh gaayab nahi hui.  Woh aurat.   Ghat road par barish ho rahi thi.  Woh safed saree me thi, sir ghuma hua, haath usi tarah.  Hum ruke.  Woh paas aayi aur boli — bus chhoot gayi, gaon tak chhodein.  Jaate hue woh chup rahi.  Ek gaon me utri aur ek ghar ki taraf chali gayi.   Ek hafte baad wahi khabar humein pahadi ke doosre gaon me mili: ek aurat jo raat ko khadi rehti hai, humesha safed saree me.  Kisi ne bhoot kaha, kisi ne “chudail”.  Sach ye tha ki woh ek ASHA worker thi jo gaon ke bachchon ko dawa dene ke baad chalti thi, kyunki bus ka aakhri time chala jaata tha.  Pahad me raat koi aur gaadi nahi milti, isliye gaon waalog ne unhe raat ke himaayat ke liye “wo” keh diya.   Main nahi kehta ki har aurat jo raat me haath hilaye insaan hai.  Main ye kehta hoon ki hum sirf unhe bhoot samajhte hain jo humari madad maang rahe hain.  Us raat ke baad driver ne apne gaadi meine ek sticker lagaya: “aurat ko chhod do, bhoot nahi hai.”
