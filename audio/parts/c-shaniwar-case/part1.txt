Shaniwar Wada ki awaaz Pune ki sabse mashhoor kahani hai.  “Kaka mala vachva” — Kaka, mujhe bachao.  Ye 1773 me ek 16 saal ke Peshwa ki aakhri pukaar thi.  Us raat ke baad qila jal kar tabah hua, aur aaj woh awaaz lautti hai.  Teen jagah.   Hum teen jagah gaye: Delhi Darwaza, mastani jharokha aur pacheh ka aangan.  Teen jagah hawa ka rukh alag tha.  Delhi Darwaza par hawa tez aati hai; wahan ek purana lohe ka gate hai, jo har jhonke par aawaz karta hai.  Uski aawaz ek pukaar jaisi lagti hai — khaas kar raat me.   Record kya kehta hai: 1773 ki hatya history hai.  Us raat ki “awaaz” ka koi record nahi hai — na koi video, na koi audio, jo verify ho sake.  Jo recordings internet par hain, unme se kisi ka source nahi hai.  Ek bahes me ek engineer ne bhi likha ki deewar ki aawaz ek pukaar jaisi goonjti hai.   Is file ka nateeja dukh bhara hai.  Us raat 16 saal ka ek ladka mara — uske chacha ne darwaza nahi khola.  Awaaz “aaj bhi aati hai” kehna us dukh ko ek ghost story bana dena hai.  Hum yahan rukte hain: 1773 me kuch hua tha.  Uske baad ka har aawaz kahani hai.
