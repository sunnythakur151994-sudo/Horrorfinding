Hailey Road ki Agrasen ki Baoli — 108 seedhiyan neeche, teen taraf deewarein, aur ek aisi khamoshi jo shehar ke beech ho.  Kaha jaata hai ki neeche paani ka rang kaala hai, aur wahan se ek aawaz aati hai jo kisi ko dhoondhti hai.  Netflix ki ek film ke baad yahan bheed lagne lagi.  Jaanch.   Hum subah 7 baje gaye.  Neeche paani hai — hara, sthir.  Uske paas khada hone par awaaz aati hai: apne hi pair ki khich, deewar se wapas.  Baoli ka dhancha aisa hai ki awaaz 3-4 baar laut kar aati hai.  108 seedhiyan bahut hain — chadhte hue saans phoolti hai, aur dil dhadakta hai.   Film ke baad yahan raat me log aane lage the, aur kuch gir gaye.  ASI ne timing fix ki aur raat me band kar diya.  “Aawaz dhoondhti hai” wali baat awaaz ki goonj hai, aur “kaala paani” — paani ke andar anjan jagah se aata hai, ghaas aur sadiyon ki mitti se.   Baoli ka asli jadoo ye hai ki 600 saal pehle yahan log paani lene aate the, aur unki awaazein inhi deewarein se takrati thin.  Aaj wahi deewarein hamari awaaz lautti hain.  Kabhi kabhi bhoot ki kahani ek itni achhi tarah bani hui imarat hoti hai ki woh khud hi kirdaar ban jaati hai.
