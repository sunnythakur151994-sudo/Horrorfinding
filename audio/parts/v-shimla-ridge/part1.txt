Shimla ke Ridge par shaam ke 5:40 baje ek lamba saaya gaon ke raste par chalta dikhta hai.  Log kehte hain woh ek angrez officer hai jo church ke paas hi mara tha.  Saaya hamesha ek hi waqt par aata hai.  5:40.   Main wahan do shaam khada raha.  Pehli shaam kuch nahi.  Doosri shaam saaya aaya — aur main samajh gaya ki woh ek post ke saaye ka hai jo church ki ghanti se hilti hai.  Ghanti ke saath ek hawa ka jhonka aata hai, aur ek board ka saaya sadak par girta hai.   “Angrez officer” wali baat us church ke register me hai — 1880s me ek officer ki maut hui thi, par unhe Shimla me dafnaya gaya, is sadak par nahi.  Gaon ne do alag baatein jod di: ek purana angrez, aur ek roz aane wala saaya.   Shimla jaise shehron me purani imaraton ka gudha hua hisaab hota hai.  Wahan saaya, ghanti aur hawa sabse purane kirdaar hain.  Unhe bhoot kehna aasan hai.  Unhe samajhna zyada maza deta hai — aur yahi is site ka kaam hai.
