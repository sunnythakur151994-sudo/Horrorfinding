Panchavati me ek jhopdi thi.  Ram, Sita aur Laxman wahan rehte the.  Ek din Shurpanakha aayi — Ravan ki behen.  Usne Ram ko dekha aur chaaha.  Woh usse maangi.  Ram ne inkaar nahi kiya; unhone mazaak me Laxman ki taraf bhej diya.  Laxman ne Sita ki taraf bhej diya.  Dono ne mazaak kiya, aur uska roop us raat ujar gaya.  Badle ka hisaab.   Shurpanakha ne Ravan ke paas jaakar apne kate hue roop ki baat kahi.  Ravan ne ek baat aur suni: Sita.  Usne Sita ko chhina.  Van me darr ka ek dohra pautaada bana — Ram ne Laxman se Sita ki raksha ko kaha, aur Laxman ne Sita ko akele chhod kar Ram ke paas jana pada, kyunki kisi ne nikala nahi tha.   Kahani me sabse ajeeb baat ye hai: Shurpanakha ke kate roop se Ravan ka yudh shuru hua.  Aur Ravan ka yudh uski galti se nahi, uski behen ke chot se shuru hua.  Ram ka mazaak bhi ek anjaam bana.  Kahani ka koi bhi kirdaar saaf-saaf sabse achha nahi hai.   Hum aksar is katha ko sirf “asur ne kiya” ki tarah sunate hain.  Par kahani jitna brahman ek insaan ka mazaak aage jaakar kitna bada khatra ban sakta hai, utna hi khatra dikhata hai.  Isliye ise padhte hue main una kirdaaron se darta hoon jo apne mazaak ko mazaak hi samajhte hain.
