Jaisalmer se door ek chhota gaon reet me dhansta ja raha tha.  Har saal ek ghar khaali hota tha, aur log shehar chale jaate the.  Jo bache the, unke paas ek kahani thi: raat ko gaon ke bahar ek aurat khadi rehti hai, aur jo usse baat karta hai, wo bhi chala jaata hai.  Woh aurat.   Main ek raat wahan ruka.  Bahar ek aurat khadi thi.  Maine paas ja kar poocha.  Woh bacchon ko dudh pilane wali thi, jo raat 9 baje tak har ghar jaati thi.  Gaon me usse “wo” kehna asaan tha, kyunki woh akeli thi aur baahar khadi rehti thi.   Gaon khaali hone ki asli wajah paani ki tanki thi — jo ab 6 mahine me ek baar bharti thi.  Aur sabse pehle jaane wale wahi the jo mazdoori ke liye shehar gaye.  Unke jaane ke baad khali ghar the, aur khali gharon ke beech ek akeli aurat — aur kahani.   Us raat us aurat ne mujhe chai pilai.  Unhone kaha ki gaon me koi bhoot nahi hai, par logon ko kahani chahiye.  “Bhoot ki wajah se dariya nahi bharti, beta,” unhone kaha.  “Tanki bharti hai to gaon bachta hai.”  Wo ek line poore Rajasthan ke khaali gaon ki sach hai.
