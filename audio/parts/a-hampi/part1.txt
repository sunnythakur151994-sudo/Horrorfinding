Hampi me aaj bas patthar hain.  Mandir, baazaar, kamal ka sarovar.  Sulah ka ek hi sach hai: 1565 me vijayanagar sena har gayi, shehar tabah hua, aur log kabhi lauta hi nahi.  Us raat ko wahan kisi ne paayal ki aawaz suni.  Aawaz ka rukh.   Main Tungabhadra ke kinare baitha tha.  Aawaz aayi — malhaar ki ghantiyaan, dholak.  Main chal kar bhitar gaya.  Kuch nahi.  Phir ek gate ke paas mera torch kisi patthar par gira, aur ek chhoti aawaz aayi — bandar.  Hampi me hazaaron bandar hain, aur unke pair patharon par aise ghoomte hain jaise ghungroo.   “Sulah ka bhoot” kahani Hampi ke veeran bazaar se aati hai.  Par asli baat ye hai ki Hampi ka vinaash ek shraap nahi tha — ek yudh tha.  Us shehar me kaam, vyapaar, mithai aur bhaasha thi.  Un sab ki yaad patthar me hai.   Raat ko wahan sabse bada aap ka saaya hota hai.  Main ek baar apni hi chhaya dekh kar ruk gaya.  Us raat ke baad main bhtt se zyada us shehar ke logon ke bare me sochta raha, jinhone 1565 me apne ghar chhore the.  Unke paas shraap nahi tha.  Unke paas sirf ek raat thi.
