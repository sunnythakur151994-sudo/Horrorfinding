Peeche se aayi aahat pehli baar mujhe bazaar se lautte hue sunaai di.  Main ruki toh woh bhi ruk gayi.  Mudkar dekha, gali khaali thi.  Us raat maine ghar mein kuch nahi bataya.  Agli subah padosan ne kaha ki kisi aur ko bhi aisa laga tha.  Do baatein milin, phir unke beech ek naam aa gaya: Pichhla Bhoot.  Naam milte hi aahat zyada saaf sunaai dene lagi—chahe bahar kuch bhi na ho.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Darwaze band hone lage.   Yeh ek kalpanik gaon ki kahani hai, kisi darj police case ka punarnirmaan nahi.  Yahan log ek-doosre ko pehchaante hain, phir bhi afwaah ke liye kaafi jagah hai.  Kisi ne chhat par saaya dekha.  Kisi ne kaha ki uski choti ke paas kuch chhua.  Teesre ne daawa kiya ki ek aurat ke baal kat gaye.  Jab us aurat se poochha gaya, toh pata chala ki baat kisi doosre ghar ki thi.   Lekin sawaal jawaab se tez chal rahe the.  Shaam ko meri chhoti behen ne khidki band kar di.  Maa ne use khola, kyunki andar garmi thi, phir kuch der baad khud band kar diya.  Mujhe gussa aa raha tha ki kisi anjaani cheez ne hamaare ghar ka roz ka tareeka badal diya.  Saath hi main khidki ke paas baithna nahi chahti thi.  Kiski awaaz thi?.   Ek raat bahar halchal hui.
