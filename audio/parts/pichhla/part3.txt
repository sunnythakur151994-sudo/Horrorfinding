Ant mein ek ladki bas apna raasta dobara istemal karna seekhti hai.  Woh dhyaan rakhti hai, zaroorat par madad maangti hai, aur jo nahi dekha uske baare mein “nahi dekha” kehti hai.   Ab kabhi peeche aahat ho toh main mudkar dekhti hoon.  Darr ab bhi hota hai.  Bas main use kisi bekasoor ka naam nahi deti.
