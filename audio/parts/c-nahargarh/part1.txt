Nahargarh Fort raat me Jaipur par girta hua dikhta hai.  Usme ek mahal hai, jaise Madhavendra Bhawan, jisme 12 kamre hain.  Kaha jaata hai raat ko wahan ek cheekh sunai deti hai — ek dulhan ki, jise ek raja ke maarne se pehle chillaaya tha.  Koi record me nahi.  Jaanch.   Guard ne humein raat 9:30 baje baahar chala diya, par baithne diya.  Unhone bataya ki cheekh ki awaaz har barish me aaati hai — au ek khaas jagah se.  Hum wahan raat 10:30 tak ruke.  Awaaz aayi.  Woh ek chhipkali thi — ek bada chipkali jo deewar par se gir kar aawaz kar rahi thi, aur mahal ki goonj se ek cheekh si lagti thi.   “Dulhan ki cheekh” wali baat qile ki ek purani aur asli ghatna se nahi aati.  Qile ki history me koi aisi dulhan nahi.  Ye kahani turismo ke saath chalne lagi, kyunki log raat ka darr chaahte hain.  Guard ne khud kaha: “Yahan koi bhoot nahi.  Yahan bas bahut bada khaali jharna hai.”   Nahargarh ka asli rahasya uski taffe ki hai — 12 kamre, ek hawaadaar chhat, aur ek aangan jahan aandhi.  Raat me wahan khade ho kar aapko cheekh sunai degi.  Woh aapke kaan aur deewar ke beech ka khel hai.  Isliye raat 10 baje ke baad entry band hai — bhuton ke liye nahi, deeware ke liye.
