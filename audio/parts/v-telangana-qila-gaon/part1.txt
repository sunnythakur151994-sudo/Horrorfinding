Warangal ke ek qile ki deewar gaon ke peeche hai.  Gaon ke bachche qile me nahi jaate, kyunki andar koi aawaz de to woh kisi ki nahi hoti.  Ek sham meri behen ne wahan se aawaz suni — aur asli madad maang rahi thi.  Woh aawaz.   Humne poora gaon bulaya.  Koi andar nahi gaya.  Unhone kaha ki ye wahi hai jo bachchon ko bulati hai.  Do ghante tak woh aawaz aati rahi, phir bandh ho gayi.  Raat ko meri behen royi, aur subah ek buzurg ne bataya ki pichle din ek gaon ka ladka lapata tha — do din baad qile ke paas mila, bhookha, dara hua, par zinda.   Bechen ka koi bhoot nahi tha.  Woh ek 9 saal ka bachcha tha jo qile ki deewar par chadh gaya aur utar nahi paaya.  Uski maa ne poore gaon se madad maangi thi — aur gaon ne darr ke naam par jawaab nahi diya.  Baad me maine socha: gaon ki kahaniyan kis liye thin?  Qila gir raha tha, deewarein thi, aur wahan jaana khatarnaak tha.  Kahani ne bachchon ko bachaya — aur us raat ek bachche ne bachon ko na bachaya.   Us din se gaon me ek niyam badla: aawaz aaye to pehle 112 aur gaon ke mukhiya ko.  Prarthana baad me.  Kahani to rahegi, par uske bhoot ko insaan ki awaaz se pehle mat rakhiye.
