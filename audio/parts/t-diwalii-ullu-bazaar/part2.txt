Jin logon ko “ashubh” ullu se darr lagta hai, unhi ke kheton mein ullu chuhon ka safaya karta hai — wo kisan ka sabse sasta dost hai.  Jo pakshi raat bhar khet ki rakhwali karta hai, use pakad kar marne se fasal nahi badhti, darr aur kam nahi hota.  Sirf ek jaanwar mara jaata hai.  Aap kya kar sakte hain.   Ullu bechte hue kisi ko dekhein to photo lene se pehle forest department ya police ko khabar dein — 1926 ya 112.  Kisi ko ullu bechne ka lalach de rahe hain to unhe yaad dilaiye: ye criminal case hai, teen se saat saal tak.  Aur agar aap un logon mein hain jo sach mein ullu se darte hain — ek raat khet ke kinare baithiye.  Wo chuhon ka shikaar karega, aapko nahi.
