Diwali ka mahina aata hai aur shehar ki ek purani mandi mein kuch cheezein mehngi ho jaati hain.  Mithai nahi.  Ullu.  Ek interview mein ek shikaari ne kaha tha: “Jo ullu chahte hain, unhe pata hota hai kahan poochna hai.  Hum sirf pakadte hain.  Maarta tantrik hai.”  Kaan wale ullu.   Sabse zyada daam un ulluon ka lagta hai jinke sar par do chhote “kaan” dikhte hain.  Wo asli kaan nahi hain — pankhon ke guchhe hain.  Kahani ne unhe sabse zyada shakti wala bana diya, aur isi wajah se rock eagle owl, dusky eagle owl aur brown fish owl sabse zyada bikte hain.  Pandrah prakar ke ullu ab tak tijaarat mein pakde ja chuke hain — unnis se zyada nahi, kul tees mein se.   Daam do hazaar se shuru hokar lakh tak jaata hai.  Beech mein thekedaar, gaadi wala, mandi ka dalal, aur ant mein wo aadmi jo apne ghar ki takleef ka ilaaj pakshi se karne aaya hai.  Ek raat ka hisaab.   Ek forest officer ne mujhe bataya ki Diwali ke aas-paas unke paas calls badh jaati hain — kisi ne gaadi mein cage dekha, kisi ne chhat par jaal.  Par pakadne par bhi case banana mushkil hota hai, kyunki gawaah darte hain.  “Log samajhte hain hum unki aastha par hamla kar rahe hain.  Hum sirf ek jaanwar bacha rahe hain,” unhone kaha.   Yahan ek chhoti si baat dhyaan dene layak hai.
