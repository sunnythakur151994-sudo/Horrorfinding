Palamu ke ek gaon mein subah ek aurat ne apne aangan mein potli dekhi — lal kapde mein lipti, dhage se bandhi, kuch andar bhari hua.  Wo cheekhi.  Aadhe ghante mein gaon ikattha tha, aur faisla ho chuka tha: ye kaam padosi ka hai.  Padosi Dalit tha.  Do saal ka nuksan.   Gaon ne potli ka “ilaaj” karne ke liye ek ojha bulaya.  Usne paanch hazaar liye.  Padosi ke ghar ka paani band kar diya gaya, unka naam bazaar mein liya jaane laga, unki beti ko school chhodna pada.  Do saal baad ek police case chalne par sach nikla: potli ek nayi bahu ne dabayi thi — apni saas se ladai ke baad, is umeed mein ki “kuch ho jaayega”.  Andar kuch aisa tha jo darne layak nahi tha: thodi mitti, ek sikka, aur ek purana dhaga.   Is kahani mein bhoot, jadu, ya shraap nahi tha.  Sirf ek ghar ki ladai thi, jise gaon ne jaadu ke naam par doosre ghar par thop diya.  Potli ka asli kaam.   Gaon mein “kala jadu ka nishan” ka ek saaf kaam hota hai — wo faisla kar deta hai ki galti kiski hai.  Jab kisi ghar mein bimari aati hai, fasal kharab hoti hai, ya paisa nahi hota, to jawab chahiye hota hai.  Science ka jawab dheere aata hai, aur potli ka jawab turant mil jaata hai.  Chhote gaon mein turant milne wala jawab sabse khatarnaak hota hai.   Aur kanoon?
