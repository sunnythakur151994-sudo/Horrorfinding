Kolkata ke Writers' Building me raat ko ek clerk dikhta hai — safed dhoti, file haath me, corridor ke aakhri kamre tak.  Kaha jaata hai ki woh ek employee hai jo kaam ke beech mara tha.  Kuch logon ne usse dekha, kuch ne bas use suna.  Jaanch.   Hum wahan ek guard ke saath gaye.  Raat 1 baje corridor me koi nahi tha.  Ek kamre me roshni thi.  Andar ek aadmi baitha tha, file khole hue.  Woh ek clerk tha — outsourced staff, jise raat ki shift mili thi aur jo apna kaam subah tak khatam nahi kar paata tha.   Guard ne kaha ki “clerk ka bhoot” ki kahani 50 saal purani hai.  Us kahani ki jad ek asli ghatna thi — ek employee ko duty par dil ka daora aaya tha aur unki maut hui thi.  Uske baad raat ki shift me logon ko lagne laga ki woh ghoom raha hai.  Us ke baad raat ki shift me kaam karne wale log kam ho gaye, isliye jagah khaali lagi, aur jagah khaali lage to kahani zyada sach lagti hai.   Us raat maine us clerk se baat ki.  Unhone kaha: “Sab mujhe bhoot samajhte hain.  Main bas 6 baje tak file khatam karna chahta hoon.”  Aaj bhi Writers' Building ke bahar koi aapko yahi kahani sunayega.  Us aadmi ne subah tak kaam kiya.  Woh asli tha.
