Kathasaritsagara me ek brahman shmashan se guzra.  Wahan ek pishach baitha tha.  Usne kaha — main bhookha hoon.  Brahman ne poocha: tum khaate kya ho?  Pishach ne kaha: main sirf woh khaata hoon jo marne se pehle kisi ka haq chhin chuka ho.  Katha ki shart.   Us raat brahman ko ek gaon jaana tha.  Us gaon me ek seth apni behen ki zameen hadap raha tha.  Brahman wahan pahunche, raat me seth ke ghar ke paas se nikle — aur kuch der baad pishach ne kaha: aaj main bhookha hi rahunga.   Kahani kehta hai ki brahman ne seth se zameen wapas dilwai, aur pishach ka bhoj bandh ho gaya.  Ye theek wahi kahani hai jo aaj bhi gaon me chalti hai — bhoot ka bhoj zameen ke jhagde se juda hua.  Pishach wahan sirf ek khaali pet nahi tha.  Woh ek hisaab tha.   Somadeva ki kahaniyan 11th sadi ki hain, par unmein aaj ka hisaab hai.  Maine ise padhte hue socha: agar har anyay se ek pishach banta ho, to hamare gaon me kitne pishach honge?  Aur un sab ko khaana khilaane ka ek hi tareeka hai — hisaab dena.
