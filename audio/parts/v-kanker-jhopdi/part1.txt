Kanker ke jungle ke kinaare ek chhoti jhopdi thi.  Jisme koi nahi rehta tha, par har subah chulha garam milta aur aangan me jhaadu ke nishaan.  Gaon kehte the ki wahan ek bhoot raat ka khana banata hai.  Chulhe wali subah.   Hum raat bhar ruke.  4 baje ek budha aadmi aaya.  Usne lakdi rakhi, aag jalai, aur chai banai.  Woh gaon ka hi tha — ek bhatka hua mazdoor jise ghar nahi mila tha, aur jo raat me wahan sota tha kyunki uske parivaar ne use chhod diya tha.   “Bhoot ka chulha” ki kahani ne usse bachaya bhi aur chhupaya bhi.  Bachaya isliye ki koi jhopdi todne nahi aaya.  Chhupaya isliye ki kisi ne uski madad nahi ki — sab ne uske chulhe ko dekha, us aadmi ko nahi.   Us subah humne uske saath chai pi.  Usne kaha ki woh kisi ko darr nahi dena chahta, bas raat bitane ki jagah chahiye.  Gaon ki kahani uske liye ek chhat ban gayi thi.  Kabhi kabhi sabse achhi jagah woh hoti hai jise log bhoot ka ghar samajh kar chhod dete hain.
