Yeh kissa usi horror waale roop se prerit hai, har yakshi-parampara ka parichay nahi.  Ghar ki roshni.   Hum chale toh mujhe ek khidki dikhne lagi.  Uske andar peeli roshni thi.  Maine poochha, “Wahi ghar hai?”  Usne haan nahi kaha.  Bas chalti rahi.  Khidki jitni paas aani chahiye thi, utni aayi nahi.  Maine apne kadam gine; phir khud par hansne ka mann hua.  Thakan mein aadmi ajeeb kaam karta hai.   Thodi der baad usne poochha, “Ghar mein tumhara intezaar kaun kar raha hai?”   Maine maa ka naam liya.  Yeh kehte hi mujhe yaad aaya ki maine ghar phone nahi kiya tha.  Main ruka.  Phone ki roshni jalaayi.  Saamne khidki nahi thi.  Do geele patton ke beech door ki roshni atki hui thi.  Pagdandi ka kinaara mere pair se bahut paas tha.  Neeche paani ki halki awaaz aa rahi thi.   Maine uski taraf dekha.  Woh ped ke us paar thi, ya shayad wahan koi tha hi nahi.  Is kalpanik kisse ka darr us ek nazar mein hai: hum jis cheez ko saath chalne waala samajh rahe the, uski jagah khaali bhi ho sakti hai.  Jo yaad ghar laayi.   Main wapas muda.  Jaldi mein bhaaga nahi; har kadam neeche dekhkar rakha.  Main us pal ko koi badi bahaaduri nahi keh sakta.  Mujhe bas ghar ki rasoi yaad aa gayi thi, aur thaali dhak kar rakhne ki maa ki aadat.   Yakshi ke kisse aksar yahin khatam nahi hote.  Kuch mein raahi bachta hai, kuch mein nahi.
