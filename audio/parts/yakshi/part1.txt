Usne raasta nahi poochha.  Bas kaha, “Thodi door saath chaloge?”  Main pehle hi der se laut raha tha.  Kerala ki us kalpanik pagdandi par baarish ke baad mitti naram thi, aur mere jooton mein paani ghus chuka tha.  Phir bhi main ruk gaya.  Uski awaaz mein aisa kuch tha jo mana karne ko badtameezi bana raha tha.  Ghar pahunchne ki jaldi ek pal ko bhool gaya.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Ped ke neeche.   Woh ped ki aadhi chhaon mein khadi thi.  Maine uska chehra saaf nahi dekha; bas halki saari aur kandhe tak aate baal.  Usne ek taraf ishaara kiya.  Main us raaste ko jaanta tha—ya mujhe aisa laga.  Din mein wahan se kuch ghar dikhte the.  Ab patton ke beech sirf andhera tha.   Bachpan mein suni yakshi ki kahani mujhe tab yaad nahi aayi.  Jab hum kahani sunte hain toh sochte hain ki hum nishaan turant pehchaan lenge.  Par asli uljhan ki tarah likhe is drishya mein koi bada sanket nahi tha.  Ek insaan ne saath maanga tha.  Maine haan keh di thi.   Kerala ki lok-kathaon mein yakshi ke kai roop hain.  Kuch daraawni kahaniyon mein woh raahi ko apni taraf kheenchti hai, pehchaane raaste se door le jaati hai.  Lekin yakshi ka vishaal saanskritik roop sirf is ek bhayankar chhavi mein sama nahi jaata.
