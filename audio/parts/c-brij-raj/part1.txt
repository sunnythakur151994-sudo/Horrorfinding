Kota's Brij Raj Bhavan ek heritage imarat hai.  1857 me yahan ek British officer mara tha, aur kahani kehti hai ki uski aatma ab bhi hai.  Guards ne kaha ki raat me unhone pipe ki khushbu mehsoos ki, aur unhe kisi ke saath koi room me baithne ka ehsaas hua.  Jaanch.   Humne do raatein wahan bitayi.  Pehli raat kuch nahi.  Doosri raat, 1 baje, corridor me taaza tambaku ki khushbu aayi.  Hum tezi se uthe — koi nahi tha.  Subah manager ne has kar kaha ki yahan raat ka guard baith kar beedi peeta hai.  Humne guard se poocha, usne has kar haan kaha.   “Officer ka bhoot” ki kahani vahan ki heritage ke liye kaafi kaam karti hai.  Hotel ke brochure me bhi zikr hai.  Par room me ek bistar, ek purana escritoire aur ek kursi hai — jo logon ko lagta hai kisi ne abhi chhoda.  Woh asli me kisi ne chhoda tha: restoration ke time.   Brij Raj Bhavan ek khoobsurat imarat hai.  Wahan sach me raat ka guard beedi peeta hai, aur heritage ki chhaten awaaz karti hain.  Aur yahi baat is jagah ki asli kahani hai — ek imarat jo 170 saal se apni yaad khud rakhti hai, bhoot ki zaroorat ke bina.
