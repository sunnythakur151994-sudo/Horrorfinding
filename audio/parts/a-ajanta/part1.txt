Ajanta ki kai gufayen aaj band hain.  Unme se ek ke darwaze par ek chhota sa lock hai, jo abhi bhi chamakta hai.  Guide ne kaha: “Andar kuch nahi hai.”  Maine poocha kya hai to unke bataya — 1950s me wahan se murtiyan nikali gayin, aur kuch kamre dhans gaye.  Andar kya tha.   Main us guide ke saath raat tak ruka — woh Ajanta ke gaon ka tha.  Usne bataya ki unke dada ke zamane me ek gufa me dhuan bhar gaya tha, kyunki kisi ne raat wahan chulha jalaya.  Baad me wahan kaam bandh kar diya gaya.  Kahani me raakh aur aawaz dono aa gaye.   Ajanta me sadiyon tak bhikshu rehte the.  Unke baad kuch bhi nahi raha — bas deewarein, chitra aur unke bante hue dhuen ke nishaan.  Jab log un chitra ke saamne jaate hain, to unhe lagta hai ki koi dekh raha hai.  Sach yahi hai: koi dekh hi raha hai — 1500 saal purane chitra, jo apne rang me aaj bhi khade hain.   Aur wo band darwaza?  Woh ASI ne band kiya tha, kyunki andar chhat giri hui thi.  Jo cheez band hai, uske bare me kahaniyan aasaani se ban jaati hain.  Par us darwaze ke peeche koi bhoot nahi — sirf kaam, mitti aur kisi ki thakan hai.
