Ahmedabad ke purane pol me ghar ek jaisa dikhte hain: rangin, chhote, aur ek doosre se chipke hue.  Ek makaan band tha.  Padosi kehte the raat ko andar se chakki chalne ki awaaz aati hai, aur ek budhiya ka chehra khidki par dikhta hai.  Chakki.   Main raat 2 baje utha.  Awaaz aa rahi thi.  Maine darwaza khatkhataya.  Kisi ne nahi khola.  Phir maine padosi ki chhat se dekha: andar ek chhoti roshni thi aur ek aurat chakki chala rahi thi.   Woh aurat 82 saal ki thi, aur us makaan ki malik thi.  Uska beta chhod ke chala gaya tha.  Woh raat ko chakki chalati thi kyunki din me bijli nahi aati thi.  Gaon ne use “bhoot” keh diya, aur asli baat ye thi ki uske makaan me bijli ka connection bhi nahi tha — woh andhere me kaam karti thi kyunki kisi ne uski madad nahi ki.   Maine us raat unke liye ek torch rakhi.  Agli subah unhone kaha ki unhone bilkul who nahi dekha.  Phir bhi ek saal tak hum har raat wahi awaaz sunte rahe.  Par ab koi nahi kehta ki woh bhoot hai — kehte hain, “budhiya ab tak kaam kar rahi hai.”
