Ye koi lok-katha nahi hai.  Pathanamthitta zile ke Elanthoor gaon mein 2022 mein ek aisa case saamne aaya jo poore desh ko hila gaya.  Do auratein — dono lottery ticket bechkar apna ghar chalati thin — ek “tantrik” aur ek jode ke paas pahuncheen, is vaade par ki unhe paisa, taraqqi ya “kismat” mil jayegi.  Dono ki hatya kar di gayi.  Case file.   Police ke mutabik, do auratein — Roslyn aur Padma — lalach aur bharose ke saath us ghar tak aayi thin.  Unke saath jo hua, wo dharm, shakti ya khazana nahi tha; wo kidnap, yatar aur hatya thi.  Kerala police ne teen aaropiyon — Muhammed Shafi, Bhagaval Singh aur unki patni Laila — ko October 2022 mein giraftar kiya.  Aaraop ke tehat hatya, apaharan, baltkaar aur saboot mitane jaisi dharaayein hain.  Muqadma abhi bhi adalat mein hai; 2025 tak aaraopiyon par dharaayein taey ki ja rahi thin aur nateeja nahi aaya.   Is case ne Kerala mein ek purani baat phir se utha di: kya “black magic” ke naam par hone wale apradhon ke liye alag kanoon ki zaroorat hai?  Kerala mein aisi ghatnaon ke khilaaf aaj bhi zyada tar mamle aam IPC dharaon ke tehat chalte hain.  Do auratein, do ghar.   Is case ki sabse chhoti detail sabse bada sach batati hai.
