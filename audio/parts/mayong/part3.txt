Raaste mein ek awaaz hui; main turant pedon ki taraf mud gaya.  Usne kaha, “Paani gir raha hai.”  Mujhe apni jaldi par sharm aayi.  Din bhar maine kahaniyan suni thi.  Ab main har awaaz se unka saboot maang raha tha.   Gaadi tak pahunchkar maine mudkar dekha.  Ek ghar mein peeli roshni thi.  Koi darwaze ke paas khada kapde nichod raha tha.  Agar main chahta, toh us photo ko andhera karke kuch bhi naam de sakta tha.  Maine photo nahi li.   Mayong ka rahasya mere liye us raat khatam nahi hua.  Bas uska chehra badal gaya.  Ab sawaal yeh nahi tha ki yahaan kaun gaayab ho sakta hai.  Sawaal tha ki ek jagah ke baare mein baar-baar ek hi kahani sunate hue hum uski kitni asli zindagi gaayab kar dete hain.
