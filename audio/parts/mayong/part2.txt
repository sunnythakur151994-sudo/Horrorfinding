Unse jude kisse kabhi upchaar, kabhi mantra, kabhi aisi shaktiyon ki baat karte hain jo rozmarra ke niyamon se bahar hain.  In daavon ko sunna aur unhe siddh maan lena alag baatein hain.  Par us kamre mein meri dilchaspi pehli baar kisi chamatkaar se hatkar ek insaan ke haath par aa gayi thi—kitni ehtiyaat se woh kaagaz pakad raha tha.  Woh kahani jo adhoori chhodi gayi.   Shaam ko baarish aayi.  Chhat ke kinaare se paani gir raha tha.  Buzurg ne ek raahi ka kissa sunaya jo apni manzil bhool gaya tha.  Har mod par use apne ghar ki koi cheez dikh jaati: aangan ka ped, ek pehchaana darwaza, kisi apne ki peeth.  Woh paas jaata toh kuch aur nikalta.  Aakhir usne chalna band kiya aur subah hone di.   “Uske saath kisne kiya?”  maine poochha.   Buzurg ne pothi lapet di.  “Tum har kahani mein kisi ko pakadna kyun chahte ho?”   Main hansa, lekin sawaal mere saath reh gaya.  Main aaya tha kisi taaqat ko pakadne, kisi chehre ko uska naam dene.  Shayad mujhe yeh maan lena aasaan lagta tha ki har anjaani cheez ke peeche ek hi chhupa hua haath hai.    Waapsi ka raasta.   Baarish ruki toh mitti ki khushboo bahut tez thi.
