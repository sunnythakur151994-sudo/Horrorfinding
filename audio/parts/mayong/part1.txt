Main Mayong ek ajeeb sawaal lekar gaya tha: kya sach mein kisi insaan ko gaayab kiya ja sakta hai?  Chai dene waale ne meri taraf dekha, phir cup mein bachi chai daal di.  “Pehle baitho,” usne kaha.  Uski awaaz mein na raaz tha, na dhamki.  Peeche ek bachcha apni copy par kuch likh raha tha.  Itni saadhaaran dopahar ke liye main taiyaar nahi tha.  Maine toh safar shuru hone se pehle hi apne dimaag mein raat kar li thi.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Naam ke peeche rehne waale log.   Assam ke Mayong ka naam jaadu aur tantra ki kahaniyon ke saath juda hua hai.  Lekin jab hum kisi jagah ko sirf uski sabse daraawni kahani se pehchaante hain, toh wahan rehne waale log dheere-dheere nazar se gaayab hone lagte hain.  Main bhi yahi kar raha tha.  Dukaan, school jaata bachcha, kapde sukhaati aurat—sab mere liye raaste ki cheezein thi.  Main kisi “asli” raaz ka intezaar kar raha tha.   Is kalpanik mulaaqaat mein mujhe ek buzurg ke ghar le jaaya gaya.  Unhone koi pradarshan nahi kiya.  Ek kapde mein lipti puraani pothi kholi aur kaha ki cheezein sambhaalna bhi kaam hota hai.
