Is kalpanik punarkathan mein saalon baad wahi bachcha bada hokar lautata hai.  Naani ka ghar khaali ho chuka hai.  Woh khidki kholta hai aur nadi ki awaaz pehchaan leta hai.  Us raat bijli chali jaati hai.  Kamre mein baithe-baithe use phir woh rona sunaai deta hai—bahut neeche, bahut thaka hua.  Is baar woh bachcha nahi hai.  Use lagta hai ki kisi ko madad chahiye.   Woh roshni lekar darwaze tak jaata hai.  Raaste par ek chhota joota pada hai.  Geela.  Uske aage mitti mein paani ki dhaar kheenchti hui nadi ki taraf jaati hai.  Woh kisi ko pukaarta hai.  Rona ruk jaata hai.   Yahi khaamoshi use rok deti hai.  Rona door se aa raha tha, lekin uske band hote hi use apne bahut paas kisi ki saans ka ehsaas hota hai.  Woh roshni ghumaa kar dekhta hai.  Koi nahi.  Phir bahut dheere awaaz aati hai: “Tumne unhe dekha?”  Ghar ki taraf.   Woh jawaab nahi jaanta.  Use pata bhi nahi ki sawaal usse kiya gaya hai ya raat se.  Woh peeche hatkar darwaze ke andar aa jaata hai.  Kundi lagaata hai, roshni mez par rakhta hai aur subah tak uske bujhne ka intezaar karta rehta hai.  Bahar paani phir apni puraani awaaz mein behne lagta hai.   Subah raaste par joota nahi milta.  Mitti geeli hai, kyunki raat mein baarish hui thi.  Woh isse koi nateeja nahi nikaalta.  Yeh kahani saboot ke saath band nahi hoti.
