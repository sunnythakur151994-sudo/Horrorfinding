Nadi ghar se dikhti nahi thi, lekin raat ko sunaai deti thi.  Bachpan mein mujhe lagta tha paani hamesha ek hi awaaz karta hai.  Us raat pehli baar laga ki uske beech koi ro raha hai.  Main khidki tak gaya.  Chandni mein kinaare ka raasta safed dikh raha tha.  Wahin, pedon ke aage, ek aur safedi khadi thi.  Hawa mein hilti hui.  Maine naani ko bulaaya.  Unhone khidki ke paas aakar bahar dekha aur mera kandha apni taraf kar liya.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Rone waali aurat.   La Llorona ka matlab hai “rone waali aurat.”  Mexico aur Latin America ki is lok-katha ke bahut-se versions hain.  Ek mashhoor roop mein ek maa apne bachchon ko paani mein kho deti hai; kuch versions mein unki maut ke liye woh khud zimmedaar hoti hai.  Pachhtaava uski kahani ka ant nahi banta.  Uske baad shuru hoti hai talaash, jo maut ke baad bhi khatam nahi hoti.   Naani ne us raat poori kahani nahi sunaayi.  Bas kaha ki nadi ki taraf nahi jaana.  Main bistar par aa gaya, lekin kaan khidki ki taraf lage rahe.  Rone ki awaaz paas aati, phir paani mein mil jaati.  Mujhe lagta koi chal raha hai.  Phir lagta, shayad main uske chalne ki aahat khud bana raha hoon.  Kinaare par chhota-sa joota.
