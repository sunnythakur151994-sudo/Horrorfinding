Tarapith ek shakti-peeth hai.  Mandir ke paas hi shmashan hai, jahan sadhana hoti hai.  Raat me wahan aawazein aati hain — dhun, hey, dhol.  Log kehte hain wahan aatmaayein ghoomti hain.  Hum wahan ek pujari ke saath gaye.  Jaanch.   Raat 10 baje hum pahunche.  Mandir ke paas bheed thi — bhakt, sadhu, aur kai log jo raat bhar jaagte hain.  Shmashan me koi aisa nahi tha jise dekh kar darr lage.  Ek sadhak dhyan me baithe the.  Pujari ne kaha: “Yahan jo aawaz hai, woh unki hai.”   Tarapith ki asli baat ye hai ki ye ek jeevit parampara hai — log yahan aate hain, dhyan karte hain, apne dukh rakhte hain.  Doosri baat: raat me yahan dhuan, shor aur bheed hai.  Aawaz ka source dhoondhna mushkil nahi hai — bhopali, sadhna, aur mandir ki ghantiyaan.   Pujari ne mujhe ek baat kahi jo maine likh li: “Jahan log dard le kar aate hain, wahan bhoot ki baat mat karo.”  Main is file me usi baat ko rakhta hoon.  Tarapith ka darr kisi aatma ka nahi hai — yahan kisi ka dard itna bada hai ki woh aawaz ban jaata hai.  Tum us aawaz ko bhoot kehkar chale jao, to dard wapas jaisa hai waisa rahega.
