Bhuj ke bahar ek jheel hai.  Uske kinare ek khaali gaon hai, jo 2001 ke bhukamp ke baad chhoot gaya.  Log kehte hain raat ko wahan ek aurat khadi rehti hai.  Gaon wale ab bhi wahan nahi jaate.  Jheel ka kinara.   Hum ek social worker ke saath gaye.  Wahan ek aurat mili jo khaali ghar me rehti thi.  Unhone bataya ki unhone apna poora parivaar bhukamp me khoya.  Woh wahan isliye rehti thin kyunki unke ghar ki jagah unke logon ki yaad thi.   “Aurat ka bhoot” wali kahani unke aas-paas bani.  Gaon ke log unse darte the, unse baat nahi karte the, aur raat ko wahan nahi jaate the.  Ek insaan ki majboori ko bhoot kehna asaan tha — aur usse unki madad nahi karni padi.   Us raat unhone hamare liye chai banayi.  Unke haath kaanp rahe the.  Humne unse poochha ki woh wahan kyun rehti hain.  Unhone kaha: “Yahan mujhe raste par unke joote dikhte hain.”  Hum wahan sirf ek khaali ghar dekhne gaye the.  Woh wahan apna dukh dekhne rehti thin.
