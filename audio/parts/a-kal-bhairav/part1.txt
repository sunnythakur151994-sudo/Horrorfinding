Kal Bhairav ke mandir ke bahar ek kutta hota hai — pathar ka, ya asli.  Kaha jaata hai woh Kashipur ka rakhwala hai, aur raat me woh kisi ko andar nahi jaane deta.  Ek mandir me raat ko kutte ke rone ki awaaz aati thi.  Rone wala kutta.   Wo asli kutta tha.  Mandir ke pujari use khilate the, aur woh raat me bhaukte hue mandir ke peechhe chala jaata tha.  Pujari kehte the ki “wo kisi cheez ko dekh raha hai”.  Par ek raat unhone bataya ki woh kutta ek hi jagah par bhauka — jahan mandir ke pusht ki chhat kamzor thi.   Kal Bhairav ki kahaniyan raksha ki hain.  Mandir ke kutton ko log dar ka naam dete hain, par woh mandir ke sabse bade rakhwale hain — kyunki woh chor, saanp aur andhere ko pehle pehchaan lete hain.  Unki awaaz bhayanak lagti hai, par kaam raksha ka hai.   Maine us raat pujari ke saath baith kar mandir ki chhat dekhi.  Agli subah ek pathar gir gaya.  Kisi ko chot nahi lagi.  Dar ki baat ye hai ki us raat jo bhaunk raha tha, woh kisi bhoot ko nahi dekh raha tha — woh ek dhili chhat ko dekh raha tha.  Zyaadatar jagahon par, mandir ka kutta sach me bhoot pakadta hai.
