Sola saal ki ladki.  Raat ke do baje uth kar cheekhna, apne aap se baat karna, aankhein palatna, phir subah kuch yaad nahi.  Gaon ne naam rakh diya: chhalawa.  Ojha aaye, jhaad-phook ki, ek “nishan” bataya gaya, aur ek kamre mein use akela baandh diya gaya — “taaki kuch galti na kare”.  Nau mahine.   Nau mahine gaye.  Do ojhe, teen mandir, ek “bhabhut wala baba”, aur ek baar tantrik ne kaha ki ye “purani chudail” hai jo unke khandan se judi hai.  Tab tak uski padhai chhoot chuki thi, wo kamzor ho chuki thi, aur ghar ka paisa khatam.  Phir ek naye doctor ne poocha: “Kabhi dimaag ka EEG karwaya hai?”   Answer: wo “mirgi” thi — epilepsy.  Uska ek khaas prakar, jisme doura aane se pehle ka anubhav ajeeb hota hai aur uske baad confusion.  Wo cheekhna, wo baatein — sab doure ka hissa.  Dawa shuru hui.  Teen mahine mein wo raat bhar sone lagi.  Aaj wo ek school mein padhati hai.  Do cheezein jo main kahna chahta hoon.   Pehli: is kahani mein ojhe ne kuch “kharab” nahi kiya — unhone wahi kiya jo unhone seekha tha.  Nuksan laaptaawahi ne kiya.  Nau mahine ka ilaaj na milna ek aisa nuksan hai jo kabhi wapas nahi aata.   Doosri: “chhalawa” ek aisa shabd hai jo har ajeeb vyavhaar ko ek hi naam de deta hai.  Mirgi, mansik bimari, nasha, neend ki kami, thyroid, aur ek bachche ka dukh — sab ek hi shabd ke neeche chhup jaate hain.
