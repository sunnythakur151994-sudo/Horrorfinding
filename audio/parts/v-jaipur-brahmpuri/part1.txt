Brahmpuri ke ek kuein par raat ko dhakna rehta tha.  Bachche kehte the ki andar ek aadmi baithta hai jo paani maangta hai.  Gaon ki baat pakki thi: use paani mat dena, warna woh tumhare saath chala aayega.  Paani.   Main ek raat wahan gaya.  Kuein se awaaz aa rahi thi.  Maine paani ka lota rakha aur door khada ho gaya.  Andar se ek haath nikla, lota liya, aur paani peel liya.  Woh ek aadmi tha — din me kaam karne wala mazdoor, jo kuein ke paas sota tha, aur jise apna koi nahi tha.   Gaon ki kahani usse bhoot banati thi.  Asli baat sirf ye thi ki woh akela tha, aur kuein ke paas sota tha kyunki wahan hawa thi.  Raat ko koi paani maange to gaon ka nikamma darr usse paani dene se rok deta tha.   Us raat ke baad maine use apne ghar ke peerhe par sone diya aur usse rusuk utaar kar dekha ki woh asli tha.  Aaj woh kisi aur sheher me kaam karta hai.  Brahmpuri ka kuaan ab dhaka hua hai, par gaon ki kahani wahi hai.  Kabhi kabhi sirf ek lota paani kaafi hota hai.
