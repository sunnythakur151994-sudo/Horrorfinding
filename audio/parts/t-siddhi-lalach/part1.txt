Bankura ke ek gaon mein ek “sadhu” ne teen ladkon ko “siddhi” ka lalach diya.  Raat bhar smashan mein jaana hoga, chaalis raat, bina kisi ko bataye.  Pehli raat hi ek ladka wapas nahi aaya.  Subah uski laash mili — saanp ke katne se.  Lalach ka hisaab.   “Siddhi” wala dhandha us lalach par chalta hai jo har jawaan aadmi ke andar hoti hai: koi shakti chahiye, kuch aisa jo baaki logon ke paas nahi.  Aur jo aadmi aapko bhejta hai, wo khud kabhi nahi jaata.  Wo aapko raat, sunsaan jagah, bina roshni, bina kisi ko bataye bhejta hai — aur us jagah jaane ka matlab hota hai saanp, kutta, gaddha, bijli ki taar, ya koi aisa aadmi jo wahan aapke jaisa hi kuch dhoondhne aaya hai.   Is gaon mein us ladke ki maa aaj bhi kehti hai ki “usne kuch galat kiya tha isliye saanp ne kata”.  Sach ye hai ki usne kuch galat nahi kiya tha — usne bus kisi ke jhooth par bharosa kiya tha.  Shakti ka asli raasta.   Agar kisi ko kuch “bada” karna hai, to raaste khule hain — padhai, kaam, khel, kala, seva.  Inmein se kisi bhi raaste par aapko raat ko smashan mein akele jaane ki zaroorat nahi padti, aur kisi par bhi jaan ka khatra nahi hota.  Jo raasta aapko andheron mein akela bhejta hai, wo shakti ka raasta nahi hota.  Wo sirf ek aadmi ka faayda hota hai.
