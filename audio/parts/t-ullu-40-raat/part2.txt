Unhone mujhe bataya ki “chaalis raat jaagane” ki baat sach hoti to is tijaarat mein lagne wale logon ke paas sabse pehle sona hota.  Par unke paas sirf toote pankh the, aankhon mein infection, aur ek chamach se doodh pilane wala kaam.   Unhone ek baat kahi jo mujhe ab tak yaad hai: “Ullu ki aankh roshni se aisi hi takti hai jaise aapki aankh phone ki torch se.  Raat bhar jaagane ka matlab uske liye maut ka raasta hai, sona ka nahi.”  Jo asli hai.   Ullu raat ka shikari hai.  Din mein wo aaram karta hai, raat mein shikaar.  Uska dimaag, uski aankhein aur uske pankh raat ke liye bane hain, insaan ki baat ke liye nahi.  Kisi bhi pakshi ke paas insaan ki tarah bolne ka gala nahi hota — jitni bhi awaazein wo nikaale, wo swar-peti ki seema ke andar hi rehti hain.  Aur “chhupa hua sona” dhoondhne wali koi indriy kisi jaanwar ke paas nahi hoti, warna puratatva vibhag ke paas ullu naukri par hote.   Jo cheez asli hai wo ye hai: is kahani se pakshiyon ki tijaarat chalti hai.  Diwali ke aas-paas demand badhti hai, daam hazaaron se lakh tak jaate hain, aur pakshi ko bechne wale aapko wahi “chaalis raat” wala sapna dikhate hain.  Sapna muft hai.  Pakshi ka daam aap bharenge, aur uske baad uske marne ka, infection ka, ya forest department ke case ka kharcha bhi.
