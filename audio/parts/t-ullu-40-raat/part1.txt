Mere ek rishtedaar ne, jinke gaon mein bijli abhi bhi shaam saat baje ke baad nahi aati, mujhe wahi baat sunai jo maine WhatsApp par sau baar padhi thi: ullu ko chaalis raat jaagakar rakhna.  Raat bhar uske saamne diya jalta rahe, use sone na do.  Chaalisvi raat ke baad wo sammohit ho jaata hai, aur phir wo apne maalik se insaan ki zubaan mein baat karta hai.  Aur jo baat sabse zyada bikti hai — wo batata hai ki sona kahan dabaya hua hai.  Gaon mein kahani kaise chalti hai.   Is kahani ke do hisse hamesha saath chalte hain.  Pehla hissa dar ka hai: ullu raat ka pakshi hai, uski aawaz ashubh maani jaati hai, chhat par baith jaaye to koi marne wala hai.  Doosra hissa lalach ka hai: agar ullu raat ka rakhwala hai, to wahi rakhwala sabse bada khazana bhi jaanta hoga.  Ek hi pakshi ko dar aur lalach dono ne pakad kar rakha hai.   Maine apne rishtedaar se poocha ki unhone khud kabhi dekha hai?  Nahi.  Kisi ne dekha hai?  Ek aadmi ne, jiske “bhaiya ke saale” ne ki thi.  Naam, gaon, saal — kuch bhi poora nahi tha.  Sirf itna tha ki kahani pakki thi.  Ek doosri taraf.   Phir main Farrukhabad ke ek aadmi se mila jo pichhle aath saal se ghayal aur bebas ullu pakad kar forest department ko deta hai.  Unke paas ek chhote kamre mein pandrah ullu the — chhoti ullu, barn owl, aur ek bada rock eagle owl jise kisi ne thekedaar se kharida tha.
