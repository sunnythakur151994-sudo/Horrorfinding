Sundarbans mein har maut ka ek karan hota hai: bagh, paani, saanp, ya darr.  Par har maut ke baad gaon mein ek naam liya jaata hai — Dokkhin Rai, jungle ka raja.  Rone waale se kaha jaata hai: wo sher ki aankhon, bandook ke nishane, aur jungle ke kanoon ko insaan se zyada samajhta hai.  Woh raat.   Hum chaar log ek khaadi mein jaa rahe the.  Machhli ka season tha aur bijli nahi thi.  Kisne pehle kaha, yaad nahi, par sab ek saath chup ho gaye — jaise koi humare aage se nikal gaya ho.  Mera dost jhuk gaya, haath upar, aur usne saamne dekhne ki himmat nahi ki.  Uske baad, dhuan aur ghaas ke beech se, ek sher nikla — lamba, patla, aur bhookha.   Toh woh sher hi tha.  Bandook wale ne hawa mein maara, kyunki bagh par goli chalana kanoon ke bahar tha.  Bagh thehar gaya, dekha, phir chala gaya.  Ek raat ke baad kisi ne kaha hum Dokkhin Rai se mile.  Par hum sirf ek bhukha bagh se mile the, aur woh kaafi tha.   Gaon mein kehte hain ki Dokkhin Rai ko kosna nahi chahiye, warna wo dhyan dega.  Mera manna hai: yahan log sher ke saath hi rehte hain, isliye darr ko ek naam dena asaan hota hai.  Naam se jungle nayi jagah nahi bana; bas khauf ko ek shakal mil jaati hai.  Sher se nazar bachakar chalna kanoon se bhi sahi hai, samajh se bhi.
