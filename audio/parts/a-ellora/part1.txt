Ellora ki gufaon me ek chhoti gufa bibhit hai jahan bijli nahi hai.  Andar jate hi hawa tham jaati hai.  Ek baar main wahan akela gaya, aur 10 minute baad mujhe laga ki koi kone me khada hai.  Main bahar bhaga.  Gufa ka sach.   Bahar ek guard baitha tha.  Unhone has kar kaha: “Sir, har dusra banda bhaagta hai.”  Wajah: andar ka temperature bahar se 8-10 degree kam hota hai, hawa ka rukh nahi hota, aur aapki hi saans ki awaaz wapas lautti hai.  Hamare kaan usse doosre ke pair samajhte hain.   Ellora me 100 se zyada gufayen hain, 600 se 1000 CE ke beech ki.  Unme se ek bhi “bhoot ki gufa” nahi hai — sabhi bujhaye hue patharon ki sadiyon ki mahak aur hawa ka khel hain.  Par har saal koi na koi wahan darr ke chakkar me chot khaata hai.   Us din ke baad main usi gufa me dobara gaya.  Andar ek dher pathar par baitha, aankh band ki, aur 3 minute lagatar suna.  Achha laga.  Jo awaz aap sun rahe hain, wo aapki hai.  Ye sabse pehli cheez hai jo main har bhoot ki jagah par karta hoon — apni sansein ginta hoon.
