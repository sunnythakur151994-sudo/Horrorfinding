Coorg ke coffee estate ke andar ek purana bungalow hai.  Angrezi zamaane ka, lal chhat wala.  Ab wahan koi nahi rehta — par uska aangan subah saaf milta hai.  Gaon waale kehte hain wahan ek memsahib rehti hai jo chai banati hai.  Chai ki khushbu.   Main wahan ek season raha.  Subah kaam par jaate waqt aangan me chai ki khushbu aati thi.  Ek raat main chhat par leta tha aur maine khirki me roshni dekhi.  Main darwaze tak gaya, aur andar kuch nahi tha — bas ek puraana samaan.   Baad me pata chala ki wahan ek watchman rehta tha jo subah 4 baje chai banata tha aur aangan saaf karta tha.  Chai ki khushbu uski thi.  “Roshni” estate ke kaamgar ki torch thi.  Usne kaha kya hua, maine kuch nahi kaha — kyunki mera aana hi uske liye ek cheez thi jo bina soche aa jaati thi.   Coorg ke estate bungalow apne hi mausam banate hain: sookhi lakdi, gili chhappar, geele kapde.  Unme roshni, awaaz aur khushbu sab hoti hai.  Par sabse badi baat ye hai ki wahan bade logon ki purani duniya ki yaadein hain — aur kabhi kabhi ek angrezi imarat ki yaad dene wala koi nahi hota.  Us raat ke baad maine us watchman ke liye do cup chai banayi.
