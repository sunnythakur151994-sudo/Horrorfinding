Bastar ke jungle mein raasta bhoolne ko “chhalawa” kehte hain.  Zyada tar jal, khadd aur lapete hue ped hain; kisi ko kuch nahi hota, bas subah khud ko apne gaon se pachas kadam door pata hai.  Par ek raat mera mama khud ko paanch ghante se usi mod par ghoomta paaya.  Teen baar wahi mod.   Woh majdoori se laut rahe the.  Raat 10 baje.  Raasta chhota tha — bees minute.  Teen mod ke baad wahi sookha kuaan aaya.  Unhone poora shauk badal diya: ek lamba raasta liya.  Kuaan aaya.  Woh doobara ringane lage, aur woh teesri baar phir aaya.   Us raat ka asli khel ye tha — unke paas adhi bhedmi, ek deewar jaisa dhundhla, aur padta hua dhoop: torche ka.  Unhone chalti hui aansoo waali awaaz suni thi.  Subah humne wo mod dekh kar samjha: do raste ek jaisi dhalan wale the aur ek khuli khet ki taraf.  Andhere mein woh dono ek lagte the.  Koi jungle ka khel nahi tha, aur koi bhi unhe “hing” nahi de raha tha.   Gaon waale ab bhi kahte hain jungle ka koi chhalawa hai.  Ho sakta hai.  Par kaam ki baat ye hai: raat ko akele na nikalna, torch aur paani rakhna, aur apne ghar ka naam bhi jahan jaana phone me note karna.  Chhalawa ek kahani ban jaaye to accha; par wahan pahunchna nahi.
