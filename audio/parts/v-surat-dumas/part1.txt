Dumas beach ki kaali ret aur raat ka samundar — yahan laashon ki kahaniya purani hain.  Kaha jaata tha ki raat ko wahan gaayab hone wale log kabhi nahi milte, aur unki aawaz paani me sunai deti hai.  Ek raat ki ginti.   Main ek raat wahan tha.  Tairne wale do ladke gayab ho gaye the.  Gaon me kaha gaya ki unhe “samundar ki rani” le gayi.  Do din baad unki laashein milee — lehar ne kinare par phenk diya.   Asli baat ye thi ki unhe tairna nahi aata tha aur woh raat me chale gaye the.  Samundar me raat ko dhara tez hoti hai; black sand me pair dhans jaata hai.  Gaon ki kahani ne unhe “gaayab” kar diya, jabki woh abhi bhi wahan the — bas paani ke liye.   Darr ki kahaniyan samundar ko ek shakti deti hain.  Samundar ko koi shakti nahi chahiye — uske paas dhara, lehar aur andhera hai.  Us raat ke baad maine kinare par ek board lagaya: “Raat me nahi.”  Kisi ne use nahi padha.  Phir bhi laga.
