Tirumala ki pahadiyon me ek purani seedhi hai, jo jungle me gum ho jaati hai.  Log kehte hain wahan ek bhakta aaj bhi chadhta hai, aur uski ghanti sunai deti hai.  Yatris us raaste ko chhod dete hain.  Ghanti.   Ek local guide ne humein bataya ki us seedhi par koi nahi chadta kyunki woh jungle me kho jaati hai, aur uske aage girne ka khatra hai.  Raat me wahan se ghanti ki awaaz aati hai — ek mandir ki ghanti, jo purane raaste par latki hui hai aur hawa se bajti hai.   “Bhakta ka bhoot” wali kahani usi purane raaste ki yaad hai.  Pahle yatris paidal aate the, aur kai log raste me mar jaate the — thand, bimari, thakan.  Us seedhi men aaj bhi un logon ka nishaan nahi hai, par unki yaad gaon me hai.   Yahan ka sabse bada sach ye hai: pahadi par raat me jaana khatarnaak hai, aur jungle me bhatakna asli khatra hai.  Kahani us raaste se logon ko door rakhti hai — aur shayad yahi uski sabse achhi duty hai.  Humne wahan chadne ki koshish nahi ki.  Humne bas ghanti suni aur laut aaye.
