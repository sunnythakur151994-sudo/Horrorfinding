“Vashikaran — 21 din, guaranteed.  Pyaar, shaadi, naukri, dushmani, property — sab ka ilaaj.  Consultation free.  Pooja ka kharcha aapka.”  Ye message mujhe teen alag numbers se aaya, teen alag shehron se, aur teenon ka rate card lagbhag same tha.  Pattern.   Is dhandhe ka dhaancha hamesha ek jaisa hota hai.  Pehle muft salah — aapki “kundli” dekh kar ek darr bataya jaata hai jo aapko lagta hai bilkul sach hai.  Phir ek chhota sa kaam: “pehle ek chhota poojan karwa lijiye”.  Phir ek rukaawat: “antrik shakti aa rahi hai”.  Har rukaawat par ek naya bill.  Aur sabse aakhir mein — jab aap thak jaate hain — “aapke bharose ki kami hai, isliye kaam nahi hua”.   Is pattern ka naam police file mein “cheating” hai.  Aur ek baat jo ise aur bhi khatarnaak banati hai: kai log apna sona, gehne, baal, ya kapde “pooja ke liye” de dete hain.  Wo cheezein wapas nahi aati.  Kanoon kya kehta hai.   India mein jaadu-tona ke naam par “guarantee” dena aur paisa lena dhokha hai — BNS/IPC ki cheating ki dharaayein, aur Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954 ke tehat bimari ke “jaadu wale ilaaj” ka vigyapan bhi rok hai.  Do rajyon mein anti-superstition kanoon bhi hain.  Matlab: ye sirf galat nahi, ye report karne layak hai.   Aur ek cheez jo is dhandhe ke bahar hai — asli darr.
