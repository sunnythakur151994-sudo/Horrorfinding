Dumas Beach ki ret kaali hai, aur raat ko wahan koi nahi rehta.  Kahaniyan kehti hain ki ek bhoot kisi ko bulata hai, aur jo aage jaata hai woh paani me kho jaata hai.  Yahan har saal log doobte hain.  Humne wahan teen raatein bitayi — ek police chowki ke saamne, ek ghat par, aur ek samundar ke kinare.  Jaanch.   Pehli raat: koi awaaz nahi, sirf lehar aur hawa.  Doosri raat: ek aadmi paas aaya, jise humein laga ki koi hai — woh nashe me tha aur bhatak gaya tha.  Humne use chowki tak chhoda.  Teesri raat: ek awaaz jo gaane jaisi thi — ek dukaan ka radio jo khula reh gaya tha.   “Bulaya jaata hai” wali baat do doobne ki ghatnaon se banti hai.  Police record ke mutabiq yahaan zyada tar maut un logon ki hoti hai jo raat me sharab pi kar paani me jaate hain, ya unhe tairna nahi aata.  Kaali ret mein pair dhans jaata hai, aur raat me dhara samajh nahi aati.   Teen raat ke baad humne wahan ek board pada dekha — “Raat me paani me na jaiye.”  Kisi ne use nahi hataya.  Bhoot ki kahani ek police warning hai jo “darawani” hai, isliye log use yaad rakhte hain.  Humein bhi yahi karna hai: kahani yaad rakhiye, par paani se door rahiye.
