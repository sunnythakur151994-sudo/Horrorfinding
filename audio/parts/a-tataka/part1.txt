Raasta ek hi tha, aur woh van se hoke jaata tha.  Van ka naam Tataka van tha.  Kaha jaata tha ki waha ek rakshasi rehti hai jo raaste ko band kar deti hai.  Rishi Vishwamitra ne Ram se kaha: jao use hataa do.  Ram chhote the.  Unka haath abhi hai.  Unhone pehli baar dekha ki hathiyaar uthana kaisa hota hai.  Katha ka peelu hissa.   Ramayan kehta hai ki Tataka pehle ek yakshini thi — khubsurat, balwan — jo ek rakt-bili se shraapit hui aur rakshasi ban gayi.  Ye kahani kehta hai ki bhoj aur shap ke baad insaan kaise badal jaata hai.  Ram ne use mara, aur van me se raasta khula.   Is prasang par sabse zyada sawaal khada hota hai: ek rakshasi ko maarna — kya ye jeev ki raksha tha, ya sirf raasta banana?  Kahani koi jawab nahi deti.  Woh bas itna batati hai ki raasta khula aur aage chalne wala bachcha bada ho gaya.   Meri nani ise sunate hue ek baat jodti thi: “Van me koi raasta band karta hai, to pehle usse poochho woh kyun khada hai.”  Ramayan me Ram ne poochha nahi.  Hindavi kahani me poochh na ek bade sawaal ki shuruaat hai, jawab nahi.
