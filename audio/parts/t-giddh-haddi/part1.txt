Ek haddi, lal kapde mein lipti.  Sadhu keh rahe the: “Jahan sona dabaya hoga, wahan ye garam hogi.  Aap keval haath mein leke ghar ke aangan mein ghoom lijiye.”  Daam chha hazaar — “aap pehle insaan hain jinhe main de raha hoon”.  Garam hone ka kamaal.   Haddi garam hoti hai.  Iska karan koi khazana nahi hai — iska karan hanth ki garmi hai.  Koi bhi cheez jise aap chalees minute tak muthi mein dabaye rakhein garam ho jaati hai.  Jo aadmi aapko ye “test” karne ko kehta hai, wo aapko sirf apna haath dikha raha hai, zameen ke neeche kuch nahi.   Is family ne haddi ko paanch din tak thanda-garam kiya, aangan mein ghooma, aur phir raat mein khud khudai shuru kar di.  Wo puraane ghar ke neeche tak pahunch gaye.  Kuch nahi mila — ek ghul raha pipe mila, aur ek daraar jo baarish ke paani se poori tarah kamzor ho chuki thi.  Chhat ke ek hisse ko sahara dena padta hai aaj.  Giddh ka sawaal.   Is kahani ka wo hissa jo sabse dard deta hai, wo haddi ka aana hai.  India mein giddh ki aabadi pichhle tees saal mein tabaah hui hai — daklofenak jaisi dawa ki wajah se, jise giddh ke marel huye jaanwar ke shareer mein jaane par unke gurde fail jaate the.  Aur yahan log haddi ko “jaadu” samajh kar kharid rahe hain.  Giddh pehle se hi mar raha hai; hum uski haddi bhi bech rahe hain.
