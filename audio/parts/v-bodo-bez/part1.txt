Assam me “bez” ka matlab jadu-tantra nhai, ek aisa daava hai jisme kisi ko bimari, bhool ya badkismati di ja sakti hai.  Goalpara ke ek gaon me do parivaron ke beech zameen ka jhagda tha, aur ek raat baad bade beta raat bhar ghoomta raha.  Ilzaam ka rukh.   Ladke ne kaha kisi ne usse “pakad” liya.  Gaon ne seedha ungli uthai — samne wale ghar ki burhiya par.  Woh viddhwa thi, akeli, aur uske bete ne zameen bech di thi.  Beech raat uske ghar ka darwaza khata khata.  Mere mama, jo gaon me doctor nahi the par compaundar the, wahan pahunche aur unhone woh cheez dekhi jo koi nahi dekhna chahta tha: ladka nashe me tha.   Woh teen din se bhang aur nasha kar raha tha kyunki uski mangetar ki shaadi kisi aur se tay ho gayi thi.  Raat bhar ghoomna, ulti, aur “koi aa raha hai” — sab nasha wapas utarne ka kaam tha.  Par gaon ko tantra wali kahani zyada pasand aayi, kyunki kahani me zameen ka jhagda chhup jaata hai.   Us raat maine ek baat seekhi: jab gaon me kuch samajh nahi aata, to woh “bez” ka naam le leta hai.  Aur us naam ka nishana aksar woh hota hai jo sabse kamzor ho.  Mayong ke museum me jo kahaniya lagayi gayi hain, unhe padhne ke baad mujhe lagta hai ki asli tantra to yahi hai — kisi ke darr ko kisi aur ke khilaf istemal karna.
