Kolkata ki National Library ek purani haveli me hai — Belvedere Estate.  Warren Hastings yahan rehte the, aur ek samay me yahan ek duel bhi hua tha.  Library ke staff ki kahani: raat me kitaabein girती hain, kursiyan khinchti hain, aur ek aawaz dhoop me bhi sunai deti hai.  Jaanch.   Hum ek staff member ke saath raat 11 baje tak ruke.  Do baar kitaabein girin — dono baar ek hi almari se.  Almari ka ek khana dhila tha.  Uske upar AC ka vent tha, jo thanda hawa chhodta tha.  “Kursi khinchne” ki awaaz lift ke darwaze se aati thi.   “Hastings ki aatma” wali baat Belvedere ki purani history se judi hai.  Haveli me ek duel hua tha — par usme koi Hastings ke khandaan ka nahi mara tha.  Jo aawaz hai, woh ek 200 saal purani imarat ki hawa, lakdi aur lift hai.   Library ke staff ne mujhe ye bhi bataya: raat me imarat me sirf do log hote hain.  Unke liye woh aawazein darr nahi, bas “kaam ka alarm” hain — agar almari gir jaaye to kitaab kharab hogi.  Unhone mujhe ek baat kahi jo mujhe pasand aayi: “Bhoot ho ya na ho, akela aadmi raat me yahan nahi rehta.”
