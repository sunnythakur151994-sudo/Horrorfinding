Purano me Shiv ke saath ek poora gana chalta hai — bhoot, pishach, pret, gana, aur unke sardar.  Unhe dekh kar devta bhi raste chhod dete hain.  Unki baraat raat me nikalti hai, shmashan se guzarti hai, aur jahan se guzarti hai wahan ka mahaul badal jaata hai.  Baraat ka raasta.   Ek gaon me aaj bhi kehte hain: raat ko agar shor, dhol aur kisi anjaan baraat ki awaaz aaye, to raaste se hat jao.  Maine ek baar woh kiya — raat 11 baje, gaon ki gali me dhol aur shor.  Main ek deewar se chipak gaya.   Woh shiv ki baraat nahi thi.  Ek shaadi ki baraat thi jo raat me gaon se guzar rahi thi, aur usme nashe me log the.  Par gaon ki baat galat bhi nahi thi — us baraat se rasta chhodna sahi tha.  Woh log nashe me the, unke haath me lathi thi, aur raat me kisi bhi baraat se door rehna samajhdari hai.   Purano ki baraat aur gaon ki baraat me ek hi niyam hai: jo raat me kisi ke raaste par aaye, usse dur hat jao.  In dono me bhoot ho ya na ho, khatra asli hota hai.  Shiv ke gana ki kahaniyan sunne me achhi hain.  Gaon ke raaste par wo niyam kaam ka hai.
