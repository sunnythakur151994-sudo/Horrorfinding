Kuttanad me zameen sagar se neeche hai.  Khet me paani, raaste me paani, aangan me paani.  Bachche shuru se hi kashti chalana seekhte hain, aur shareer ka sabse bada khatra paani hai.  Ye baat kahani me ghati nahi — chhota bachcha paani me gir sakta hai.  Woh saaf raat.   Main apne nana ke saath kashti se lautar raha tha.  Raat 9 baje.  Dhaan ke khet sunne the, hawa thami hui thi.  Nana ne achanak kashti ka bhaar mehsoos kiya — kashti thodi dheemi ho gayi, jaise kisi ne kinara thaam liya ho.  Unhone jor se maara.  Ek pal baad humne chhota sa khaalira dekha — ek vyakti ka lin, ya koi bachcha gaadha hone ka intezaar.   Wo ek saaye jaisi cheez nahi thi.  kisi ne kinare par aag jala rakhi thi; ek ladka machhli pakad raha tha.  Usne hume daraya nahi, usne bas paani me haath badhaya.  Par nana ne kashti nahi rok di — unhone kaha, koi doosra ho to bachega nahi.  Main aaj bhi uske chehre ko ek maut ke bhram ke saath jodta hoon.   Kerala ki lakhon kahaniyan paani se hain: yakshi, athiswara, mrenu.  Inke pichhe ek asli dar hai jo har saal baadh me laut aata hai.  Kahani tabhi badi hoti hai jab sach itna bada ho ki sach ko hi bhool jayein.
