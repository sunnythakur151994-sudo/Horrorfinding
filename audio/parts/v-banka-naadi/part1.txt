Banka ke ek gaon me ek chhoti nadi thi jo har saal ek hi jagah se bahar nikal kar kheton me chali jaati thi.  Gaon ke buzurg kehte the ki us mod ke neeche ek saanp rehta hai jo paani ko apni taraf bulaata hai.  Isliye us jagah ko “naag mod” kehte the.  Naag mod.   Barsaat ke baad humne wahan khudai karwai.  Gaon wale bura maan gaye.  Teen foot neeche purani deewar ki neev mili — ek chhota pul jaisa dhancha jo kisi zamane me paani rokta tha.  Woh ab toota hua tha, isliye paani har saal rukh badal kar usi taraf aata tha.   Saanp koi nahi tha.  Ek purana nirman tha jise kisi ne bhula diya.  Panchayat ne us jagah ko saaf karwaya, aur us saal gaon me pahli baar baadh ka paani kheton me nahi gaya.  Buzurg chup rahe, phir unhone kaha: “Naag bhi theek hi kehta tha.”   Isse mujhe ek baat samajh aayi.  Gaon ki kahani ne us jagah ko yaad rakha jahan paani khatarnaak tha.  Kahani galat thi, par uski nishan-dehi bilkul sahi — usne ek asli khatre par ek board laga diya tha, bas us par “naag” likh diya tha.
