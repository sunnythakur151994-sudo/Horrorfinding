Chambal ke kinare ke gaon me har bachche ko tairna zaroori tha.  Isi liye paani ko le kar ek ajeeb sa dar mila hua tha: kahani kehti thi ki behte paani me doobne wale ka jism kabhi nahi milta, aur jo milta hai woh chalta hai.  Ghaat par raat ko roshni dikhti hai.  Ghaat ki roshni.   Meri nani kehte the ki unke chacha ek raat ko ghaat gaye, aur wahan ek diya jal raha tha.  Unhone use uthaya — nahin, unhone use nahi uthaya.  Unhone use dekha, chhooa, aur woh diya unke haath me bujh gaya.  Us raat ke baad se unke kaan se khoon aata tha.   Baad me pata chala ki us raat ghaat par ek shaadi ka samaan khula tha.  Diya wahi tha.  Kaan ka khoon kuch aur tha — unke kaan me infection tha jo unhe kabhi maloom nahi hua, aur ek raat unhe kaan se khoon aane laga.  Par gaon me bas yahi chala ki unhe “us” ne chhua.   Chambal ki darr ki asli wajah behta paani nahi hai.  Yahan daku, gareebi, aur nojawan ladkon ke ghunghat the — aur unke chale jaane ne gaon me sapne chhod diye.  Baaki sabhi jagah ki tarah, yahan bhi khaali jagah ko bharne ke liye kahani chahiye thi.
