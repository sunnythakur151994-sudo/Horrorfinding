Kolli Hills jaane waale koi bhi driver aapko ek baat bata dega: gina hua mod khatam hone ke baad aage mat jaaiye.  Kuch mod raat me do baar aate hain.  Bus ka driver, jise sab “anna” bulate the, har baar yahi kehte the — route me nahi, sabar me hai.  70 ke baad.   Hum late laut rahe the.  Barish ne hills ko chamka diya tha.  Anna gaadi dheemi kar rahe the.  69va mod aaya, 70va aaya — aur phir sadak wahi lagne lagi.  Unhone gaadi rok di.  Humne kaha aage badhaiye.  Unhone headlight band kardi.  Andhere me humne sirf barish suni.   Unhone kaha ki pahadi raaste me barish ke baad aksar mitti dhasak jaati hai.  Ek baar unki gaadi ek mod par ruki thi jo aage nahi tha — aage gadda tha.  Us din se woh barish me gina mod ke baad rok ke rukte hain, ek lambi saans lete hain, aur dheere chalte hain.   Woh raat unke liye bhoot ki nahi, barish ki thi.  Aur hum jo 71va mod samajh rahe the, wo actually barish me ek aisa jhukav tha jo pehle kabhi nahi dikha.  Bhoot ho ya na ho — pahadi par raat, barish, aur guarantee chahiye hoti hai to ruk jaana sabse samajhdari ka kaam hai.  Us raat us driver ne humein bachaya, aur yahi asli kahani hai.
