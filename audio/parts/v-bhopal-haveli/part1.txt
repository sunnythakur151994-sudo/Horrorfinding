Shahjehanabad ki ek haveli me ek kursi thi jo darwaze ki taraf mud kar rakhi thi.  Gaon kehte the ki uspar us ghar ki aakhri bahu baithti hai, jo kisi raat ko chali gayi thi aur wapas nahi aayi.  Kursi ko kisi ne nahi chheda.  Kursi mudti kyun hai.   Ek baar maine use mudaya.  Raat ko ghar me kholi ki awaaz aayi.  Subah kursi phir darwaze ki taraf thi.  Main dar gaya.  Phir pata chala ki haveli ka chowkidaar roz shaam us kursi ko wapas ghuma deta tha — kyunki uska apna yakeen tha ki aisa karne se ghar me sukoon rehta hai.   Us bahu ki kahani sach thi: woh ek shaadi ke jhagde ke baad apne maayke chali gayi thi, aur do saal baad unki maut hui.  Uske baad kisi ne unki kursi nahi hilayi.  Ek kursi poora ghar bana gayi.   Gaon me aise nishaan har ghar me hain — ek joota, ek thaali, ek kursi.  Woh bhoot nahi hain, par woh kisi ki kami ka sabse saaf nishan hain.  Unhe dekhte waqt “unke saath kya hua” poochhna, “wahan kya rehta hai” nahi.
