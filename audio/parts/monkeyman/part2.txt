Ek tasveer mere dimaag mein us jagah pahunch gayi thi jahan pehle sirf puraana saamaan rakha tha.  Pahra dene waale bhi thake the.   Hamaari gali mein log der tak jaagne lage.  Koi torch rakhta, koi bas doosre ghar ki chhat par baithe aadmi se baat karta.  Raat ke do baje ki baatein aksar bahut saadhaaran hoti thin—paani kab aayega, kaam kitne baje jaana hai, bachche soye ya nahi.  Un saadhaaran baaton ke beech achanak koi poochhta, “Udhar kuch hila?”  Aur sab ek saath chup ho jaate.   Us raat ki cheekh ke baad jab hum neeche pahunche, meri chhoti behen ro rahi thi.  Usne kuch dekha nahi tha.  Use bas laga ki main chhat par reh gaya hoon.  Main uske paas baith gaya.  Bahar ab bhi log poochh rahe the ki kaun aaya tha.  Ghar ke andar sawaal chhota tha: sab aa gaye?   Pita waapas upar gaye, munder ke paas giri charpai seedhi ki aur hamaari chadarein le aaye.  Unhone kisi praani ka bayaan nahi diya.  Kaha, “Aaj neeche hi soenge.”  Garam farsh par hum paas-paas let gaye.  Neend der se aayi, par behen ne mera haath chhod diya.  Jab shehar phir soya.   Afwaah ka zor ghata toh zindagi apni zarooraton ke saath wapas aayi.  Logon ko subah kaam par jaana tha.  Humein bhi hawa chahiye thi.  Hum phir chhat par sone lage.  Maine tanki ke paas ki bori din mein khud hataayi.  Usmein puraane kapde the.
