Dilli ki us garmi mein hum chhat par sote the.  Neeche kamre ki deewarein din bhar ki dhoop chhodti rehti thin.  Upar kam se kam hawa thi.  Phir ek raat pados ki chhat se awaaz aayi: “Aa gaya!”  Main neend se utha toh pata bhi nahi tha ki kis se bhaagna hai.  Bas sab bhaag rahe the.  Kisi ki chappal reh gayi, kisi ki charpai ulat gayi.  Mere pita seedhi ke saamne khade ho gaye aur bole, “Ek-ek karke.  Dhakka mat do.”   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Jiski shakal koi ek nahi bata saka.   2001 ka Delhi Monkey Man scare is kalpanik parivaar ki kahani ka sandarbh hai.  Us daur ki reporting mein raat ka darr, alag-alag daave aur police ki jaanch milti hai.  Lekin ek hi praani ki pakki pehchaan nahi milti.  Kisi bayaan mein dhaatu ke panje the, kisi mein laal aankhein, kisi mein chhaton ke beech lambi chhalaang.  Har badhti tafseel ne use aur aasaani se sochne laayak banaaya, zaroori nahi ki aur sachcha.   Mere liye uska pehla roop akhbaar mein bana sketch tha.  Maine use der tak dekha.  Phir shaam ko paani ki tanki ke paas ek kaali bori dekhi toh wahi chehra yaad aaya.  Bori hilli nahi.  Main use dekhkar bhi nazar hata nahi paa raha tha.
