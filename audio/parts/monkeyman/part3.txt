Isse har us waqt ki shikayat ka jawaab nahi mil jaata.  Darr aur choton ko halka kehna aasaan hai jab raat guzar chuki ho.  Lekin khabar mein kisi daave ka chhapna, ya kisi ka ghabraakar gir jaana, us daave waale jeev ka saboot nahi banta.   Mujhe us daur ki sabse saaf yaad kisi panje ki nahi hai.  Woh seedhi par khade mere pita hain—khud jaage hue, khud pareshan, phir bhi humein dheere utarne ko keh rahe hain.  Dilli ki us raat mein hamaare liye sabse zaroori awaaz wahi thi.
