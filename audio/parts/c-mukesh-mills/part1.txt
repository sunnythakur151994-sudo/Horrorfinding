Mazgaon ki Mukesh Mills 1980s me bandh ho gayi.  Tabah chhat, khaali shed, aur samundar ki nami.  Yahan shooting ke liye log aate hain — kyunki ye jagah film ke liye perfect hai.  Aur kai units ne yahan kaam ke baad bataya ki kuch “hai”.  Jaanch.   Ek unit ke lightman ne bataya ki raat me unhe kandhe par haath ka ehsaas hua.  Dusre ne kaha ki ek aurat ka dupatta dekha.  Humne wahan ek raat bitayi.  Shed me hawa ka rukh ajeeb tha — ek darwaze se andar, doosre se bahar.  Samundar ki nami ne chhat ki chadar ko bhaari kar diya tha.   Raat 2 baje humne woh cheez suni — ek khich-khich, jo deewar ke andar se aati thi.  Woh purane lohe ke pipe the, jinme samundar ki hawa ke saath paani ghusta tha.  “Kandhe par haath” wala hissa: chhat se girti pattiyan, jo chalte hue kandhe se takraati thin.   Uski ek aur wajah thi — mill ki chhat ka ek hissa itna kamzor tha ki ASI jaisi koi agency nahi, par BMC ne usse band kar diya.  Jo log wahan shot karte hain, unhe chhat aur bijli se darr lagna chahiye.  Woh cheez real hai.  Aur woh cheez zyada khatarnaak hai.
