Mehrauli ke Archaeological Park me ek chhota kamra hai jisme do qabrein hain — Jamali aur Kamali.  Kaha jaata hai ki yahan raat ko koi jinn guzarta hai, aur jo andar jaata hai woh bahar nahi aata.  Ye kahani Delhi ke school ke bachchon me sabse popular hai.  Jaanch.   Hum din me gaye.  Kamra khula tha, ek guard bahar baitha tha.  Andar do qabrein, ek chhoti khidki, aur deewar par likha hua tha.  Guard ne kaha ki raat me yahan koi nahi ruka.  Humne poocha ki phir kahani kahan se aayi, to unhone ek baat batayi jo aaj bhi sach hai: yahan ke aas-paas ek jungle hai, aur raat me yahan bijli nahi hoti.   Baahar park me sadiyon purane khandahar hain, aur raat me yahan saanp, kutton ke jhund aur bhatke hue log aate hain.  ASI ne is jagah par “raat me entry mana” ka board lagaya hai — safety ke liye, bhoot ke liye nahi.  Us board par bhoot ka zikr nahi hai.  Log us board ko padhte nahi, kahani padh lete hain.   “Jamali ka jinn” ki kahani 1500s ke ek sufi sant aur unke saathi ki yaad se judi hai.  Unke bare me likha hua kaafi hai.  Unki qabrein ek heritage hain — aur unke kamre me raat ko ghus kar “proof” dhoondhna na jaan ko khatre me daalna hai, na unki izzat ko.
