Goa me aise ghar bahut hain — chhajje, rang birange khidki, aangan me phool.  Kuch band pade hain kyunki unke maalik Portugal chale gaye.  Ek aise ghar se raat me gaana aata tha.  Gaon ke ladke wahan jaane se darte the — par padosi ke aangan me chup kar sunte zaroor the.  Gramophone.   Main ek shaam wahan gaya.  Ghar ke aage chhota sa mandir tha, aur ek budha aadmi roti baant raha tha.  Unhone kaha ki woh gaana unki maa ka hai.  Ghar me unke bhai rehte the jo pichle saal mare the — aur aaj bhi gaana bajta hai.   Andar ja kar pata chala ki gramophone nahi, ek purana radio tha jise kisi ne chhota generator par chalaya tha.  Gaon ke bachche raat me wahan jaate the aur radio chalate the.  Phir aage baat badhi.  “Bhoot” ka dar wahan raat ko jaanvar aur chor se bachane ka bahana ban gaya tha.   Us ghar ki asli kahani ye thi: ek budha akela reh gaya tha, aur gaon ke bachche uske ghar ko bhool gaye the.  Jab unhone suna ki wahan se gaana aata hai, to kuch log wahan jaane lage.  Kuch gaane sunate the, kuch unke liye khana le jaate the.  Bhoot ki kahani ne unke paas log pahuncha diye — aur ye is site ka sabse ajeeb sach hai.
