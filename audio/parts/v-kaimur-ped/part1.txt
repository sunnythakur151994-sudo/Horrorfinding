Kaimur ke ek gaon mein bargad ke neeche se raat ko nikla ek bhi aadmi baad mein apni zameen par khada nahi mila — aisa nahi ki maut tay thi, bas hosla jawab de deta tha.  Teen saal mein teen maut hui, aur teenon raat ki thi.  Gaon ne ped ko rakshas keh diya.  Kisi ne use kaat diya.  Ped katta, darr nahi gaya.   Ped katne ke baad bhi gaon ke log raat ko us jagah se ghoom ke jaate.  Ek chhota-sa pile ka dher bhi wahan pada tha, jo kabhi ghaas mein duba rehta.  Main us raat wahan tha, kyunki mera dost apni behen ko paani laane ke liye usi raaste bhejta tha aur use rasta badalne ki baat samajh nahi aati thi.   Raat 9 baje hum wahan khade the.  Sabse pehle koi awaaz nahi thi.  Phir air mein ek halki khusar-phusar.  Torch se dekha toh jabra thi — koi teen gaon ke ladke, jo wahan malai lene aaye the.  Woh bhi hum se darr gaye.  Do darr ek saath takraye aur ghar bhag gaye.   Baat pata chalne par gaon ko apni kahani ka sabse bada hissa bhoolna pada: ek aisa raasta jo bilkul andhera tha, jahan saanp, khabad, aur baarish me gaddha ban jaata tha.  Teen maut ke asli kaaran wahi the.  Ped ko kaat dene se raasta gaya nahi — bas us raat ke baad kisi ne wahan jaana band kar diya, aur darr wapas baith gaya.
