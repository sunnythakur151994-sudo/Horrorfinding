Chennai ke ek ladkiyon ke hostel me aakhri kamra hamesha khaali rehta tha.  Warden kehti thin kuch nahi hua, par usme koi nahi rehna chahti thi.  Raat ko wahan se paani ki tapak aur ek purana Tamil gaana sunai deta tha.  Woh gaana.   Main us kamre me do raat rahi.  Pehlip raat tapak suni.  Doosri raat gaana bhi suna — ek radio, jo kisi ne chalu chhod diya tha.  Kamre me ek purana window jis se tapak rahi thi.  Aur gaane wala radio us kamre ki aakhri rehne wali ladki ka tha, jisne hostel chhod diya tha.   Woh ladki padhai chhod kar gaon chali gayi thi.  Uska naam gaon me chalta tha — “wo jo pagal ho gayi”.  Kamre ka “bhoot” uski yaad thi, kisi aur cheez ka nahi.  Usne 2 mahine kuch nahi khaya, sirf radio suna.  Warden ko lagta tha kamre me problem hai, aur ladki ko nahi.   Warden ne mujhe bataya ki woh ladki ab theek hai.  Kya woh sach me theek hai?  Nahi maloom.  Par ek baat pata thi: hostel ka bhoot kaafi saal tak us ladki ka naam bacha raha — aur uske saath uski bimari ka naam bhi chhupa raha.  Har jagah, har kahani me, wahi hota hai.
