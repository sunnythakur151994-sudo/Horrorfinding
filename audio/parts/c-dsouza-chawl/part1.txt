Mahim ke D'Souza Chawl ke beech ek kuaan hai, jise uthli se dhaka gaya hai.  Log kehte hain ki ek aurat wahan raat 9 baje khadi dikhti hai, aur jo usse dekhe woh bimaar pad jaata hai.  Kuch logon ne bataya ki woh apne baal pehne parde me hai — wahi shakal jo Mumbai ki purani film me thi.  Jaanch.   Hum do log gaye.  Chawl ke logon ne bahut kuch sunaya.  Humne kuein ki uthli khud ka ali — gili, phislan.  Kuein ke andar paani ka level kam tha aur usme kachra pada tha.  Hawa me ek ajeeb badbu thi.   Aadhi raat ko humne khud dekha: ek safed shakal.  Woh ek aurat thi jo kuein ke paas kapde dhone aayi thi — raat me, kyunki din bhar paani nahi hota.  Safed kapde, geele baal, aur kuein ki roshni me uska chehra chamak raha tha.  Usne humein dekh kar kuch bhi nahi kaha.  Usne apni balti uthai aur chali gayi.   Us raat ke baad humne kuein ka paani test karwaya.  Usme bacteria kaafi zyada tha.  Jo log bimaar pad rahe the, unme zyada tar bachche the — jinke ghar ke paas kuein tha.  Chehra real tha, bhoot nahi.  Aur kuein ki water quality ek asli public health issue hai.
