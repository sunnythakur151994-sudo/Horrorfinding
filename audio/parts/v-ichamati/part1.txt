Ichamati ke kinare ke gaon me raat ko koi nadi par phool nahi chhodta.  Kahani kehti hai ki ek aurat paani ke kinare khadi rehti hai aur jo bhi phool le kar jaata hai, usse woh apne paas bulati hai.  Durgapuja ke baad ye khof alag hota hai.  Phool aur paani.   Meri mausi ke gaon me ek ladki raat ko nadi gayi thi pratahkal dekhne.  Wo jaani na thi — kahin galti se to nahi?  Woh gayi, aur wapas nahi aayi.  Teen din baad paani se nikli.  Kahani pehle hi tay ho chuki thi: woh aurat mil gayi.   Par gaon ki police-report me ek baat thi jo kisi ko sunana nahi tha: kinare par chatri ki mitti dhalak gayi thi, upar barish ke baad kai.  Saavdhaan par unhe lagta tha ki ek aurat usse bulati hai, isliye kisi ne mitti ki naali ko theek nahi karaya.   Kuch din baad maine gaon ki auraton ko nadi kinare jate dekha, phool le kar.  Unhone kaha ki ye barish-baandh ke liye hai.  Woh bhoot ko nahi dhoondh rahi thin — woh usi nadi ko theek kar rahi thin jisne ek ladki li thi.  Is file ka sabak yahi hai: kahani ko dosh mat dijiye, kinare ki mitti ko dekhiye.
