Main use uthaane jhuka.  Bahar se aata hua ek padosi ruk gaya.  “Hamare yahan bhi kar dena,” usne kaha.  Woh wahi aadmi tha jo dopahar ko sab par hans raha tha.  Humne uski taraf dekha bhi nahi.  Kuch darr aise hote hain jinmein ek-doosre ki sharm bacha lena aasaan lagta hai.  Pehli dastak.   Raat ko kundi hilli.  Main turant uth baitha, lekin maa mujhse pehle jaag rahi thi.  Doosri taraf se mere bhai ki awaaz aayi.  Woh kaam se der se lauta tha.  Roz ka waqt tha; roz ka andaaz bhi.  Phir bhi maa ne darwaza nahi khola.   “Phone karo,” usne mujhse kaha.   Maine number milaaya.  Bahar phone baja.  Bhai ne uthaakar poochha ki kya hua.  Us ek chhoti-si tasdeeq ke baad maa ne kundi kholi.  Woh andar aaya toh hansne laga, phir hamaare chehre dekhkar ruk gaya.  Usne khaana maanga.  Maa thaali laane gayi aur main darwaze par khada safed akshar dekhta raha.  Mujhe laga, darr ka sabse bada kaam yahi hai: woh pehchaani awaaz ko bhi pehli baar suna hua bana deta hai.  Kal jo aata hi nahi.   Agle din kuch nahi hua.  Uske agle din bhi nahi.  Dheere-dheere log der tak bahar baithne lage.  Bachche phir gali mein khelne lage.  Darwaze par likha raha.  Baarish ne pehle ek akshar halka kiya, phir doosra.  Kisi ne use dobara gehra nahi kiya.   Kaafi baad ghar ki putai hui.  Rang waale ne poochha ki yeh likha hua bhi mita de?  Maa ne kaha, “Haan.”
