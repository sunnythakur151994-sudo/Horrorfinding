Subah darwaza khola toh saamne waale ghar par safed akshar likhe the: Naale Baa.  Dopahar tak gali ke chaar aur darwazon par wahi shabd aa gaye.  Shaam ko maa ne mujhse chalk mangwaayi.  Maine kaha, “Aap bhi?”  Usne mere haath se chalk le li.  “Likh dene se kya jaata hai?”  Mujhe tab laga tha ki darrne waale humse alag log hote hain.  Raat tak meri yeh galatfahmi door ho gayi.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Gali mein pahunchi khabar.   Kannada mein Naale Baa ka matlab hai “kal aana.”  Bengaluru se judi is shehri lok-katha mein darwaze par likhe yeh shabd raat ko aane waali aatma ko agle din lautne ke liye kehte hain.  Jab woh kal aaye, tab bhi uske liye kal hi likha mile.  Is chhote-se taalne mein kahani apna bachav dhoondhti hai.   Hamaari kalpanik gali mein khabar kisi ek aadmi ke paas nahi thi.  Dukaan waala kehta tha ki usne paas ke mohalle mein suna hai.  Paas ke mohalle ka aadmi kehta tha ki uske rishtedaar ke yahan hua hai.  Kisi ke mutabiq aurat apne ki awaaz mein bulaati thi; kisi ke mutabiq bas dastak deti thi.  Chehra kisi ne theek se nahi dekha tha.  Lekin har agla sunane waala pichhle se zyada yakeen mein tha.   Maa ne likha toh chalk ka tukda beech mein toot gaya.
