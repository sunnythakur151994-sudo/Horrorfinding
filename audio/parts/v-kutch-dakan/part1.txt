Kutch ke ek gaon mein “dakan” ka matlab hai ek aurat jo raat ko ghoomti hai.  Koi use nahi dekhta; log bas us ek baat se bachte hain — raat ko apna naam sun kar pichhe mat mudna.  Meri bua 17 saal ki thi jab unhone mud kar dekha.  Kya hua tha.   Woh ghar se pachas kadam door the — aangan ke kuye par.  Unke kaanon mein naam aaya, unki maa ki awaaz me, par unki maa to dilli me thi.  Unhone mud kar dekha.  Kuch nahi tha.  Ghar jaakar unhone tawa garam kiya aur poora baat sunaya.  Dada ne kuch nahi kaha, bas kaha ki raat ko narial waali chhitti pe mat jaana.   Baad mein pata chala ki us raat gaon mein ek kutta khula tha jo aangan ke phaatak se raatein rota.  Awaaz kisi ki nahi, hawa ke rukh aur ghar ke lohe ke jhagad ka kisa tha.  Unki maa so rahi thi, aur dadi ki chik ka swar aage aa gaya.   Lekin ek baat saaf-saaf hai: un adaazon ke gaon mein aurat ko raat ke khauf se ghar ke andar rakhne ka taqaza asli tha.  Dakan ka naam gaon ki aurat ko niyantrit karta tha, aur kahaniyon ko bahana banaya jaata tha.  Aaj bhi jo gaon dakan ko chase kaheta hai, wahan auratein akeli pit rahi hain.  Dar ko sant ya mantar ke ghode ka rukh mat do.
