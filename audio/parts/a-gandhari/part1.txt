Gandhari ne apni aankhein bandhi hui thin — shaadi ke din se, kyunki unke pati Dhritarashtra andhe the.  Usne 100 bete paaye.  Yudh mein woh sab mare.  Jab unhe bataya gaya, unhone aankhein kholi — aur us pal se pehle unhone Kurukshetra ko bina dekhe hi ek shraap de diya.  Shraap ka waqt.   Krishna unke paas aaye.  Gandhari ne kaha: agar maine tumhe roka hota, to kya yudh nahi hota?  Krishna ne kuch nahi kaha.  Gandhari ne kaha: jis tarah mere bete mare, usi tarah tera kul bhi marega — 36 saal baad.   Mahabharat kehta hai ki woh shraap poora hua.  Dwarka ka vinaash hua, aur Yadav kul khatam ho gaya.  Kahani ka darr ye hai ki ek aurat ne sirf aankhein khol kar itna bada shraap diya tha.  Uski aankhon me ek bhi aansu nahi tha, kyunki usne saalon se aankh band ki hui thi.   Mere liye ye Mahabharat ka sabse bhayanak hissa hai.  Koi rakshas nahi, koi bhoot nahi.  Ek maa thi, 100 laashein, aur ek aisa shraap jo apne hi ghar ko ukhaad dega.  Darr aksar sabse shant logon se aata hai — kyunki jab woh chup rehte hain, to sab kuch andar hi jalta rehta hai.
