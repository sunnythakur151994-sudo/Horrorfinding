Woh apne kaka ko bulaata hai.  Awaaz mein hukm nahi, bharosa hai—wahi bharosa jisse hum bachpan mein kisi bade ko pukaarte hain aur maan lete hain ki ab kuch bura nahi hoga.  Ek awaaz ka reh jaana.   Mujhe is kahani ka yahi hissa sabse bhaari lagta hai.  Hum aksar uske baad kya hua, uski daraawni tafseel dhoondhte hain.  Lekin main pukaar aur jawaab ke beech ki jagah par atak jaata hoon.  Woh chhota-sa waqt, jab bulaane waala abhi ummeed kar raha hota hai.  Jab use abhi nahi pata hota ki madad aayegi ya nahi.   Lok-katha kehti hai ki woh pukaar Wada mein lautti hai.  Log use raat se, khaamoshi se, kabhi poornima se jodte hain.  Magar kisi awaaz ki kahani dohraayi jaana us awaaz ka pramaan nahi.  Jo itihaas mein hua, aur jo baad mein uske baare mein mehsoos kiya gaya, dono ko ek hi cheez keh dena us dukh ko bhi badal deta hai.   Main jis deewar ke paas khada tha, us par dhoop sarak rahi thi.  Ek chhota ladka apne pita se aage nikal gaya.  Pita ne uska naam liya.  Woh palatkar daudta hua wapas aaya aur unka haath pakad liya.  Bahut saadhaaran baat thi.  Phir bhi main use dekhta reh gaya.  Ghar lautne ki awaaz.   Baad mein jab hum bahar aaye, dost ne poochha ki mujhe kuch sunaai diya tha kya.  Maine mana kiya.  Main koi awaaz sunne nahi aaya tha, aur koi recording mere paas nahi thi.
