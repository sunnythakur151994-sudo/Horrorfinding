Shaniwar Wada mein main ek darwaze ke saamne zaroorat se zyada der khada raha.  Mere aas-paas log tasveerein le rahe the.  Kisi ne bachche ko kinaare hone ko kaha; kisi ka phone baja.  Sab kuch din ke ujale mein tha.  Phir bhi, Narayanrao ki kahani sunne ke baad mujhe har darwaza ek hi sawaal jaisa lag raha tha: jab koi jaan bachaakar aapki taraf bhaage, toh aap kis taraf khade hote hain?   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Mahal ke andar ka darr.   Narayanrao ki 1773 mein hui hatya Shaniwar Wada ke itihaas ka hissa hai.  Is ghatna ke aas-paas saazish, satta aur parivaar ke tanaav ki bahut-si baatein kahi gayi hain.  Lekin us itihaas ko lok-katha mein badalne waali cheez ek pukaar hai: “Kaka, mala vachva”—Kaka, mujhe bachao.   Aage ka drishya usi riwaayat ka kalpanik punarkathan hai.  Mahal ka ek raasta socho jise ek naujawan bachpan se jaanta hai.  Kaunsa mod kis kamre tak jaata hai, kis dehleez par pair sambhaalna padta hai—use sab yaad hai.  Aaj usi pehchaan par uski jaan tiki hui hai.  Peeche kadam tez ho rahe hain.  Woh mudkar dekhna chahta hai, lekin mudne mein bhi waqt lagega.   Us pal uska pad, uski gaddi, uska naam usse bachane nahi aa sakte.  Use ek apna chahiye.
