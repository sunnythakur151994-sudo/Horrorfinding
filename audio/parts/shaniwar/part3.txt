Lekin ek baat thi jo main seedhe keh nahi paaya: kuch jagahon par darr isliye nahi lagta ki wahan koi anjaan maujood hai.  Darr isliye lagta hai ki kabhi wahan apne maujood the, aur phir bhi koi akela reh gaya.   Us shaam ghar pahunchkar maa ne doosre kamre se bulaaya.  Main aam taur par “aaya” kehkar phone dekhta rehta hoon.  Is baar turant uth gaya.  Shaniwar Wada ki kahani mere saath kisi bhoot ki tarah nahi aayi thi.  Woh bas ek chhoti-si aadat badal gayi: koi apna bulaaye, toh jawaab dene mein der mat karo.
