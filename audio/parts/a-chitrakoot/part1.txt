Chitrakoot me Ram ka vanvaas tha.  Mandakini behti thi, ped the, aur zindagi saadi thi.  Sita ek din ek pipal ke neeche gayi.  Us ped ke neeche ek aurat khadi thi, jise kisi ne nahi dekha tha — us ne Sita ko kaha: tum rajkumari ho, tum yahan kyun ho?  Us aurat ka naam.   Ramayan me aisi koi aurat nahi hai.  Par Chitrakoot ki lok-katha me hai — ek van-devi, jo har vanvaasi se poochhti hai ki woh ghar kyun chhod kar aaya.  Sita ne kaha: main apne pati ke saath hoon.  Devi ne kaha: phir tumhara ghar tumhare saath hai.   Maine ye katha Chitrakoot ki ek budhi se suni.  Unhone kaha ki woh devi “bharat ki har aurat” hai jo ghar chhod kar aayi.  Aur us katha me Ramayan ka sabse bada sach hai — vanvaas mein sabse zyada dukh Sita ka tha, aur use koi pucchta bhi nahi tha.   Ramayan ek dharm-granth hai, par woh ek ghar ki kahani bhi hai.  Jab aap Chitrakoot jaayein, aur wahan ki hawa dekh kar “bhoot” ki baat karein — ek baar us aurat ke bare me bhi sochiye jise apna ghar chhodna pada tha.  Uska dukh kisi shraap se kam nahi tha.
