Raat ko chhat par lete hue main pehle taaron ko dekhta tha.  Us garmi mein main sirf roshni dhoondhta tha—koi chhoti-si chamak jo achanak meri taraf aaye.  Neeche se maa ne bulaaya ki andar aa jaao.  Kamra bhatti ki tarah garam tha.  Chhat par hawa thi, lekin ab neend nahi thi.  Kisi ne ek naam de diya tha, aur us naam ne khula aasmaan bhi chhota kar diya tha: Muhnochwa.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Khabar jo har ghar mein badli.   2002 mein Uttar Pradesh se judi Muhnochwa ki khabrein aur afwaahein is kisse ka sandarbh hain.  Neeche ka parivaar kalpanik hai.  Unki gali mein pehli khabar chai ki dukaan par aayi: kisi par raat mein kuch jhapta.  Doosre aadmi ne roshni jodi.  Teesre ne kaha ki woh chehra nochta hai.  Shaam tak har kisi ke paas uski shakal thi, aur koi do shaklein ek jaisi nahi thi.   Baba ne kaha, “Suni-sunaai baat hai.”  Phir unhone torch mein naye cell daale.  Maine unhe dekh liya.  Unhone mujhe dekh liya.  Hum dono ne kuch nahi kaha.  Ghar ke bade aksar bachchon ko bharosa dene ki koshish mein apna darr chupaa lete hain; kabhi-kabhi bas torch ki nayi roshni use zahir kar deti hai.  Roshni ka chhota tukda.   Us raat gali ke doosre sire se cheekh aayi.
