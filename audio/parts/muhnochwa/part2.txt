Main charpai se uchhal kar khada hua.  Saamne ki chhat par koi torch ghuma raha tha.  Uski roshni meri aankhon par padi aur ek pal ke liye mujhe kuch nahi dikha.  Main peeche hata.  Baba ne meri baanh pakad li.  Munder meri soch se zyada paas thi.   Neeche log poochh rahe the ki kya hua.  Har jawaab mein “suna hai” tha, lekin bolne ka andaaz “dekha hai” waala hota ja raha tha.  Kisi ne kaha ki agle mohalle mein hamla hua.  Agle mohalle se aaye aadmi ne kaha ki usne toh hamaari taraf ki awaaz suni thi.  Khabar ghoomkar apne shuru hone ki jagah dhoondh rahi thi, aur use woh jagah nahi mil rahi thi.   Maa mujhe neeche le gayi.  Baba bhi aa gaye.  Unhone torch mez par rakhi; roshni chhat ki taraf thi.  Pankhe ki thami hui parchhaai deewar par badi lag rahi thi.  Maine poochha, “Aapne dekha?”  Unhone der se kaha, “Nahi.”  Mujhe us raat unka yeh chhota jawaab sabse sachcha laga.  Naam se pehle nishaan.   Shikayat, chot aur ghabraahat ko suna jaana chahiye.  Lekin ek hi naam ke neeche aayi har baat ka ek hi kaaran maan lena bhi galat hai.  Is ghatna ke baare mein alag vyakhyaayein di gayi hain; yahaan kisi ki chot ka door se diagnosis nahi kiya ja raha.  Udte praani ki lokpriya tasveer ko pramaan samajhne ka aadhaar nahi hai.   Kuch din baad hamaari kalpanik gali dobara der tak jaagne lagi—ab baatcheet ke liye.
