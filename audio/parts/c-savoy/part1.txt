Mussoorie ka Savoy 1902 se chal raha hai.  Yahan ek writer ne khud ko mara tha — woh sach hai.  Uske baad se hotel me ek “lady in grey” ki kahani chalti hai, jo kamre ke darwaze par aati hai.  Staff kehte hain woh guest ki dress me hoti hai.  Jaanch.   Hum office ke record tak nahi pahunche, par ek purane staff se baat hui.  Unhone bataya ki “lady” wali kahani 1960s se hai, jab ek guest ne raat me help ke liye bell bajai thi aur phir koi nahi mila.  Us raat hotel ke staff me sirf do log the.   Hotel ka dhancha aisa hai ki purane wings me darwaze bilkul ek jaise hain.  Log galat kamre khol dete hain.  Ek baar humne khud ek aurat ko corridor me dekha — woh ek guest thi jo apna kamra dhoondh rahi thi.  Humne use raasta dikhaya.  Us raat “lady in grey” ka wahi silsila tha.   Writer ki maut ke bare me jo sach hai, woh bhoot ki kahani se badi hai: ek aadmi akela tha, aur usne dard me aisa kiya.  Hotel ne us kamre ko band nahi kiya.  Aaj wahan log rehte hain, aur kahani chalti hai.  Asli dukh ko hotel marketing me badal dena sabse ajeeb cheez hai jo humne dekhi.
