Sonepur ka mela duniya ka sabse bada pashu mela kaha jaata tha.  Mela khatam hone ke baad maidan khaali hota hai.  Ek raat ek budhe aadmi ne wahan haathiyon ki chain suni.  Woh apni nar na, gaya, aur phir usne uski taraf dekha.  Chain jo baji.   Wo aadmi mela dekhta tha.  Usne kaha raat me ek haathi lapata tha aur gaon ke log uski talash me the.  Mela khaali tha.  Chain ki awaaz us haathi ke pairon se aayi thi.  Kuch der baad unhe chain mili — kisi ne use ped me latka diya tha, jisse hawa mein awaaz aati thi.   Mela ke baad ka maidan ajeeb jagah hota hai: bhookhe pashu, bache hue khaane, aur sookhi zanjeerein.  Gaon ke bachche wahan chain paate hain aur bajate hain.  Woh awaaz raat me sunai de to khaufzada hone ki zaroorat nahi, par wahan jaana mana hona chahiye kyunki wahan janwar ke leye khali jagah nahi hoti.   Us raat us aadmi ne mujhe kuch kaha jo main use karta hoon: “Bhoot ki kahani tab tak chalti hai jab tak koi un cheezon ko dekhne wahan na jaye jo asli hain.”
