Bellandur ke ek jheel ke kinare raat ko log paani me girte the.  Darr ki baat ye thi ki wahan koi peene nahi jaata tha — bas ghoomne.  Gaon kehte the ki jheel ka koi “bulaava” hai.  Do khambhe.   Maine ek raat wahan guzari.  Jheel ke kinare pathar, ghaas, aur ek toota hua railing.  Hawa me ek ajeeb si badbu thi, aur aankh me jalan hoti thi.  Main 15 minute me wapas aaya.  Agle din pata chala ki us jheel me kabhi kabhi jhag ka gas banti hai, jo kam hawa me zameen ke paas jam jaati hai.   “Bulaava” koi nahi tha.  Log railing ke paas jaate, ghaas gili hoti, aur ek jhag se sar chakra jaata.  Do logon ne kaha ki unhe koi kheench raha tha — aur woh sahi keh rahe the.  Unhe jhag khud kheench raha tha.   Shahar ke kinare-paas ke jheel ab gaon nahi hain — par unki kahaniyan wahi hain.  Asli khatra: railing, gili ghaas aur gas.  Bhoot ki kahani sunane se pehle taar lagaiye.  Ek pothole, ek band railing — inka “mantar” asli hai.
