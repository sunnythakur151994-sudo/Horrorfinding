Vetala ne apna pehla sawaal poochha.  Ek raja ne ek rakshas ko maarne ke liye jangal me jalaaya, aur woh rakshas ne uski baat maani.  Us rakshas ne kaha: ek baat suno.  Raja ne kaha: suna do.  Aur raja ne apni shart bhooli — jo jawaab dega, woh phaans jaayega.  Katha aur shart.   Kahani me ek brahman tha jiske ghar me ek chor aaya.  Brahman ne chor ko pakad liya.  Chor ne kaha mujhe chhod do.  Brahman ne use chhoda.  Us raat chor ne brahman ka gala kaat diya.  Agli subah woh brahman mara hua mila.  Raja ka pradhan bada hua.  Aur usne nirnay sunaya.   Vetala ne poochha — brahman sahi tha?  Chaudhary ne kaha: brahman galat tha, kyunki usne chor ko chhoda.  Vetala ne kaha: jawaab theek hai.  Laash uda gayi.  Raja ko phir wapas jaana pada.   Aur yahi is katha ki sazа hai.  Raja ko galat jawaab dene se nahi, sahi jawaab dene se sazaa milti hai.  Aaj bhi aisa hi hota hai — jab aap sach bolte hain aur mazaak khatam ho jaata hai, log aapse naaraaz hote hain.  Vetala ka darr ye nahi ki woh laash hai.  Darr ye hai ki woh aapki zubaan ko aapke haath se alag kar deta hai.
