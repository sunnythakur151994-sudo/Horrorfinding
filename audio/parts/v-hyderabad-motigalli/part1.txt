Hyderabad ke Motigalli ke ek bungalow me koi nahi rehta tha.  Raat 1 baje kursi ghaseetne ki awaaz aati, aur ek purana fan chalta rehta.  Paanch logon ne ye suna.  Main bhi unme se ek tha.  Kursi.   Hum chhat se jhaanke.  Andar ek bujhta diya tha, aur ek aadmi kursi par baitha tha — kursi hil rahi thi, kyunki woh nouskha nahi, bas kursi ka ek pair adha toota hua tha.  Aadmi purana chowkidar tha jo bungalow ke malik ke jaane ke baad bhi rehta tha.   Unhone bataya ki unke malik ne unhe bachpan me yahan rakha tha, aur wahi unka ghar ban gaya.  Ab woh 78 saal ke the aur wahi rehte the.  Humne paanch din tak uski awaaz sun kar bhoot ki kahaniyan banai — aur us raat unki kursi ke pair par darr raha.   Us raat ke baad humne unke liye kursi ka naya pair marammat karwa diya.  Awaaz band ho gayi.  Aur gaon ki bhoot ki kahaniyan bhi.  Main aaj bhi sochta hoon — hum kitni aasaani se kisi ke ghar ko khaali bhookh ke naam par darr ki jagah bana dete hain.
