Konark ke mandir ka ek hissa gir gaya.  Baaki khada hai, par uske andar kuch andhera hai jise log mehsoos karte hain.  Kaha jaata hai ki kaal diye gaye nirmaan shilpi ne poori raat kaam kiya tha, aur jab mandir ka patthar nahi utar paaya, to unhone apni jaan de di.  Chaka aur suraj.   Maine wahan ka chaka dekha — chhote-chhote jharokon me, ek chakra jiski har kiran me ek insaan hai.  Jo patthar itna saaf tarasha gaya hai, us par us shilpi ke haath ka nishaan hai.  Woh patthar kis kaam se bacha tha, kisi ko nahi maloom.   “Konark ka bhoot” wali kahani usi toote patthar se banti hai.  Asli baat ye hai ki mandir ka kuch hissa 600 saal pehle gir gaya, aur ASI ne use bacha liya.  Jo log us toote patthar par khade hokar darr ki kahaniyan sunaate hain, unhe shilpi ki mehnat ki kahani sunani chahiye.   Maine us raat socha: bhooot ki kahaniyan aksar toote hue kaam ke around banti hain.  Magar jo cheez kaafi bhaari ho, woh khud hi darr aur shraddha dono bana deti hai.  Konark me mujhe koi aatma nahi dikhi.  Mujhe chhote patharon par khade log dikhe — aur unki thakan ka puraana hisaab.
