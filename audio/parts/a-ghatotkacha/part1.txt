Raat ka yudh tha.  Kurukshetra me din ka pahaad banta tha, par raat sirf rakshason ki thi.  Pandavon ki taraf Ghatotkacha lada — Bhima aur Hidimbi ka beta.  Woh aakash me khada tha, badalon ko kheenchta tha, aur Kaurava sena ki neend chhin leta tha.  Ek astra, ek baat.   Karna ke paas ek aisa astra tha jo usne Arjun ke liye rakha tha.  Us raat Duryodhana ne zor diya: ye rakshas abhi mara jaana chahiye.  Karna ne apna ek hi bacha hua astra chhod diya — Ghatotkacha par.  Woh gira.  Uska maut ka weight Pandavon ne Bheem ke haath par mehsoos kiya.   Kahani kehti hai ki us raat Pandavon ne ek bhookhe rakshas ko bhejnay ki koshish nahi ki; unke paas ek rakshas beta tha jo unke liye lada.  Kurukshetra me koi safed-pushpak nahi tha.  Sab ghoomte hue dhuze, khoon aur khaas the.   Is katha me mujhe sabse zyada ye lagta hai: Ghatotkacha ko sabne “rakshas” kaha, par kisi ne uska pita ban kar unka haath nahi thaama tha.  Is site par hum in kahaniyon ko horror ki tarah padhte hain, par ye bhi yaad rakhiye — kabhi kabhi asli darr ye hota hai ki koi apne “hum” me nahi aata.
