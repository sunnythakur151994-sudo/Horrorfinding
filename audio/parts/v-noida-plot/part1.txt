Noida ke ek sector me ek plot khaali pada tha.  Uske chaaron taraf towers the.  Plot par koi imarat nahi thi, sirf ek toota hua boundary wall aur kuch jhopadiyaan jinme koi nahi rehta tha.  Raksah kehte the wahan raat ko ek mazdoor aur uski aurat dikhte hain.  Jhopdi.   Main ek raat wahan gaya.  Jhopdi me ek chulha tha, ek thaali, aur ek bachche ka joota.  Bahar ek chipped si chadar thi.  Main wapas aaya aur doosre din site ke record dekhe.  Us plot par teen saal pehle ek accident hua tha — ek mazdoor gir kar mara, aur uska parivaar wahan hi reh gaya tha.   Wo jhopdi khaali nahi thi.  Us raat main wapas pahuncha to ek ladki thi jo apne bhai ke liye roti bana rahi thi.  Unke papa ki maut ke baad unke paas rehne ki jagah nahi thi, isliye woh site par hi neend guzaarte the.  Gaon ne unhe “bhoot” keh diya kyunki unhe dekhna asaan nahi tha.   Us plot ka sach developer ke record me nahi hai.  Woh ek khaali plot ke bhoot ki kahani nahi, ek safety ki kahani hai — kyunki mazdoor gir kar maare jaate hain aur unke parivar bina kisi cheez ke reh jaate hain.  Kahani wahan bhoot ki nahi hai.  Wahan ek darr ki nahi, ek hisaab ki zaroorat hai.
