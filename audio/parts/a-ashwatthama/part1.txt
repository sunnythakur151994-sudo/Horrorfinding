Yudh khatam ho gaya tha.  Pandav jeet gaye the, aur jeetne wale apne dero me so rahe the.  Us raat Dronacharya ka beta Ashwatthama jungle me baitha tha, aur uske paas ek hi baat thi: mere pita ko dhokhe se mara gaya.  Uska dukh ek astra ban gaya.  Raat ka yudh.   Usne Shiv ka naam liya, aur ek astra chhoda.  Kahani kehti hai ki woh astra unke apne sar par hi laga.  Usne apne pita ka naam liya aur ek brahman ko mara — aur us brahman ki patni ka shraap uske peechhe aa gaya: teri maut bhi nahi hogi, tu sirf jiya karega.   Mahabharat kehta hai ki uske sar se ek ratna nikal kar liya gaya, aur uska shraap yahi tha ki sadion tak woh ghaav aur dard ke saath jeevit rahega.  Woh apne pita ke liye lad raha tha, aur usne apne hi haathon se sabse bada anyay kiya.  Isi liye ye kahani itni bhaari hai — jo sabse zyada pyaar karta hai, wahi sabse zyada andha hota hai.   Maine ise gaon ke ek buzurg se suna tha.  Unhone aakhir mein kaha: “Beta, shraap kisi ne nahi diya.  Woh apne aap ko us raat ke baad chhod nahi paaya.”  Aur ye shayad sabse sach wali baat hai.
