Mujhe is kahani mein ped se latakti deh se zyada woh baar-baar palatkar chalta aadmi yaad rehta hai.  Thaka hua, jhunjhlaaya hua, phir bhi ek kathin sawaal ka aasaan galat jawaab dene ko taiyaar nahi.  Vetala uski raah rokta hai, lekin usi raaste par uski soch bhi saaf hoti hai.   Raat khatam hoti hai.  Kissa band hota hai.  Sawaal phir bhi paas baitha rehta hai: agar sahi jawaab dene se tumhara kaam aur mushkil ho jaaye, tab bhi kya tum use bologe?
