Kuch anaaj bhi log utha le jaate hain.  Subah maalik pahredaar ko doshi kehta hai.  Pahredaar apni galti nahi chhupaata: usne darwaza khola tha.  Lekin woh yeh bhi kehta hai ki us waqt use ek awaaz sunaai di thi, aur us awaaz ka jawaab dena tha.  Raja bol pada.   Vetala poochhta hai, “Kya pahredaar ne apna kartavya toda?  Usse godaam bachaane ko rakha gaya tha.”   Vikram kuch kadam chup chalta hai.  Use shart yaad hai.  Phir woh kehta hai ki jaan bachaana us pal pehla kartavya tha.  Chori ki jaanch baad mein ho sakti thi; aag mein mar chuke insaan se baad mein koi sawaal nahi poochha ja sakta.  Darwaza kholna aur chori karna ek hi kaam nahi.  Ek ko doosre ka dosh dekar nyaay nahi hoga.   Kandhe par wazan halka hua.  Hansi sunaai di.  Vikram ko samajh aa gaya ki phir bol pada hai.  Vetala ped ki taraf laut chuka tha.  Raja kuch pal wahin khada raha, phir wapas muda.  Uske jawaab ne kisi khel ko nahi jeeta tha.  Phir bhi woh use wapas nahi leta.  Aakhri mod.   Paramparik katha-chakra ke ant ki taraf Vetala raja ko us tapasvi ki chhupi chaal se saavdhaan karta hai jisne use deh laane bheja tha.  Raja aakhir sirf aadesh maanne se nahi, samajhkar faisla lene se bachta hai.  Alag versions mein vivaran badalte hain; yahaan unhe ek hi aitihasik ghatna nahi maana gaya.
