Laash ka wazan pehli baar uthaate hue Vikram ko jitna laga tha, waapsi mein usse zyada lag raha tha.  Kandhe par kapda bheeg chuka tha.  Peeche pedon ki shaakhein hil rahi thi.  Raja ne kadam sambhaale.  Use bas ek kaam karna tha: is deh ko le jaana, aur raaste bhar chup rehna.  Tab uske kaan ke bilkul paas awaaz aayi—“Raja, raasta lamba hai.  Ek kahani sunoge?”   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Jawaab ki keemat.   Vikram aur Vetala ki kathaaon ke kai roop milte hain.  Is punarkathan mein wahi parichit dhaancha hai: deh mein Vetala, use laata raja, aur raaste mein poochha gaya sawaal.  Jawaab jaante hue chup rehne par anisht ki shart hai; jawaab dene par maun tootega aur Vetala laut jaayega.  Raja ko sirf bal se nahi, apne vivek se bhi parakha jaata hai.   Is baar Vetala jo kahani sunaata hai, woh is edition ke liye likhi gayi ek nayi paheli hai.  Ek kasbe mein raat ko anaaj ka godaam jalne lagta hai.  Pahredaar ke paas uski chaabi hai.  Andar se ek aadmi ki awaaz aati hai.  Pahredaar jaanta hai ki koi andar hona nahi chahiye.  Shayad chor hai.  Darwaza khole toh anaaj lootne ka khatra hai; band rakhe toh aadmi mar sakta hai.   Woh darwaza kholta hai.  Aadmi bach nikalta hai.
