Tantra sahitya me shmashan ka ek alag sthan hai.  Wahan Kali ki upasana hoti hai — raat me, diye ke saath, aur ek aisi dhyan-avastha me jisme darr apne aap girr jaata hai.  Gaon wale ise “kali maa ka basera” kehte hain.  Raat me wahan diya dikhta hai.  Diya aur aadmi.   Meri bua ke gaon ke shmashan me ek aadmi raat ko diya jalata tha.  Gaon ke log use “pagal” kehte the.  Unhone kisi ko kuch nahi bataya.  Wo sadhak the — ek parampara ke, jisme shmashan-sadhana hoti hai.  Wo wahan dhyan karte the, aankh band, aur ghar laut jaate the.   Yeh kaam karna aasan nahi hai.  Shmashan me raat ka mahaul, jaanvar, aur akelapan — sabse bada dushman andar ka darr hota hai, bahar ka nahi.  Unhone mujhse kaha: “Jo yahan darr ke aata hai, woh darr ke hi chala jaata hai.”   Is file me koi vidhi nahi hai, aur jaan-boojh kar nahi hai.  Tantra sahitya ka samajhna aur uski nakal karna do bilkul alag baatein hain.  Jo mujhe samajh aaya, woh ye: unki raat ki sadhana ka sabse bada hissa discipline tha — roz, ek hi waqt, bina kisi ko dikhaye.  Darr wahan sirf ek aadat thi jise unhe chhodna tha.  Humein bhi wahi chhodna hai.
