Shekhawati ke woh khaali haveli hamare gaon ki sabse badi imarat thi.  Saath darwaze, ek aangan, aur chhat par chhoti si baurdi.  Aaj usme kisi ka koi nahi.  Lekin wahan ek aadat bani hui thi: raat ko pachhe ka darwaza khud khul jaata tha.  Darwaza aur hawa.   Mere mama, jo bijli ke karamchari the, ek raat wahan gaye the.  Unhone darwaza khula paaya, andar mashaal jaisi roshni dekhi, aur bahar nikal aaye.  Agle din unhone wahi rakha hua daulat ka peepa dekha — andar kuch nahi tha.  Unhone apni baat gaon me nhi kahi.  Ek mahine baad haveli gir gayi.   Haveli ka khatra that ki uski deewarein mitti ke lohe se bandhi thin.  Woh andar se gili ho jaati thi.  Darwaza khula isliye mila kyunki baarish se uski lakdi phail gayi thi aur kundi theek se nahi baith rahi thi.  Andar ki roshni kisi chirag ki nahi thi — woh aas-paas ke khet me ja rahi kisi gaadi ki headlight thi, jo toote darwaze se aangan me pad rahi thi.   Yahan ka asli horror ye hai ki haveli 2010 me aaj bhi khadi hai, aur gaon ke bachche uske andar cricket khelte hain.  Kahaniyan khaali jagahon ko bahana deti hain — aur wo bahana kisi ki jaan bacha sakta hai.  Girne wali imarat ke bahane asli the; aatma se darr banaya gaya.
