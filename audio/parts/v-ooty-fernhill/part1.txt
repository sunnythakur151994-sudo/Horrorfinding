Ooty ke ek purane palace hotel me raat ko piano bajta tha.  Manager kahte the staff baja raha hai, staff kahta tha manager.  Guests kehte the ek madam ka bhoot hai jo apni beti ko dhoondhti hai.  Do saal me hotel ki booking gir gayi.  Piano ke andar.   Main wahan ek raat ruka.  Piano ke paas ek purana harmonium tha, aur uske peeche ek khidki jo hawadar se takrati thi.  Raat 1 baje piano bajana shuru hua — par awaaz ki taraf dekha to khidki ka kaanp raha tha, aur piano ke taar us hawa se goonj rahe the.   Us hotel ka sach isse bhi zyada ajeeb tha.  Ek purana staff member raat ko wahan aata tha aur hawa ki khidki band karta tha, par kabhi piano ke taar ko theek nahi karwaya.  Kyunki piano ki kahani aaane wale guests ko kheenchti thi.  Manager ne khud kaha: “Bhoot se booking aati hai.”   “Madam ka bhoot” wali kahani bhi hotel ki brochure me thi, bina kisi source ke.  Aaj wahan ek renovation hui, aur piano wahan ek concert ke liye theek kiya gaya.  Awaaz bandh ho gayi.  Booking wapas aayi — is baar sangeet se, bhoot se nahi.
