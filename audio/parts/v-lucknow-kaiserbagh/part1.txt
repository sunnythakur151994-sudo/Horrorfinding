Kaiserbagh ke ek bade kamre me koi nahi jaata tha.  Raat 2 baje usme pankha chalne ki awaaz aati thi, aur ek baar kisi ne wahan se “dhoop me mat jao” jaisi line suni.  Us kamre par ek purana khauf tha jo vyapaari zyada bol rahe the.  CCTV ka sach.   Ek kiraaydar ne wahan CCTV lagwai.  Do raatein kuch nahi hua.  Teesri raat, 2:40 baje, darwaza khula aur ek aadmi andar aaya.  Woh chowkidaar tha jo garmi me wahan sota tha.  Pankha woh chala deta tha.  “Awaaz” uski sansein aur uthne ki khifin thi.   Kamre ki asli kahani purani haveli ki malik thi: woh kisi zamaane me us kamre me rehti thi, aur uska koi waris nahi tha.  Kiraaydar ne kamre ko lock kar diya tha, aur uska khauf bolne se chalne laga.   Kahani khatam nahi hui — sirf badal gayi.  Ab log kehte hain chowkidaar ko “wo” bulati hai, isliye woh raat 2 baje wahan jaata hai.  Woh chowkidaar ke paas jawaab hai: “Wahan fan hai, aur mujhe bahut garmi lagti hai.”  Kabhi kabhi sabse darawni sach sabse saada ho sakta hai.
