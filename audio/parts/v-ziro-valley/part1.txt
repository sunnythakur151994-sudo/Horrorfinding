Ziro valley me chawal ki kheti sabse khoobsurat dhalee hai.  Gaon me ghar bade hote hain, aur raat ko pichhli khidki khuli rehti thi.  Ek raat mehku bhai ne wahan se kuch dekha tha, aur unhone hamesha ke liye sheesha laga diya.  Khidki se aawaz.   Unhone kaha ki raat ko kisi ne unka naam liya — bahar se.  Unhone khidki me se jhaanka.  Andere me ek insaan ka silhouette tha.  Woh ek bachcha tha jo gaon ke raste bhool gaya tha.  Unhone use naam se pukara, to woh bhag gaya.  Subah woh bachcha paani ke kinare mila, bhookha, thanda, par zinda.   Gaon walon ne use daanav nahi kaha.  Unhone bas ek niyam bana diya: raat ko pichhli khidki khuli nahi chhodni.  Aaj us gaon me nahi hai koi pichhli khidki — sab me sheesha hai.  Bacche mazaak me ab bhi poochhte hain ki “khidki kyun bandh hai?”   Har gaon ka ek sach hai jo kahani me chhupta hai: yahan kisi ke ghar ki pichhli khidki jungle ki taraf kholti hai.  Raat me wahan se koi insaan, koi jaanvar, ya koi hawa aa sakti hai.  Sheesha ek dar ka nahi — ek kaarname ka part hai.
