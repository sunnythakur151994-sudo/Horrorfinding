Bara Imambara ka Bhool Bhulaiya 1784 me bana.  Isme 489 darwaze hain aur sirf ek asli exit.  Kaha jaata hai ki andar jaane wale kuch log kabhi bahar nahi aaye — unhe raste me koi mila, koi aawaz aayi, koi haath pakdaane ko aaya.  Jaanch.   Hum guide ke saath andar gaye.  3 minute me hum bhool gaye ki hum kis taraf se aaye.  Corridor tang, chhat neechi, aur hawa nahi.  Guide ne kaha: “Bina guide ke aaye to 2 ghante lag jaayenge.”  Humne 11 minute me 3 door — aur sabhi band.   “Aawaz” wali baat aaj bhi hoti hai.  Andar awaaz 4-5 baar goonjti hai, aur saamne se aati lagti hai.  Log use “koi hai” samajh lete hain aur ghabra kar bhaagte hain, phir kisi doosre corridor me pahunch jaate hain.  Ek baar ek tourist aadhi raat tak andar rah gaya, aur security ne use nikala.   Ye imambara ek engineering ka kamaal hai.  Uska “bhoot” uski architecture hai.  Darr ka asli kaaran: andhera, tang raasta, aur doosri taraf jaane wale log.  Kahani sunne me maza aata hai, par bina guide ke andar na jaana sabse samajhdari ki baat hai.
