Ramappa temple ke bare me do ajeeb sach hain: ek, ki ye apne shilpi ke naam par hai — jo shayad hi kisi mandir ke saath hua ho.  Doosra, iske patthar (brick) paani me tairte hain.  Aur ek kahani hai ki raat me shilpi ka bhoot kaam karta hai.  Jaanch.   Hum shaam ko wahan gaye.  Mandir ke bahar ek chhota board tha, jise ASI ne lagaya hai: “Raat me entry mana.”  Andar ek guard tha.  Unki baat: “Yahan koi shilpi nahi aata, sirf bandar aate hain.”  Us raat humne patthar par khich-khich suni — woh ek bandar hi tha.   Shilpi ki kahani isliye bani ki mandir ka naam Ramappa hai, aur mandir ke andar ek shilpi ki murti hai.  Kaam ki shreshthatā ne ye kahani banai ki woh aaj bhi kaam kar raha hai.  Par asli sach ye hai ki 1213 me is mandir ko banane me saikdon logon ka jeevan laga — unke naam kisi register me nahi hain.   UNESCO ne 2021 me Ramappa ko World Heritage status diya.  Ye site ka sabse bada imaandari ka hissa hai: hum ek mandir ko bhoot ki jagah banate hain, jabki woh insaan ke hunar ki jagah hai.  Us raat humne us shilpi ki murti ke saamne khade ho kar unhe salaam kiya — jinhe naam nahi mila.
