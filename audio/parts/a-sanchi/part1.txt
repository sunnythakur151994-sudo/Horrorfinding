Sanchi ka stoop 2,300 saal purana hai.  Uske charon or chhoti chhatriyan hain, aur ek seedhi jo andar jaati hai.  Raat ko wahan ek bhikshu khada dikhta hai — kaha jaata hai woh us stoop ka rakhwala hai.  Ek raat humne seedhi par chad kar dekha.  Stoop ke andar.   Andar kuch nahi tha.  Ek pathar ka dhancha, hawa, aur ek chhoti roshni.  Wo roshni ASI ke chowkidaar ki torch thi.  Unhone humein seedhi se utaara aur daanta.  Uske baad unke bhookh se baten ki — unhone bataya ki unke pita bhi yahan chowkidaar the.   Stoop ke bare me shraap ki koi kahani nahi hai.  Jo picture “bhikshu ki chhaya” ki hai, woh Sanchi ke ek chhote se mandir ke pujari ki hai, jo raat ko diya jala kar jata hai.  Log use usi roshni me dekhte hain, aur kahani ban jaati hai.   Sanchi ki asli shakti ok hai.  Wahan 2,300 saal se log aate hain, aur kisi ne kuch nahi dekha jo proof ban sake.  Par us ek stoop ke andar jaana mana hai kyunki usme hawa nahi hai.  Kahani ko mana karna itna asaan hai.  Isliye ab bhi wahan “bhoot” kehti hai — board par likha hai: andar mat jaiye.
