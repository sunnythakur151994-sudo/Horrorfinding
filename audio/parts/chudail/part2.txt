Dadi uski baat kabhi poori nahi bataati thi.  Woh kehti, “Jo suna hi nahi, use dohraayenge kaise?”    Subah kisi aur ka naam.   Raahi ki kahani ka ant har baar badal jaata.  Kabhi woh mandir pahunchta, kabhi ghar; kabhi subah log use raaste mein paate.  Lekin mere liye uska ek doosra ant tha.  Agle din main gali ki ek akeli rehne waali aurat ko dekhkar usse bachkar nikal gaya.  Uske baal khule the.  Bas itni-si baat mere bachpan ke dimaag ko kaafi lagi.   Dadi ne dekh liya.  Usne mujhe waapas bulaaya aur us aurat ko paani diya.  Phir mere saamne usse ghar ki chhat ki marammat ke baare mein baat karti rahi.  Woh dono hansin bhi.  Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha tha ki raat ki kahani aur dopahar ki yeh baat ek hi duniya mein kaise hain.   Aur jab woh aurat chali gayi, dadi ne kaha, “Kahani ka darr kisi insaan ke peeche mat laga dena.”  Is punarkathan mein mujhe sabse zaroori wahi line lagti hai.  Usne kahani ka maza nahi chheena.  Usne bas uski seema bata di.  Darr ko uska ghar do.   Chudail aur daayan ke naam alag ilaakon mein alag maayne aur kahaniyan lete hain; unhe ek hi tayashuda roop kehna sahi nahi.  Yahan ulte pair waala drishya ek kalpanik horror-kissa hai.  Kisi ki shakal, akelapan, umr ya zindagi ki mushkil uske khilaaf saboot nahi banti.   Aaj bhi kabhi hawa se darwaza hile toh dadi ki awaaz yaad aa jaati hai.
