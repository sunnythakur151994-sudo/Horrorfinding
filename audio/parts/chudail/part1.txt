Dadi kahani sunate waqt sabse pehle diye ki baati chhoti karti thi.  Main kehta, “Roshni kyun kam kar rahi ho?”  Woh kehti, “Tel bachega.”  Mujhe lagta tha ki woh jaan-boojhkar andhera kar rahi hai.  Phir woh khet ke paas waale ped ki baat shuru karti, aur main apne pair charpai par upar kar leta.  Uski kahani mein ek aurat hoti thi.  Uska naam kabhi nahi hota tha.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Pair jinhe dekhna nahi tha.   Dadi ke kisse ka raahi mela dekhkar laut raha tha.  Jeb mein bache do sikke aapas mein bajte the.  Woh thaka hua tha aur ghar ki roti soch raha tha.  Ped ke neeche ek aurat ne use roka.  “Aage tak chhod doge?”  usne poochha.  Raahi ne bina soche haan kar di.   Kuch door chalne par use ajeeb laga ki aurat ke kadam ki awaaz uski chaal se nahi mil rahi.  Jab woh rukta, do kadam aur padte.  Jab chalta, awaaz der se shuru hoti.  Woh neeche dekhna chahta tha, lekin andhera tha.  Aur phir, kahani mein, baadal hata.  Chaand ki roshni mein usne pair dekhe—ulte.   Yahin main dadi ka haath pakad leta.  Woh thoda rukti, mujhe paani deti, phir aage sunaati.  Raahi ne bhaagne ke bajaay saamne door jalta diya dekha aur usi taraf chalna jaari rakha.  Aurat uske kaan ke paas kuch kehti rahi.
