Hiranyakashipu ko var mila tha: na din me maro, na raat me; na ghar me, na bahar; na insaan se, na kisi jaanwar se.  Usne sabko kaabu me kar liya — apne hi bete Prahlad se nafrat ki kyunki woh Vishnu ko pujaata tha.  Sandhya ka waqt.   Woh apne bete ko maara.  Prahlad ne kaha Vishnu hum hain.  Usne ek khambhe mein mazaak se maara — zara to dikha, is khambhe me Vishnu.  Khambha phat gaya, aur usme se kuch nikla jo insaan nahi, jaanwar nahi.  Ek sher jaisa, ek insaan jaisa.   Woh samay itna sahi tha ki var lagu na ho — din nahi, raat nahi, sandhya.  Jagah itni ajeeb thi ki var lagu na ho — dehleez, ghar nahi, bahar nahi.  Woh usse dehleez par rakha, apne godh me, nakhunon se.  Kaid ho — var ka.  Isliye Prahlad ke Prahlad rehne ko wo katha aaj bhi gaate hain.   Kahani ka sabse darawna hissa devta nahi hai.  Woh ek pita hai jo apne bachche ko marne chala tha, kyunki bachcha apne Vishnu ko nahi chhod raha tha.  Darr yahin se aata hai — pyaar aur haq ka jakadna.
