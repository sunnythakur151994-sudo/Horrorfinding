Kahani kehti hai ki log aakhir saath chhodne ka faisla karte hain: apni marzi bachane ke liye apni zameen se juda hona padega.   Meri maa ne us ladki ka naam nahi bataya.  Maine poochha bhi nahi.  Darwaze par khadi meri badi behen baar-baar apna dupatta sambhaal rahi thi.  Pehli baar mujhe laga ki bade log bhi kabhi-kabhi bina samjhe hue chup ho jaate hain.  Unke paas jawaab nahi hota; phir bhi unhe bachchon ka haath pakadkar aage chalna hota hai.  Jo saath nahi jaa saka.   Baba ne chulhe ke paas rakha lota uthaya, phir wapas rakh diya.  Potli pehle hi bhaari thi.  Maa ne deewar ki ek darar mein ungli phiraai—usi jagah, jahan barsaat ke baad mitti bhari thi.  Woh darar ab bhi thi.  Ghar se nikalne se pehle ka aakhri kaam bhi adhoora reh gaya.   Bahar kisi ne dheere se awaaz di.  Hum nikal aaye.  Gali mein log the, lekin roz jaisa shor nahi tha.  Ek bachcha neend mein apne baap ke kandhe se lagkar ro raha tha.  Kisi ke haath mein chaabi thi, kisi ke haath mein paani.  Ek budhiya ko do log sambhaal rahe the.  Maine peeche dekha toh apna darwaza khula mila.  Maa ne mera chehra aage kar diya.  “Raasta dekh.”     Kahani isi jagah shraap ka zikr karti hai: jaane waalon ne kaha ki yahan phir koi bas nahi paayega.  Main jab yeh kissa sunta hoon, toh mujhe woh shraap gusse se zyada dukh mein bola hua lagta hai.
