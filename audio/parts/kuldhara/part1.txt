Us raat mujhe sabse zyada darr registan se nahi laga tha.  Darr is baat ka tha ki subah hamara ghar wahi rahega, lekin hum usmein nahi honge.  Maa ne anaaj ki potli baandh li thi.  Baba darwaze ki chaukhath par haath rakhe khade the.  Main apni mitti ki chhoti gaadi dhoondh raha tha.  Jab mili, toh maa ne kaha, “Bas yahi le sakte ho.  Aur kuch haath mein mat lena.”  Bachpan mein ghar chhodna shayad pehle isi tarah samajh aata hai: apni cheezon mein se ek ko chunna.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Faisla jo dheere bola gaya.   Kuldhara ki lok-katha ko is kalpanik parivaar ki aankhon se dekho.  Jaisalmer ke paas Paliwalon ki ek basti hai.  Ghar paas-paas hain, aanganon ki awaazein ek-doosre tak pahunchti hain.  Lekin is shaam baat karne waale bhi awaaz neeche rakh rahe hain.  Bachchon ko jaldi khila diya gaya hai.  Kisi ne bartan dhokar ulte nahi rakhe.  Kaam ruk gaya hai, jaise sab kisi ek khabar ka intezaar kar rahe hon.   Mashhoor kisse mein hukmraan Salim Singh ki nazar gaon ki ek ladki par padti hai.  Uski ichchha ke peeche hukumat ki taaqat hai.  Ladki aur uske logon ke liye mana karna sirf ek rishta thukraana nahi reh jaata.  Khatra poori basti tak aa pahunchta hai.
