Jaise koi apne ghar se keh raha ho—agar hum tumhare nahi rahe, toh tum kisi aur ke kaise ho jaaoge?  Yeh meri padhat hai; us raat ke asli shabd hamare paas nahi hain.  Subah ko kaun bulaayega?.   Hum ret par chalte rahe.  Mujhe neend aane lagi.  Maine maa se poochha, “Kal wapas aayenge?”  Usne kaha, “Abhi chalo.”  Tab main us jawaab ko samajh nahi saka.  Bahut saal baad laga ki uske paas bhi kal ki koi tasveer nahi thi.   Kuldhara ke khandahar dekhte hue aaj bhi us kal ko socha ja sakta hai.  Ek chhota kamra, deewar ke saath baithne ki jagah, bahar nikalti gali.  Yahan rehne waalon ki zindagi sirf kisi shraap ki bhoomika nahi thi.  Unka kaam, unki thakan, unke roz ke jhagde aur khushiyaan bhi thi.  Hum khandahar ki photo lete hain; us ghar ki aadat ko kaise tasveer mein rakhein?   Ek hi raat mein poori basti chhod dene wala mashhoor version lok-katha hai.  Bastiyan kyun khaali hoti hain, uska jawaab paani, rozgaar, dabaav aur badalti paristhitiyon mein bhi talaashna padta hai.  Kuldhara ke liye koi ek saral wajah yahaan antim sach nahi kahi ja rahi.   Meri kalpanik yaad mein us raat koi bhoot nahi dikha.  Bas subah ki pehli roshni mein maa ka haath dikha tha.  Uski ungliyon par hamare ghar ki mitti lagi hui thi.  Usne use jhaada nahi.
