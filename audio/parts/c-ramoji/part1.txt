Hyderabad ke film city me ek purane studio ke floor par raat ki shift me kaam karna mushkil tha.  Log kehte the wahan ek junior artist ki aatma hai, jo scene ke beech me lights gira deti hai.  Do haadse hue bhi — koi zyada ghayal nahi hua.  Jaanch.   Production manager ne do camera lagwaye aur ek structural engineer bulaya.  Teen hafte tak recording hui.  Recording me ek raat, 1:40 baje, ek light ka stand jhukta dikha.  Uske baad 3 ghante me do baar aur.  Kisi ko kuch dikha nahi.   Engineer ne stand ke base par rust aur jhukav dekha, aur studio ke floor me seepage.  Purana wiring bhi kaam nahi kar raha tha — short circuit se light flicker kar rahi thi.  “Aurat ka saaya” wali baat ek purani junior artist ki kahani se aayi thi jo wahan kaam karti thi aur film chhod kar chali gayi thi, kisi ki maut se nahi.   Manager ne teeno stands badle aur wiring theek karwai.  Lights girna bandh ho gayi.  Us raat ke baad staff ne kaha studio “saaf” ho gaya.  Sach yahi tha: koi aatma nahi thi, sirf ek zang laga hua stand tha jise kisi ne teen saal se check nahi kiya.
