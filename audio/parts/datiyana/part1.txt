Maine socha tha “Bhootonwala Mandir” ke paas pahunchkar log awaaz dheemi kar denge.  Lekin subah wahan pahunchte hi ghanti sunaai di.  Ek aadmi pooja karke laut raha tha.  Kisi ne jhaadu ek taraf rakha tha.  Main apne saath jo daraawna drishya laaya tha, usmein yeh rozmarra ki shaanti fit nahi ho rahi thi.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Eenton ke paas khada sawaal.   Datiyana, Hapur, Uttar Pradesh ka yeh mandir ek aisi lok-katha se juda hai jisme bhoot vinaash karne nahi, nirmaan karne aate hain.  Mashhoor riwaayat kehti hai ki mandir ek raat mein bana.  Mandir ka hona aur uske banne ka yeh kissa do alag baatein hain.  Meri yatra aur neeche ki baatcheet ek kalpanik narration hain.   Main eenton ko dekh raha tha.  Kisi imarat ko door se dekhne par uska roop dikhta hai; paas jaao toh chhote faisle dikhne lagte hain—ek satah, ek kona, ek jod.  Maine paas baithe buzurg se poochha, “Raat mein ban gaya tha?”  Unhone meri taraf dekha.  “Yahi kahani hai,” bole.  Unhone “maine dekha tha” nahi kaha.  Farq chhota tha, lekin mujhe sunaai diya.  Raat ki kalpana.   Phir maine us raat ko apne dimaag mein banaaya.  Gaon so raha hai.  Kisi ghar mein bachcha karvat badalta hai.
