Bahar bahut halki aahat hai, jaise koi ek eent doosri ke paas rakh raha ho.  Ek aur, phir ek aur.  Na kisi mazdoor ki pukaar, na kisi ka naam.  Bas kaam badhta ja raha hai.   Is kalpanik drishya mein ek aadmi neend se uthta hai, darwaze tak aata hai, phir ruk jaata hai.  Use darr bhi hai aur jaanne ki ichchha bhi.  Woh darwaza nahi kholta.  Subah bahar nikalta hai toh us jagah ek imarat khadi milti hai jahan kal usne khula aasmaan dekha tha.  Kahani sunne waala uske saath hairaan hota hai.  Phir sawaal aata hai: jo haath dikhe nahi, unka naam kaun bataayega?   Yeh drishya lekhak ki kalpana hai.  Lekin isse mujhe lok-katha ki ek khoobi samajh aati hai.  Kabhi kahani kisi cheez ka jawaab dene se zyada uske saamne rukna sikhaati hai.  Purani imarat itni aasaan nahi thi jitni door se dikh rahi thi.  Use banaane mein kisi ka hunar laga hoga, kisi ka waqt.  Jahan log darne nahi aate.   Buzurg uthkar andar chale gaye.  Main bahar reh gaya.  Ek parivaar aaya; bachche ne poochha ki ghanti baja sakta hai kya.  Uski maa ne use utha diya.  Ghanti ki awaaz hawa mein phaili.  Yeh unka pooja ka sthaan tha.  Mere liye sirf ek horror location maan lena bahut chhoti nazar hoti.   Lautte waqt maine mandir ko phir dekha.  Mujhe koi saaya nahi dikha.
