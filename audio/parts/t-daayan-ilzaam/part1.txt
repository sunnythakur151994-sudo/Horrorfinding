Gumla ke ek gaon mein ek vidhwa rahti thi, jo apne teen bachchon ko khud pakata, khud dhoondhti thi.  Jab unka chhota beta bimaar pada aur doctors ne kuch “bada” nahi bataya, to gaon ki ek sabha ne faisla kar diya: ye aurat daayan hai.  Uske baad jo hua, use ye site likh kar kabhi puri tarah dikhayegi nahi.  Itna hi kaafi hai — use gaon se nikaal diya gaya, aur uske bete ko school chhodna pada.  Kaun “daayan” banta hai.   Agar aap India ke in cases ko dekhein, ek pattern bar-bar dikhta hai.  Daayan aksar woh aurat hoti hai jiske paas zameen ho, jo akeli ho, jo vidhwa ho, jo tribal ho, ya jise koi dushmani ho.  Aisa koi case nahi milta jisme “daayan” koi chhota bachcha ya taakatwar aadmi ho.  Ilzaam un logon par lagta hai jinhe bachana mukhil hota hai.   Police records mein aise mamle “witchcraft accusation” ke naam se darj hote hain, aur kai rajyon mein ye alag se apradh hai — Bihar mein Prevention of Witch (Daain) Practices Act, 1999, Jharkhand mein 2001 ka kanoon, Maharashtra mein 2013 ka Anti-Superstition Act, Karnataka mein 2017 ka kanoon.  Matlab: ye sirf galat nahi, ye illegal hai.  Sabse bada darr.   Asli baat ye hai kaun aise ilzaam se sabse zyada darta hai.  Wo gaon ki wahi aurat hai jo sabse pehle mandir jaati hai, jo jhaad-phook par sabse zyada bharosa karti hai.  Darr usi ka sabse mehnga sauda hai.
