Mussoorie se Lambi Dehar jaane wala raasta chuna-patthar ke dher ke beech se jaata hai.  Yahan 1960s tak khaanein chalti thin.  Phir ek din kaam band ho gaya.  Kisi ne bataya ki maalik ne aakhri baar andar dekha aur baad me uski laash mili.  Aaj bhi raat ko wahan kaali kali ghaas aur khaali gufa dikhti hain.  Jaanch.   Hum ek dhopahar gaye.  Gufa ke bahar ka hawa bhaari tha.  Andar jaate hi aankh me jalan — chuna patthar ki dhool aur kai saal ki band hawa.  Hamare saath ek local guide tha, jo bola: “Yahan 10 minute se zyada nahi.”  Hum 8 minute me bahar aaye, sar dard kar raha tha.   Khaan ka band hona asli hai.  Uske aas-paas chuna patthar ki kheti, sookhe kuein aur aadhi giri hui deewarein hain.  Raat ko “aawaz” wahan hawa ke rukh aur chhote pattharon ke girne se aati hai.  Jo log yahan ghoomne aate hain, unme se koi bhi andar 20 minute se zyada nahi rukta — kyunki andar saans lena mushkil hota hai.   “Aadmi ka bhoot” wali kahani kisi verified police file me nahi hai.  Par ek baat file me hai: khaan band hone ke baad yahan kai log ghoomne aaye, aur kuch gir kar ghayal hue.  Unhe bachane ke liye yahan board lagane pade.
