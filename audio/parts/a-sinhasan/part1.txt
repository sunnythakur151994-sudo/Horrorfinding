Raja Bhoj ko ek purana singhasan mila.  Us par 32 murtiyan thin — sundar, patthar ki.  Jab woh baithne gaya, ek murti ne kaha: ruko.  Main tumhe ek katha sunati hoon, phir batao ki tum is singhasan ke layak ho?  32 kathayein.   Har murti ek katha sunati thi — ek raja, ek daan, ek nyay, ek shart.  Har katha ke baad sawaal wahi: kya tum Vikramaditya jaisa ho?  Bhoj har katha sunte, jawab dete, aur phir baithne se ruk jaate.  Aakhir me 32 kathayein khatam hui, aur murtiyan chali gayi.  Singhasan khaali reh gaya.   Yeh kahaniyan aaj bhi Rajasthan, Gujarat aur Bundelkhand me gaayi jaati hain.  Main jab bhi sunta hoon, mujhe lagta hai ki ye singhasan sirf ek aasan nahi hai — ye ek prashn-patra hai.  Kissi bhi kursi par baithne se pehle 32 sawaal hone chahiye.  Aaj ke samay me kaun 32 sawaal se guzarta hai?   Bachpan me mujhe ye katha lambi lagti thi.  Ab lagta hai ki woh sabse zyada imaandar horror hai — kyunki ismein koi bhoot nahi, koi raat nahi.  Sirf 32 sawaal hain jinse bachna nahi tha.  Aur woh 32 sawaal raja ke liye nahi the.  Woh sunne wale ke liye the.
