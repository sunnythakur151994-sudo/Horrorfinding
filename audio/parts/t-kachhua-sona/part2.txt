“Jis jaanwar ko aap khazana samajh kar ghar le jaate hain, wo nadi ka safai-karmchari hai.  Ghar mein wo bhookha mar jaata hai,” unhone kaha.  Jaal aur kanoon.   Bihar, UP aur Bengal mein kachhue ki tijaarat ek bada illegal dhandha hai.  Pakadne wale, thekedaar, aur bechne wale — teenon ek hi zanjeer ke kadi hain.  Aur kachhua ghar mein rakhne wale ko is baat ka pata bhi nahi hota ki wo bhi us zanjeer ka hissa ban gaya hai.
