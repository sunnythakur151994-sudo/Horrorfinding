Chhoti si dukaan, ek steel ka tub, usme paani, aur usme ek tara-kachhua.  Dukaandaar keh raha tha: “Iske pet mein sona hai.  Jis ghar mein ye rahega, wahan khazana khud baahar aayega.”  Daam — chalis hazaar, “aap to jaante hi hain, main aapko kam mein de raha hoon”.  Bazaar ka hisaab.   Kachhua ko sona dhoondhne wala, sona ugalne wala, ya khazana sambhalne wala batana koi naya kaam nahi hai.  Kayi jagah isi kahani ke saath kachhue ki haddi, kachhue ka kavach aur “kachhue ka tel” bikta hai.  Kahani ka dhaancha ek hi hai: ek jaanwar jo dheere chalta hai, isliye lagta hai bahut kuch jaanta hai; aur jo bahut dheere chalta hai, use bechne ke liye zyada mehnat nahi chahiye.   Jo maine us dukaan mein dekha, wo kahani se zyada bhayankar tha.  Kachhue ke kavach par spray paint se “sone” jaisa rang, aankhon mein infection, aur tub ke paani mein wahi gandagi jisme wo hafton se baitha tha.  Uske saath wala doosra kachhua mar chuka tha — dukaandaar ne use ek kapde mein lapet kar peeche phenk diya.  Ek vaigyanik ki baat.   Ek wildlife veterinarian ne mujhe bataya ki kachhua bikta hai kyunki log usse “zameen ka rakhwala” maante hain.  Par asli kachhua ka kaam doosra hai: wo nadi ke andar ki safai karta hai, mara hua maans aur gandi cheezein khaata hai, aur Ganga ko saaf rakhne mein uski bhoomika insaan se kam nahi.
