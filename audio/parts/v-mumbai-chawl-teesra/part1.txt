Mumbai ke ek chawl me teesre floor ka ek kamra band tha.  Us par lakdi pakki thi.  Padosi kehte the raat ko andar se TV chalta hai, aur ek baar ek phool se sajaya hua ek chhota mandir bhi dikha.  Bachche darr ke maare seedhi chadhne se kaat-te the.  TV.   Wo kamra ek budhe ka tha, jo saat saal pehle mara tha.  Uska koi nahi tha.  Par TV ki awaaz sach thi.  Ek raat main neela chamakta roshna dekha.  Padosi ne bataya ki building ke kisi meter me leakage hai, aur uske kamre me ek purana connection chal raha tha.   Jo darwaaze ke neeche se aati awaaz thi, wo building ke pipe ki thi.  Ek baat aur nikli: us budhe ke kamre se lagta hua ek karza document ab bhi kisi ko chahiye tha.  Woh padosi hi the, jo raat ko us kamre me jaate the aur TV chalate the — kyunki kamre me kuch cheez unhe chahiye thi.   Uski koi maut nahi thi.  Ek band kamre ne sabki zindagi me ek ajeeb sa darr bhar diya.  Maine bas utna kiya jo ho sakta tha: building society se kamra kholwaya.  Andar ek TV, ek bistar aur ek dher kapde the.  Bhoot ki kahani khatam.  Suru hui building ki property ki kahani.
