Woh kahani ka sabse daraawna hissa sunna chahte the.  Mujhe bas apne muh se bina soche nikli pehli “haan” yaad thi.   Kuchisake-onna mere liye uske baad ek chehre se zyada ek dabaav ban gayi—woh pal jab kisi aur ko santusht karne ke liye hum apni bechaini ko nazarandaaz kar dete hain.  Kabhi-kabhi kahani padhte hue bhi main pehle sawaal par rukta hoon.  Is baar jawaab dene ki jaldi nahi karta.
