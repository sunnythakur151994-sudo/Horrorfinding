Woh aurat sadak ke beech nahi khadi thi.  Bas kinaare, jahaan se main roz guzarta tha.  Isliye pehle mujhe laga hi nahi ki mujhe rukna padega.  Phir usne poochha, “Kya main khoobsurat hoon?”  Main school ka bag ek kandhe se doosre par kar raha tha.  Sawaal itna achanak tha ki mere muh se aadat mein “haan” nikal gaya.  Usne mask ke kinaare par ungli rakhi.  “Ab bhi?”   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Ek sawaal, do baar.   Japan ki Kuchisake-onna lok-katha ka darr isi dohraaye gaye sawaal mein hai.  Pehli baar jawaab dena aasaan lagta hai.  Doosri baar lagta hai ki pehla jawaab hi tumhe phansa gaya.  Uske naam ka sambandh kate hue muh ki chhavi se hai; is punarkathan mein uski graphic tafseel se zyada us pal par dhyaan hai jab bachche ko samajh aata hai ki woh is baatcheet ke niyam nahi jaanta.   Mask neeche hua toh maine aankhein hata lin.  Mujhe bas uski muskurahat ka asaamaanya phailaav yaad raha.  Maine na poori tarah dekha, na poori tarah bach saka.  Usne phir sawaal poochha.  Is baar awaaz mein gussa nahi tha.  Intezaar tha.  Jaise uske paas poori raat ho, aur mere paas ek hi jawaab.  Sahi shabd ka intezaar.   School mein bachche uske kisse sunaate the.
