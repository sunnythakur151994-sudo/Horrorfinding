Har kisi ke paas bachne ka ek tareeka tha, aur doosra bachcha turant kehta tha ki woh tareeka galat hai.  Us waqt mujhe sab yaad aaya, aur kuch bhi kaam ka nahi laga.  Maine socha ki main bhaagoon toh?  Pair zameen par jam gaye.  Main jhooth boloon toh?  Pehla jawaab toh waise hi bina soche diya tha.   Mujhe sabse zyada darr is baat se laga ki main use naaraaz nahi karna chahta tha.  Hum bachchon ko anjaanon se bachna sikhaate hain, lekin saath hi har bade ke sawaal ka tameez se jawaab dena bhi.  Main dono baatein ek saath karne ki koshish mein wahin khada tha.   Door se cycle ki ghanti aayi.  Maine us taraf dekha.  Roshni ka ek tukda sadak par sarak raha tha.  Maine uske paar dukaan ka khula darwaza dekha aur usi taraf chal diya.  Pehle dheere, phir tez.  Aurat ne mera naam nahi liya.  Bas wahi sawaal mere peeche aaya, itna shaant ki usse meri jaldi aur ajeeb lagne lagi.  Agle din wahi jagah.   Dukaan mein log the.  Koi sikke gin raha tha.  Koi packet maang raha tha.  Maine pehli baar kisi bheed ko itni rahat se dekha.  Dukandaar ne paani diya, phir ghar phone kiya.  Is kalpanik ant mein bachcha bach jaata hai; yeh kisi lokpriya “survival rule” ki pushti nahi hai.   Agle din us jagah koi nahi tha.  Main doston ke saath nikla.  Unhone poochha ki chehra kaisa tha.  Maine kaha ki theek se nahi dekha.  Unhe niraasha hui.
