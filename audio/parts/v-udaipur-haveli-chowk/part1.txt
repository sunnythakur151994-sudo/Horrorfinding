Udaipur ki ek haveli ki teesri khidki peele parde se bandhi hui hai.  Kaha jaata hai ki 1800s me ek dulhan wahan se jhaanki thi, aur us raat ke baad ek bhi khidki nahi khuli.  Gaon wale kehte the woh dulhan raat ko wapas aati hai.  Peele parde.   Main andar gaya — aaj wahan ek boutique hai.  Teesri khidki ke peeche se ek kamra hai jisme kisi ki almari thi, ek purana palang, aur deewar par ek bada sa safed nishaan.  Dukaandaar ne kaha ki khidki kabhi nahi khulti kyunki wahan se dhup se purani lakdi kharab ho jaati hai.   “Dulhan” ki kahani mein ek hissa sach tha: us haveli ki ek bahu ki shaadi ke do mahine baad maut ho gayi thi, aur uske baad us kamre me koi nahi gaya.  Par raat ko jhaankne wali koi nahi.  Jo log “dulhan” dekhte the, unke hisaab se woh ek chhaya thi; asal mein woh ek chhota turist tha jo tasveer lene wahan chadha tha.   Haveliyan apni kahaniyan sambhal ke rakhti hain.  Unke parde bandh rehte hain kyunki unke logon ki yaadein bandh hain.  Unhe kholne ka haq tour ke paas nahi hai.  Jo khidki band hai, us par bhoot ka label chipkane se pehle, uske peeche ke ghar ke bare me sochiye.
