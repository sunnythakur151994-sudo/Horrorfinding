Konkan ke ek gaon me brahminon ka ek puraana wada tha.  Uska aakhri rahne wala puraani umar me chala gaya.  Uske baad ghar me kuch nahi chheda gaya — kyunki gaon kahentha tha ki jo bhi andar se kuch nikaalega, usse wahi cheez wapas maangi jaayegi.  Sindoor ka dibba.   Main wahan apni mausi ke saath gaya tha.  Mausi ek chhota dibba le kar aayin — usme sindoor aur ek peetal ki nath thi.  Raat me wahi barish shuru hui jo Konkan me ek hafte rehti hai.  Teesri raat wada ki taraf se kholi baji.  Subah darwaza khula tha.  Dibba gir gaya tha, aur nath zameen par.   Mausi ne baad me bataya ki wo dibba unke nani ka tha, jo usi wada me rehti thi.  Unhone use isliye liya tha ki unki beti ki shaadi thi.  Gaon waalon ki baat sunkar unhone use rakh diya tha.  Awaaz kisi ki nahi thi — wada ke purane kivad barish aur hawa se aise hi bajte hain.  Par us raat unhone sab wapas rakh diya, sirf ek yaad ke liye.   Konkan me ghar sirf imarat nahi hote.  Unme zameen, paani, aur purvajon ki chizon ka hisaab hota hai.  Yun “bhoot” keh dena aasan hai; par asli baat ye thi ki kisi choti si cheez se poori family ki yaadein judi thin — aur unhe wapas rakhne me kisi ka nuksan nahi tha.
