Osmanabad ke paas ek pahadi par purani dargah hai.  Raat ko wahan se ghode ki taa-pak sunai deti hai — kaha jaata hai ki woh ek peer hain jo aaj bhi apni pahadi ka chakkar lagate hain.  Gaon ke log raat me wahan nahi jaate.  Taa-pak.   Hum chowkidaar ke saath gaye.  Raat 11 baje awaaz aayi.  Woh has kar bole: “Dekh lo.”  Hum seedhi utre, aur wahan ek ghoda khada tha — dargah ke mutawalli ka, jo usse roz wahan baandhta tha.  Pathar par uske khaaron ki awaaz door tak jaati thi.   “Peer ki sawari” wali baat purani hai aur gaon me bahut izzat se sunai jaati hai.  Us raat maine kisi ki aastha par sawaal nahi uthaya.  Maine sirf itna dekha ki ek jaanwar ki rozana ki aawaaz ne ek kahani ko zinda rakha hai.   Wapas aate hue chowkidaar ne kaha: “Yahan log shraddha se aate hain, dar se nahi.”  Aur yahi farq hai.  Dargah ki raat ka kissa bhoot ka nahi, ek riwaaj ka hai.  Us riwaaj ne ek pathar par bani imarat ko 300 saal tak sambhala hai.
