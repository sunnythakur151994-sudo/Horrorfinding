Sardiyon ki raat.  Ek kamre mein do log, ek koyla ki angithi, aur band khidki.  Raat ke teen baje ek ko uthne mein dikkat hui — sar dard, ulti jaisa, dil ki dhadkan tez.  Doosre din unhone kaha ki kamre mein koi saaya tha, aur gaon mein baat phail gayi: us kamre mein bhoot hai.  Ek hafta.   Ek hafta kamra khaali pada.  Phir ek purane doctor ne, jo mahine mein ek baar wahan aata tha, kamre mein dekha — band khidki, angithi, aur thodi si kaali gardan.  Unhone sabse pehle khidki kholi.  Phir bole: “Koyla jalane se ek gas banti hai jise carbon monoxide kehte hain.  Wo dikhti nahi, sunai nahi deti, aur usi se sar dard, ulti, dhundhla dikhna aur ajeeb anubhav hote hain.”   Unhone bataya ki hadse aksar sardi mein hote hain, aur har saal log angithi se aise hi hote hain — neend mein hi, kyunki carbon monoxide ka pehla asar neend hi hai.  Aapko darr nahi lagta, aap so jaate hain.  Kamre ki deewar.   Us hafte jo “saaya” dikha tha, uski bhi wajah thi: deewar par purana choona, ek bulb jo angithi ke dhuen se pila pad gaya tha, aur neend mein deewar dekhta ek aadmi.  Aur jab ek kamre mein gas bharne lagti hai, to aankhon ke saamne dhabbe aana ek jaani hui baat hai.   Yahan sabse ajeeb baat kya thi?  “Bhoot” wali baat sunaye jaane ke baad, parivaar ne ek tantrik ko bulaya — aur kisi ne khidki kholne ka khayal nahi kiya.
