Nashik ke ek bazaar mein ek afwaah phaili: “do aadmi bachche utha rahe hain, kala jadu ke liye”.  Panch minute mein bheed ikatthi ho gayi.  Do aadmi — dono mazdoor, ek apne bhatije ko dhoondh rahe the — bheed ke ghere mein aa gaye.  Police ne bachaya.  Us raat unke ghar walon ne unhe pehchanne se pehle soch vichaar kiya.  Afwaah ka dhaancha.   “Kala jadu ke liye bachche uthaye ja rahe hain” — ye afwaah India mein pichhle bees saal mein sabse zyada jaan lene wali afwaahon mein se hai.  Iska dhaancha hamesha ek jaisa hota hai: ek ajnabi, ek bachcha, ek gaadi, ek “nishan”.  Photo nahi, naam nahi, gawaah nahi — sirf darr.   Iska sabse bada karan ye hai ki logon ko asli darr hai — aur wo darr sach mein hai.  Bachchon ki tijaarat hoti hai.  Bachche gum hote hain.  Par jo log is dhandhe mein hote hain, wo aksar pehchan ke wale hote hain, gaadi wale nahi; aur unka maksad paisa hota hai, jaadu nahi.  Do darr, ek galti.   Jab ek afwaah shuru hoti hai, to use rokne wala koi nahi hota.  Gaon mein ek aadmi “shak” mein aa jaata hai, aur poora gaon us shak ko sach maan leta hai.  Us shak ne itne logon ko maara hai ki police ko har saal is par advisory nikalni padti hai.   Aur asli bachav?  Wo photo, number plate, thana, 112, aur 1098 (Childline) se hota hai.  Bheed se nahi.
