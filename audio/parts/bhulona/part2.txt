Gaon ke kisse mein aise bhatkaave ko Bhulona ya Bhulauna kaha jaata hai.  Kahin ek adrishya saathi raasta badal deta hai; kahin ek hi mod baar-baar laut aata hai.  Koi kapda ulta pehenne ki baat karta hai.  Yeh kahani ke niyam hain, raasta milne ki bharosemand vidhi nahi.   Mere kalpanik raahi ko bhi dadi ki kahi baat yaad aayi.  Usne kurta kheenchkar dekha, phir haath neeche kar liya.  Use pehli baar mehsoos hua ki woh bahut tez saans le raha hai.  Kaafi der se chala aa raha tha, lekin soch nahi raha tha.  Har baar bas is yakeen ke saath mod leta ki abki baar ghar zaroor hoga.   Main kuaan se door ek saaf jagah baith gaya.  Lantern paas rakh li.  Paani ka aakhri ghunt piya.  Door se kisi engine ki awaaz aayi, phir band hui.  Maine apna naam zor se nahi pukaara; gaon ki taraf madad ke liye awaaz di.  Jawaab nahi mila.  Kuch der baad phir di.  Is baar bahut door se awaaz lauti.  Raasta subah ne nahi, aadmi ne dikhaya.   Mujhe lene aaya aadmi mera padosi tha.  Usne poochha ki main idhar kyun baitha hoon.  Maine kuaan dikhaya.  Usne roshni ghumaa kar doosri taraf ki med bataayi.  Main uske peeche chala.  Ghar itna paas nikla ki mujhe aur sharm aayi.  Usne meri lantern pakad li aur kaha, “Pehle khaana kha lena.”  Usne kahani nahi maangi.   Subah wapas gaya toh mod saaf the.
