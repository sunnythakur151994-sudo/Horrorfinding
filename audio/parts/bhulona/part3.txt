Raat ko jo ek deewar jaisi dikhti fasal thi, uske beech raasta khula tha.  Andhera aur thakan ek sambhav vyakhya hain; yeh kahani kisi vyakti ki medical jaanch nahi.  Bhulona ka apna lok-katha waala roop alag hai.   Uske baad maine us raaste ko chhod nahi diya.  Bas der ho toh apni pehchaan par itna ghamand nahi karta.  Mujhe kuaan yaad rehta hai.  Aur usse bhi zyada woh waqt, jab maine pehli baar chalna band kiya—aur tabhi ghar lautna shuru hua.
