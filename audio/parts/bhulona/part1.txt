Teesri baar kuaan dikha toh maine lantern zameen par rakh di.  Main wahi raasta chal raha tha jahan bachpan mein bina chappal dauda tha.  Din mein aankhein band karke ghar pahunchne ka ghamand tha mujhe.  Ab raat mein aankhein khuli thin, aur main apne ghar se door tha ya paas, yeh bhi nahi bata paa raha tha.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Bas ek mod aur.   Khet se nikalte waqt der ho gayi thi.  Maine socha, lamba pakka raasta kyun loon?  Med se nikal jaaunga.  Ghar par roti rakhi hogi.  Pehle mod tak sab theek tha.  Doosre par laga ki ped kuch alag jagah hai.  Main ruka nahi.  Thaka aadmi apni pehli galti maanne mein der karta hai, kyunki maanne ka matlab hota hai wapas chalna.   Phir kuaan aaya.  Uske kinaare ka toota hissa pehchaana hua tha.  Maine khud se kaha ki galat taraf nikal gaya.  Peeche muda, ek doosri med pakdi aur kadam tez kar diye.  Thodi der baad lantern ki roshni mein wahi toota kinaara ubhar aaya.   Is baar maine kuaan chhooa.  Jaise haath se jaanch lene par pata chal jaayega ki yeh pehla waala hai ya koi aur.  Patthar thanda tha.  Mere haath par nami lagi.  Main hasna chahta tha.  Awaaz gale mein hi atak gayi.  Apne hi kadmon ka ghera.
