Nuh ke us gaon mein kuaan ke paas raat ko jaana mana tha.  Hum paanch ladke gaye — isliye nahi ki hum bahadur the, balki isliye ki humein apne se bade kisi ki baat maan lena sharm ki baat lagti thi.  Vahan pahunch kar hum chup ho gaye.  Khet ke kinaare wala kuaan dhakka hua tha, par dhakkan adhoora tha.  Woh teen awaazein.   Pehle ek thi — jhaanjhar jaisi, dheemi.  Phir kinare ki mitti par kisi ke pairon ki khisak.  Teesri, ek aurat ki saans, jo chalne ke saath nahi, rukne ke saath tez hoti thi.  Humne torch maari.  Kuch nahi.   Bade ladke ne kaha chalo.  Hum uthe hi the ki kisi ne poora naap kar kaha: “Mujhe ghar bula rahe hain.”  Wahi awaaz mein, wahi muhavra, jo uske ghar mein use ghar walon ki thi.  Woh bhaaga.  Hum bhi.   Subah gaon ke chowk mein humne sab sunaaya.  Ek budhiya hansa bahut der.  Phir chup ho gayi.  Usne kaha ki 1962 ke baad is kuaan mein paani ka swaad badal gaya tha, aur gaon ke teen log usmein mare the.  Baad mein logon ko paani se darr hone laga, kuaan band hua, aur darr ko kahani ki zaroorat padi.   Khabar aise banti hai: kuch log sukh jaate hain, kuaan band ho jaata hai, aur phir woh jagah logon se khaali rehti hai — aur khaali jagah dheere-dheere apna kirdaar bana leti hai.  Par us raat ki baat main aaj bhi gaon waalon ko nahi batata.  Main sirf itna kehta hoon ki kuaan pe jaana band kar diya.
