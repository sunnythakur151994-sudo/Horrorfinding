Aur majboori wahi darwaza hai jahan se ye saara dhandha ghar mein aata hai.   Unke bete ne baad mein mujhse kaha ki unki maa ko sahi ilaaj ki zaroorat hai, aur wo mahine mein ek baar doctor ke paas jaati hain.  Ghar ki baat ghar mein rahe.  Par ek baat main zaroor likhunga: us kamre mein sabse zyada asli cheez unki takleef thi, aaina nahi.
