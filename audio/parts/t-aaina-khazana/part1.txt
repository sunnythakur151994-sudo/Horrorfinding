Punjab ke ek gaon mein ek budhiya mujhe apne kamre mein le gayi.  Farsh par ek thali, usme sarson ka tel, aur usme ek chhota aaina.  “Aankh band karo, khazane ki jagah socho, phir aankh kholo.  Aaina jahan andhera dikhaye, wahan khudai karo.”  Unki aankhon mein poori yaqeen thi.  Unke bete ne saat saal pehle ek zameen bechi thi, aur wo aaj bhi kehti hain ki paisa zameen ke neeche hi hai.  Jo dikhta hai.   Tel mein doobta hua aaina andhere ko laal aur dhundhla kar deta hai.  Aankh ko ek jagah tikane mein takleef hoti hai, aur thodi der baad dimaag khud hi aakritiyan bana deta hai — chehre, darwaze, siko.  Iska naam koi jaadu nahi, ye wahi cheez hai jisse aap badal mein jaanwar dekhte hain.  Jab aap pehle se “khazana” soch kar baithe hain, to dhundhle dhabbe ko khazana dikhna asambhav nahi, swabhaavik hai.   Asli khel aaine ke baad shuru hota hai.  Aaine mein jo “jagah” dikhi, uske aas-paas jo aadmi khudai karwata hai, wahi aapko “saamagri” bechta hai, wahi mazdoor lagata hai, wahi aapki zameen ke papers dekhta hai.  Budhiya ki baat.   Maine us budhiya se poocha ki aap aaina rakh kar ek din bhi khud khudai kyun nahi karwayi?  Unhone kaha: “Beta, khudai ke paise chahiye.  Isliye main doosra raasta dhoondh rahi hoon.”  Unke paas lalach nahi tha — majboori thi.
