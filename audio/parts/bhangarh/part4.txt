Woh mere jawaab se khush nahi hua.  Shayad use kuch aur sunna tha.   Raat ko tasveerein dekhte hue mujhe bazaar wali photo mili.  Usmein koi saaya nahi tha, koi chehra nahi.  Bas ek chabootra tha jis par kabhi koi baithta hoga.  Maine photo band kar di.  Bhangarh se lauta hua mera darr ab kisi bhoot ka intezaar nahi kar raha tha.  Woh soch raha tha ki itne saare ghar ek din aakhri baar khule honge—aur us din kisi ko kaise pata chala hoga ki ab lautna nahi hai?
