Maine haan kaha, lekin sach yeh tha ki mujhe sirf itna yaad tha: ek rajkumari, ek tantrik, aur ek shraap.  Usne kahani shuru ki, aur dheere-dheere saamne ke khandahar mere dimaag mein dobara basne lage.  Ittar ki woh sheeshi.   Lok-katha mein Ratnavati Bhangarh ki rajkumari thi.  Ek tantrik usse paana chahta tha.  Usne is chaahat ko pyaar ka naam diya hoga; lekin kisi ki marzi chheen lene ki ichchha mein pyaar kitna bachta hai?  Kahani ke mutabiq, usne rajkumari ke liye kharide ja rahe ittar par jaadu kiya, taaki uski khushboo Ratnavati ko uske vash mein kar de.   Main us pal ko soch raha tha: ek chhoti sheeshi, mahal ka roz ka kaam, aur usmein chhupa hua aisa iraada jiska kisi ko pata nahi.  Ratnavati ko chaal ka andaaza ho gaya.  Usne ittar patthar par phenk diya.  Kisse mein wahi patthar tantrik ki taraf ludhakta hai aur uski jaan le leta hai.  Marte hue woh Bhangarh ke vinaash ka shraap deta hai.  Alag sunane waale is pal ko alag tarah batate hain; har version mein ek baat reh jaati hai—apni haar ka badla woh poore shehar se chahta hai.     Uske baad ki barbaadi ko kahani shraap se jod deti hai.  Lekin meri nazar mahal se neeche basti ki taraf chali gayi.  Rajkumari aur tantrik ke naam sabko yaad hain.  Us aadmi ka kya naam tha jo yahan roz dukaan kholta tha?  Us maa ka, jo shaam ko bachche ko ghar bulaati thi?
