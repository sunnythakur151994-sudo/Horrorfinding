Main Bhangarh se laut aaya tha.  Phir bhi, us shaam ki ek chhoti-si baat mere saath ghar tak aa gayi: bazaar ke aakhri mod par mujhe laga tha ki koi mere peeche chal raha hai.  Main ruka, toh awaaz bhi ruk gayi.  Mudkar dekha toh patthar ki khaali dukaanon ke beech sirf dhoop ka ek patla tukda bacha tha.  Maine tab apne dost se kuch nahi kaha.  Kuch darr bol dene se kam hote hain; us waqt laga, yeh wala bol diya toh aur sach lagne lagega.   Yeh lok-katha se prerit rachnatmak punarkathan hai.  “Main”, samvaad aur vyaktigat drishya kalpanik hain; tathya aur seemaayein ant ke “Sach kya hai?”  mein hain.   Woh shehar jahan shaam jaldi ho jaati hai.   Hum dopahar mein pahunche the.  Paani ki bottle garam ho chuki thi aur mera dost har doosre darwaze ki tasveer le raha tha.  Main usse aage nikal gaya.  Dono taraf dukaanon ke bache hue chabootre the.  Kisi zamaane mein yahin kisi ne kapda naapte hue daam bataya hoga, kisi bachche ne mithai ke liye zid ki hogi.  Ab main unhi jagahon ko paar kar raha tha, aur mere joote ki awaaz itni saaf aa rahi thi jaise main kisi ke khaali ghar mein bina poochhe ghus aaya hoon.   Ek jagah main deewar ki thandi taraf baith gaya.  Dost ne camera neeche kiya aur poochha, “Ratnavati wali kahani suni hai?”
