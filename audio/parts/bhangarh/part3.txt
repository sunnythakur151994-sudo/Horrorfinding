Ek shehar ujadhne ki kahani mein sabse pehle aam logon ke naam kho jaate hain.  Ek aur shart, ek aur saaya.   Bhangarh ki doosri lok-katha Baba Balau Nath ki hai.  Unki shart thi ki mahal ka saaya unke tapasya-sthal tak nahi pahunchna chahiye.  Imarat unchi hui, saaya badha, aur shart toot gayi.  Phir vinaash aaya.  Yeh Ratnavati wale kisse ka agla adhyaay nahi, ek alag kahani hai.  Magar dono mein ek ajeeb samaanta hai: koi apni seema bhoolta hai, aur uska nateeja sabko bhugatna padta hai.   Maine saamne zameen par badhti deewar ki parchhaai dekhi.  Kuch der pehle tak woh mere jooton se door thi.  Ab ek joota uske andar aa chuka tha.  Main bina soche uth gaya.  Dost hans pada.  Maine bhi hansne ki koshish ki, phir use bottle pakda di.  “Chalein?”    Darwaze ke is paar.   Waapsi mein wahi bazaar lamba lagne laga.  Roshni kam hui toh dukaanon ke andar ka andhera ek jaisa ho gaya; pehle jo alag kamre dikhte the, ab sab ek gehri khaali jagah lag rahe the.  Tabhi peeche woh kadam sunaai diya.  Main ruka.  Dost kuch door aage tha.  Ek chhota kankad mere paas ludhak kar thehar gaya.  Ho sakta hai awaaz usi ki rahi ho.  Maine use uthakar dekha bhi nahi.   Darwaze ke bahar gaadiyon ki awaaz mili toh seena halka hua.  Raaste mein dost ne poochha, “Kuch mehsoos hua?”  Maine kaha, “Jagah bahut khaali hai.”
