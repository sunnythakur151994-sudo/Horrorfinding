Deoghar ke ek purane math me raat ko ek awaaz aati thi — “andar aa jao.”  Vahi awaaz jo mandir ke pujari bolte the, aur wahi unke baba ki.  Pujari ne kaha ki woh awaaz unke guru ki hai, aur unhe naam se bulati hai.  Andar.   Main andar gaya.  Wahan ek chhota kamra tha jisme ek palang, ek ghadi, aur ek baba ki tasveer.  Awaaz phir aayi.  Main darr gaya.  Phir pata chala ki awaaz kamre ke andar se nahi, wahan rakhe ek purane tape recorder se aa rahi thi, jo ek bhajan ka program record karta tha aur chhoot se bajta tha.   Pujari ne kaha ki unke baba ne woh recorder wahan isliye chhoda tha ki unhe apni awaaz yaad rahe.  Woh har raat tape chalu karte the, apni marzi se.  Par gaon me “baba ki awaaz” ki kahani chali, aur log wahan aane lage — darr ke, aastha ke.   Aaj us math me ek chhota sa satsang hota hai.  Log kehte hain bhoot nahi hai, sirf baba ki awaaz hai.  Maine pujari se kaha: “Aap ne sach bata diya.”  Unhone has kar kaha, “Main darr nahi, yaad rakhna chahta tha.”  Yahi farq hai.
