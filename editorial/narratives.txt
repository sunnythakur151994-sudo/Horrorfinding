@@ bhangarh
Main Bhangarh se laut aaya tha. Phir bhi, us shaam ki ek chhoti-si baat mere saath ghar tak aa gayi: bazaar ke aakhri mod par mujhe laga tha ki koi mere peeche chal raha hai. ### Woh shehar jahan shaam jaldi ho jaati hai
Hum dopahar mein pahunche the. Paani ki bottle garam ho chuki thi aur mera dost har doosre darwaze ki tasveer le raha tha. Main usse aage nikal gaya. Dono taraf dukaanon ke bache hue chabootre the. Ek jagah main deewar ki thandi taraf baith gaya. Dost ne camera neeche kiya aur poochha, “Ratnavati wali kahani suni hai?” Maine haan kaha, lekin sach yeh tha ki mujhe sirf itna yaad tha: ek rajkumari, ek tantrik, aur ek shraap. Usne kahani shuru ki, aur dheere-dheere saamne ke khandahar mere dimaag mein dobara basne lage.

### Ittar ki woh sheeshi
Lok-katha mein Ratnavati Bhangarh ki rajkumari thi. Ek tantrik usse paana chahta tha. Usne is chaahat ko pyaar ka naam diya hoga; lekin kisi ki marzi chheen lene ki ichchha mein pyaar kitna bachta hai? Kahani ke mutabiq, usne rajkumari ke liye kharide ja rahe ittar par jaadu kiya, taaki uski khushboo Ratnavati ko uske vash mein kar de.

Main us pal ko soch raha tha: ek chhoti sheeshi, mahal ka roz ka kaam, aur usmein chhupa hua aisa iraada jiska kisi ko pata nahi. Ratnavati ko chaal ka andaaza ho gaya. Usne ittar patthar par phenk diya. Kisse mein wahi patthar tantrik ki taraf ludhakta hai aur uski jaan le leta hai. Marte hue woh Bhangarh ke vinaash ka shraap deta hai. Alag sunane waale is pal ko alag tarah batate hain; har version mein ek baat reh jaati hai—apni haar ka badla woh poore shehar se chahta hai.

### Ek aur shart, ek aur saaya
Bhangarh ki doosri lok-katha Baba Balau Nath ki hai. Unki shart thi ki mahal ka saaya unke tapasya-sthal tak nahi pahunchna chahiye. Imarat unchi hui, saaya badha, aur shart toot gayi. ### Darwaze ke is paar
Waapsi mein wahi bazaar lamba lagne laga. Roshni kam hui toh dukaanon ke andar ka andhera ek jaisa ho gaya; pehle jo alag kamre dikhte the, ab sab ek gehri khaali jagah lag rahe the. Tabhi peeche woh kadam sunaai diya. Main ruka. Dost kuch door aage tha. Ek chhota kankad mere paas ludhak kar thehar gaya. Ho sakta hai awaaz usi ki rahi ho. Maine use uthakar dekha bhi nahi.

Darwaze ke bahar gaadiyon ki awaaz mili toh seena halka hua. Raaste mein dost ne poochha, “Kuch mehsoos hua?” Maine kaha, “Jagah bahut khaali hai.” Woh mere jawaab se khush nahi hua. Shayad use kuch aur sunna tha.

Raat ko tasveerein dekhte hue mujhe bazaar wali photo mili. Usmein koi saaya nahi tha, koi chehra nahi. Bas ek chabootra tha jis par kabhi koi baithta hoga. Maine photo band kar di. Bhangarh se lauta hua mera darr ab kisi bhoot ka intezaar nahi kar raha tha. @@ kuldhara
Us raat mujhe sabse zyada darr registan se nahi laga tha. Darr is baat ka tha ki subah hamara ghar wahi rahega, lekin hum usmein nahi honge. Maa ne anaaj ki potli baandh li thi. Baba darwaze ki chaukhath par haath rakhe khade the. Main apni mitti ki chhoti gaadi dhoondh raha tha. Jab mili, toh maa ne kaha, “Bas yahi le sakte ho. Aur kuch haath mein mat lena.” Bachpan mein ghar chhodna shayad pehle isi tarah samajh aata hai: apni cheezon mein se ek ko chunna.

### Faisla jo dheere bola gaya
Kuldhara ki lok-katha ko is kalpanik parivaar ki aankhon se dekho. Jaisalmer ke paas Paliwalon ki ek basti hai. Ghar paas-paas hain, aanganon ki awaazein ek-doosre tak pahunchti hain. Lekin is shaam baat karne waale bhi awaaz neeche rakh rahe hain. Bachchon ko jaldi khila diya gaya hai. Kisi ne bartan dhokar ulte nahi rakhe. Kaam ruk gaya hai, jaise sab kisi ek khabar ka intezaar kar rahe hon.

Mashhoor kisse mein hukmraan Salim Singh ki nazar gaon ki ek ladki par padti hai. Uski ichchha ke peeche hukumat ki taaqat hai. Ladki aur uske logon ke liye mana karna sirf ek rishta thukraana nahi reh jaata. Khatra poori basti tak aa pahunchta hai. Kahani kehti hai ki log aakhir saath chhodne ka faisla karte hain: apni marzi bachane ke liye apni zameen se juda hona padega.

Meri maa ne us ladki ka naam nahi bataya. Maine poochha bhi nahi. Darwaze par khadi meri badi behen baar-baar apna dupatta sambhaal rahi thi. Pehli baar mujhe laga ki bade log bhi kabhi-kabhi bina samjhe hue chup ho jaate hain. Unke paas jawaab nahi hota; phir bhi unhe bachchon ka haath pakadkar aage chalna hota hai.

### Jo saath nahi jaa saka
Baba ne chulhe ke paas rakha lota uthaya, phir wapas rakh diya. Potli pehle hi bhaari thi. Maa ne deewar ki ek darar mein ungli phiraai—usi jagah, jahan barsaat ke baad mitti bhari thi. Woh darar ab bhi thi. Ghar se nikalne se pehle ka aakhri kaam bhi adhoora reh gaya.

Bahar kisi ne dheere se awaaz di. Hum nikal aaye. Gali mein log the, lekin roz jaisa shor nahi tha. Ek bachcha neend mein apne baap ke kandhe se lagkar ro raha tha. Kisi ke haath mein chaabi thi, kisi ke haath mein paani. Ek budhiya ko do log sambhaal rahe the. Maine peeche dekha toh apna darwaza khula mila. Maa ne mera chehra aage kar diya. “Raasta dekh.”

Kahani isi jagah shraap ka zikr karti hai: jaane waalon ne kaha ki yahan phir koi bas nahi paayega. Main jab yeh kissa sunta hoon, toh mujhe woh shraap gusse se zyada dukh mein bola hua lagta hai. Jaise koi apne ghar se keh raha ho—agar hum tumhare nahi rahe, toh tum kisi aur ke kaise ho jaaoge? Yeh meri padhat hai; us raat ke asli shabd hamare paas nahi hain.

### Subah ko kaun bulaayega?
Hum ret par chalte rahe. Mujhe neend aane lagi. Maine maa se poochha, “Kal wapas aayenge?” Usne kaha, “Abhi chalo.” Tab main us jawaab ko samajh nahi saka. Bahut saal baad laga ki uske paas bhi kal ki koi tasveer nahi thi.

Kuldhara ke khandahar dekhte hue aaj bhi us kal ko socha ja sakta hai. Ek chhota kamra, deewar ke saath baithne ki jagah, bahar nikalti gali. Yahan rehne waalon ki zindagi sirf kisi shraap ki bhoomika nahi thi. Unka kaam, unki thakan, unke roz ke jhagde aur khushiyaan bhi thi. Hum khandahar ki photo lete hain; us ghar ki aadat ko kaise tasveer mein rakhein?

Ek hi raat mein poori basti chhod dene wala mashhoor version lok-katha hai. Bastiyan kyun khaali hoti hain, uska jawaab paani, rozgaar, dabaav aur badalti paristhitiyon mein bhi talaashna padta hai. Kuldhara ke liye koi ek saral wajah yahaan antim sach nahi kahi ja rahi.

Meri kalpanik yaad mein us raat koi bhoot nahi dikha. Bas subah ki pehli roshni mein maa ka haath dikha tha. Uski ungliyon par hamare ghar ki mitti lagi hui thi. Usne use jhaada nahi.

@@ mayong
Main Mayong ek ajeeb sawaal lekar gaya tha: kya sach mein kisi insaan ko gaayab kiya ja sakta hai? Chai dene waale ne meri taraf dekha, phir cup mein bachi chai daal di. “Pehle baitho,” usne kaha. ### Naam ke peeche rehne waale log
Assam ke Mayong ka naam jaadu aur tantra ki kahaniyon ke saath juda hua hai. Lekin jab hum kisi jagah ko sirf uski sabse daraawni kahani se pehchaante hain, toh wahan rehne waale log dheere-dheere nazar se gaayab hone lagte hain. Main bhi yahi kar raha tha. Dukaan, school jaata bachcha, kapde sukhaati aurat—sab mere liye raaste ki cheezein thi. Main kisi “asli” raaz ka intezaar kar raha tha.

Is kalpanik mulaaqaat mein mujhe ek buzurg ke ghar le jaaya gaya. Unhone koi pradarshan nahi kiya. Ek kapde mein lipti puraani pothi kholi aur kaha ki cheezein sambhaalna bhi kaam hota hai. Maine poochha, “Ismein kya hai?” Woh panne ko ungli se dabaaye rahe, taaki hawa use palat na de. “Jo humein mila. Jo humse reh jaayega.”

Mayong ki paramparaon mein bez kahe jaane waale paramparik practitioners ka zikr aata hai. Unse jude kisse kabhi upchaar, kabhi mantra, kabhi aisi shaktiyon ki baat karte hain jo rozmarra ke niyamon se bahar hain. In daavon ko sunna aur unhe siddh maan lena alag baatein hain. Par us kamre mein meri dilchaspi pehli baar kisi chamatkaar se hatkar ek insaan ke haath par aa gayi thi—kitni ehtiyaat se woh kaagaz pakad raha tha.

### Woh kahani jo adhoori chhodi gayi
Shaam ko baarish aayi. Chhat ke kinaare se paani gir raha tha. Buzurg ne ek raahi ka kissa sunaya jo apni manzil bhool gaya tha. Har mod par use apne ghar ki koi cheez dikh jaati: aangan ka ped, ek pehchaana darwaza, kisi apne ki peeth. Woh paas jaata toh kuch aur nikalta. Aakhir usne chalna band kiya aur subah hone di.

### Waapsi ka raasta
Baarish ruki toh mitti ki khushboo bahut tez thi. Mere saath chal raha ladka apne phone ki roshni neeche rakh raha tha. Raaste mein ek awaaz hui; main turant pedon ki taraf mud gaya. @@ shaniwar
Shaniwar Wada mein main ek darwaze ke saamne zaroorat se zyada der khada raha. Mere aas-paas log tasveerein le rahe the. Kisi ne bachche ko kinaare hone ko kaha; kisi ka phone baja. Phir bhi, Narayanrao ki kahani sunne ke baad mujhe har darwaza ek hi sawaal jaisa lag raha tha: jab koi jaan bachaakar aapki taraf bhaage, toh aap kis taraf khade hote hain?

### Mahal ke andar ka darr
Narayanrao ki 1773 mein hui hatya Shaniwar Wada ke itihaas ka hissa hai. Is ghatna ke aas-paas saazish, satta aur parivaar ke tanaav ki bahut-si baatein kahi gayi hain. Lekin us itihaas ko lok-katha mein badalne waali cheez ek pukaar hai: “Kaka, mala vachva”—Kaka, mujhe bachao.

Aage ka drishya usi riwaayat ka kalpanik punarkathan hai. Mahal ka ek raasta socho jise ek naujawan bachpan se jaanta hai. Kaunsa mod kis kamre tak jaata hai, kis dehleez par pair sambhaalna padta hai—use sab yaad hai. Aaj usi pehchaan par uski jaan tiki hui hai. Peeche kadam tez ho rahe hain. Woh mudkar dekhna chahta hai, lekin mudne mein bhi waqt lagega.

Us pal uska pad, uski gaddi, uska naam usse bachane nahi aa sakte. Use ek apna chahiye. Woh apne kaka ko bulaata hai. Awaaz mein hukm nahi, bharosa hai—wahi bharosa jisse hum bachpan mein kisi bade ko pukaarte hain aur maan lete hain ki ab kuch bura nahi hoga.

### Ek awaaz ka reh jaana
Mujhe is kahani ka yahi hissa sabse bhaari lagta hai. Hum aksar uske baad kya hua, uski daraawni tafseel dhoondhte hain. Lekin main pukaar aur jawaab ke beech ki jagah par atak jaata hoon. Woh chhota-sa waqt, jab bulaane waala abhi ummeed kar raha hota hai. Jab use abhi nahi pata hota ki madad aayegi ya nahi.

Lok-katha kehti hai ki woh pukaar Wada mein lautti hai. Log use raat se, khaamoshi se, kabhi poornima se jodte hain. Magar kisi awaaz ki kahani dohraayi jaana us awaaz ka pramaan nahi. Jo itihaas mein hua, aur jo baad mein uske baare mein mehsoos kiya gaya, dono ko ek hi cheez keh dena us dukh ko bhi badal deta hai.

Main jis deewar ke paas khada tha, us par dhoop sarak rahi thi. Ek chhota ladka apne pita se aage nikal gaya. Pita ne uska naam liya. Woh palatkar daudta hua wapas aaya aur unka haath pakad liya. Bahut saadhaaran baat thi. Phir bhi main use dekhta reh gaya.

### Ghar lautne ki awaaz
Baad mein jab hum bahar aaye, dost ne poochha ki mujhe kuch sunaai diya tha kya. Maine mana kiya. Main koi awaaz sunne nahi aaya tha, aur koi recording mere paas nahi thi. Lekin ek baat thi jo main seedhe keh nahi paaya: kuch jagahon par darr isliye nahi lagta ki wahan koi anjaan maujood hai. Darr isliye lagta hai ki kabhi wahan apne maujood the, aur phir bhi koi akela reh gaya.

Us shaam ghar pahunchkar maa ne doosre kamre se bulaaya. Main aam taur par “aaya” kehkar phone dekhta rehta hoon. Is baar turant uth gaya. Shaniwar Wada ki kahani mere saath kisi bhoot ki tarah nahi aayi thi. Woh bas ek chhoti-si aadat badal gayi: koi apna bulaaye, toh jawaab dene mein der mat karo.

@@ nishi
Maa ne mujhe pehli baar bulaaya toh main neend mein hi “haan” kehne waala tha. Awaaz aangan se aayi thi—bilkul usi tarah, jaise woh subah doodh lene bhejte waqt bulaati thi. Mera haath chadar se bahar nikla. Phir bagal ki charpai ki rassi hili. Maa mere paas so rahi thi. Uski saans ki awaaz mujhe saaf sunaai de rahi thi.

### Do baar mera naam
Main kuch der seedha leta raha. Neend ka ek hissa abhi bhi mujhse keh raha tha ki galat suna hai. Bahar baarish band ho chuki thi. Chhat se girti boondein aangan mein rakhi baalti par pad rahi thi. Uske beech dobara mera naam aaya. Is baar thoda dheere, jaise bulaane waali nahi chahti ki ghar mein koi aur jaag jaaye.

Maine maa ka kandha chhua. Usne aankhein khole bina poochha, “Kya hua?” Main jawaab nahi de paaya. Mujhe uski awaaz abhi bahar bhi sunaai di thi. Usne mera chehra dekha toh uthkar baith gayi. Maine darwaze ki taraf ishaara kiya. Hum dono chup ho gaye.

Bengal ki Nishi Daak ki lok-katha isi pehchaan se darr banaati hai. Raat ki pukaar kisi ajnabi ki nahi hoti. Woh kisi apne ki awaaz leti hai. Kisse ke kuch versions mein kaha jaata hai ki pukaar ginne se dhokha pehchaana ja sakta hai; doosron mein niyam badal jaata hai. Is kahani ke ghar mein bas itna sikhaaya gaya tha: pehle dekho ki tumhe bulaane waala hai kaun.

### Kundi tak ka faasla
Bahar se ek halki khurach aayi. Shayad geeli daali darwaze se lagi thi. Maine socha, agar koi sach mein madad maang raha ho? Baba ghar par nahi the. Kya woh laut aaye hain? Dimaag ko ek theek wajah chahiye thi, aur woh jaldi-jaldi wajah bana raha tha.

Main uthkar darwaze tak gaya. Neeche ki daraar se bas andhera dikh raha tha. Kundi thandi thi. Use chhoone se pehle maa ne meri kalai pakad li. Zor se nahi. Bas itna ki mera haath ruk jaaye. Main uski taraf muda. Usne mere paas aakar kaha, “Pehle yahin se poochho.”

### Subah ka aangan
Ujale mein aangan chhota nikla. Darwaze ke paas koi nishaan nahi tha. Ek daali neeche jhuki hui thi; hawa mein hilne par lakdi se lagti thi. Maa ne use hata diya. Main sochta raha ki aadhi neend, baarish aur pehle se suni kahani milkar kitna kuch bana sakti hain. Mujhe aaj bhi nahi pata ki us raat maine kya suna hota—kyunki yeh Nishi ki lok-katha ka kalpanik anubhav hai, kisi gawah ki report nahi.

Lekin kahani ka woh pal aasaani se nahi jaata. Andhere se koi tumhara naam le, toh tum awaaz se pehle rishta pehchaante ho. Nishi ka darr usi chhote-se bharose mein rehta hai. Kundi abhi band hai. Tumhara haath us tak pahunch chuka hai.

@@ naalebaa
Subah darwaza khola toh saamne waale ghar par safed akshar likhe the: Naale Baa. Dopahar tak gali ke chaar aur darwazon par wahi shabd aa gaye. Shaam ko maa ne mujhse chalk mangwaayi. ### Gali mein pahunchi khabar
Kannada mein Naale Baa ka matlab hai “kal aana.” Bengaluru se judi is shehri lok-katha mein darwaze par likhe yeh shabd raat ko aane waali aatma ko agle din lautne ke liye kehte hain. Jab woh kal aaye, tab bhi uske liye kal hi likha mile. Is chhote-se taalne mein kahani apna bachav dhoondhti hai.

Hamaari kalpanik gali mein khabar kisi ek aadmi ke paas nahi thi. Dukaan waala kehta tha ki usne paas ke mohalle mein suna hai. Paas ke mohalle ka aadmi kehta tha ki uske rishtedaar ke yahan hua hai. Kisi ke mutabiq aurat apne ki awaaz mein bulaati thi; kisi ke mutabiq bas dastak deti thi. Chehra kisi ne theek se nahi dekha tha. Lekin har agla sunane waala pichhle se zyada yakeen mein tha.

Maa ne likha toh chalk ka tukda beech mein toot gaya. Main use uthaane jhuka. Bahar se aata hua ek padosi ruk gaya. “Hamare yahan bhi kar dena,” usne kaha. Woh wahi aadmi tha jo dopahar ko sab par hans raha tha. Humne uski taraf dekha bhi nahi. Kuch darr aise hote hain jinmein ek-doosre ki sharm bacha lena aasaan lagta hai.

### Pehli dastak
Raat ko kundi hilli. Main turant uth baitha, lekin maa mujhse pehle jaag rahi thi. Doosri taraf se mere bhai ki awaaz aayi. Woh kaam se der se lauta tha. Roz ka waqt tha; roz ka andaaz bhi. Phir bhi maa ne darwaza nahi khola.

“Phone karo,” usne mujhse kaha.

Chori ki jaanch baad mein ho sakti thi; aag mein mar chuke insaan se baad mein koi sawaal nahi poochha ja sakta. Darwaza kholna aur chori karna ek hi kaam nahi. Ek ko doosre ka dosh dekar nyaay nahi hoga.

Kandhe par wazan halka hua. Hansi sunaai di. Vikram ko samajh aa gaya ki phir bol pada hai. Vetala ped ki taraf laut chuka tha. Raja kuch pal wahin khada raha, phir wapas muda. Uske jawaab ne kisi khel ko nahi jeeta tha. Phir bhi woh use wapas nahi leta.

### Aakhri mod
Paramparik katha-chakra ke ant ki taraf Vetala raja ko us tapasvi ki chhupi chaal se saavdhaan karta hai jisne use deh laane bheja tha. Raja aakhir sirf aadesh maanne se nahi, samajhkar faisla lene se bachta hai. Alag versions mein vivaran badalte hain; yahaan unhe ek hi aitihasik ghatna nahi maana gaya.

Mujhe is kahani mein ped se latakti deh se zyada woh baar-baar palatkar chalta aadmi yaad rehta hai. Thaka hua, jhunjhlaaya hua, phir bhi ek kathin sawaal ka aasaan galat jawaab dene ko taiyaar nahi. Vetala uski raah rokta hai, lekin usi raaste par uski soch bhi saaf hoti hai.

Raat khatam hoti hai. Kissa band hota hai. Sawaal phir bhi paas baitha rehta hai: agar sahi jawaab dene se tumhara kaam aur mushkil ho jaaye, tab bhi kya tum use bologe?

@@ monkeyman
Dilli ki us garmi mein hum chhat par sote the. Neeche kamre ki deewarein din bhar ki dhoop chhodti rehti thin. Upar kam se kam hawa thi. Phir ek raat pados ki chhat se awaaz aayi: “Aa gaya!” Main neend se utha toh pata bhi nahi tha ki kis se bhaagna hai. Bas sab bhaag rahe the. Kisi ki chappal reh gayi, kisi ki charpai ulat gayi. Mere pita seedhi ke saamne khade ho gaye aur bole, “Ek-ek karke. Dhakka mat do.”

### Jiski shakal koi ek nahi bata saka
2001 ka Delhi Monkey Man scare is kalpanik parivaar ki kahani ka sandarbh hai. Us daur ki reporting mein raat ka darr, alag-alag daave aur police ki jaanch milti hai. Lekin ek hi praani ki pakki pehchaan nahi milti. Kisi bayaan mein dhaatu ke panje the, kisi mein laal aankhein, kisi mein chhaton ke beech lambi chhalaang. Har badhti tafseel ne use aur aasaani se sochne laayak banaaya, zaroori nahi ki aur sachcha.

Mere liye uska pehla roop akhbaar mein bana sketch tha. Maine use der tak dekha. Phir shaam ko paani ki tanki ke paas ek kaali bori dekhi toh wahi chehra yaad aaya. Bori hilli nahi. Main use dekhkar bhi nazar hata nahi paa raha tha. Ek tasveer mere dimaag mein us jagah pahunch gayi thi jahan pehle sirf puraana saamaan rakha tha.

### Pahra dene waale bhi thake the
Hamaari gali mein log der tak jaagne lage. Koi torch rakhta, koi bas doosre ghar ki chhat par baithe aadmi se baat karta. Raat ke do baje ki baatein aksar bahut saadhaaran hoti thin—paani kab aayega, kaam kitne baje jaana hai, bachche soye ya nahi. Un saadhaaran baaton ke beech achanak koi poochhta, “Udhar kuch hila?” Aur sab ek saath chup ho jaate.

Us raat ki cheekh ke baad jab hum neeche pahunche, meri chhoti behen ro rahi thi. Usne kuch dekha nahi tha. Use bas laga ki main chhat par reh gaya hoon. Main uske paas baith gaya. Bahar ab bhi log poochh rahe the ki kaun aaya tha. Ghar ke andar sawaal chhota tha: sab aa gaye?

Pita waapas upar gaye, munder ke paas giri charpai seedhi ki aur hamaari chadarein le aaye. Unhone kisi praani ka bayaan nahi diya. Kaha, “Aaj neeche hi soenge.” Garam farsh par hum paas-paas let gaye. Neend der se aayi, par behen ne mera haath chhod diya.

### Jab shehar phir soya
Afwaah ka zor ghata toh zindagi apni zarooraton ke saath wapas aayi. Logon ko subah kaam par jaana tha. Humein bhi hawa chahiye thi. Hum phir chhat par sone lage. Maine tanki ke paas ki bori din mein khud hataayi. Usmein puraane kapde the.

Isse har us waqt ki shikayat ka jawaab nahi mil jaata. Darr aur choton ko halka kehna aasaan hai jab raat guzar chuki ho. Lekin khabar mein kisi daave ka chhapna, ya kisi ka ghabraakar gir jaana, us daave waale jeev ka saboot nahi banta.

Mujhe us daur ki sabse saaf yaad kisi panje ki nahi hai. Woh seedhi par khade mere pita hain—khud jaage hue, khud pareshan, phir bhi humein dheere utarne ko keh rahe hain. Dilli ki us raat mein hamaare liye sabse zaroori awaaz wahi thi.

@@ llorona
Nadi ghar se dikhti nahi thi, lekin raat ko sunaai deti thi. Bachpan mein mujhe lagta tha paani hamesha ek hi awaaz karta hai. Us raat pehli baar laga ki uske beech koi ro raha hai. Main khidki tak gaya. Chandni mein kinaare ka raasta safed dikh raha tha. Wahin, pedon ke aage, ek aur safedi khadi thi. Hawa mein hilti hui. Maine naani ko bulaaya. Unhone khidki ke paas aakar bahar dekha aur mera kandha apni taraf kar liya.

### Rone waali aurat
La Llorona ka matlab hai “rone waali aurat.” Mexico aur Latin America ki is lok-katha ke bahut-se versions hain. Ek mashhoor roop mein ek maa apne bachchon ko paani mein kho deti hai; kuch versions mein unki maut ke liye woh khud zimmedaar hoti hai. Pachhtaava uski kahani ka ant nahi banta. Uske baad shuru hoti hai talaash, jo maut ke baad bhi khatam nahi hoti.

Naani ne us raat poori kahani nahi sunaayi. Bas kaha ki nadi ki taraf nahi jaana. Main bistar par aa gaya, lekin kaan khidki ki taraf lage rahe. Rone ki awaaz paas aati, phir paani mein mil jaati. Mujhe lagta koi chal raha hai. Phir lagta, shayad main uske chalne ki aahat khud bana raha hoon.

### Kinaare par chhota-sa joota
Is kalpanik punarkathan mein saalon baad wahi bachcha bada hokar lautata hai. Naani ka ghar khaali ho chuka hai. Woh khidki kholta hai aur nadi ki awaaz pehchaan leta hai. Us raat bijli chali jaati hai. Kamre mein baithe-baithe use phir woh rona sunaai deta hai—bahut neeche, bahut thaka hua. Is baar woh bachcha nahi hai. Use lagta hai ki kisi ko madad chahiye.

Woh roshni lekar darwaze tak jaata hai. Raaste par ek chhota joota pada hai. Geela. Uske aage mitti mein paani ki dhaar kheenchti hui nadi ki taraf jaati hai. Woh kisi ko pukaarta hai. Rona ruk jaata hai.

Yahi khaamoshi use rok deti hai. Rona door se aa raha tha, lekin uske band hote hi use apne bahut paas kisi ki saans ka ehsaas hota hai. Woh roshni ghumaa kar dekhta hai. Koi nahi. Phir bahut dheere awaaz aati hai: “Tumne unhe dekha?”

### Ghar ki taraf
Woh jawaab nahi jaanta. Use pata bhi nahi ki sawaal usse kiya gaya hai ya raat se. Woh peeche hatkar darwaze ke andar aa jaata hai. Kundi lagaata hai, roshni mez par rakhta hai aur subah tak uske bujhne ka intezaar karta rehta hai. Bahar paani phir apni puraani awaaz mein behne lagta hai.

Subah raaste par joota nahi milta. Mitti geeli hai, kyunki raat mein baarish hui thi. Woh isse koi nateeja nahi nikaalta. Yeh kahani saboot ke saath band nahi hoti. Yeh bas ek kamre mein baithe aadmi ko chhod jaati hai, jo soch raha hai ki kisi ko dhoondhne ki aadat kab shok se badi ho jaati hai.

La Llorona ke is version mein aakhri darr uska chehra nahi hai. Woh ek sawaal hai jo sunne waale ko apni zindagi ki taraf mod deta hai: jiski talaash khatam nahi hoti, usse kaise bataayein ki ab ghar lautne ka waqt ho gaya?

@@ kuchisake
Woh aurat sadak ke beech nahi khadi thi. Bas kinaare, jahaan se main roz guzarta tha. Isliye pehle mujhe laga hi nahi ki mujhe rukna padega. Phir usne poochha, “Kya main khoobsurat hoon?” Main school ka bag ek kandhe se doosre par kar raha tha. Sawaal itna achanak tha ki mere muh se aadat mein “haan” nikal gaya. Usne mask ke kinaare par ungli rakhi. “Ab bhi?”

### Ek sawaal, do baar
Japan ki Kuchisake-onna lok-katha ka darr isi dohraaye gaye sawaal mein hai. Pehli baar jawaab dena aasaan lagta hai. Doosri baar lagta hai ki pehla jawaab hi tumhe phansa gaya. Uske naam ka sambandh kate hue muh ki chhavi se hai; is punarkathan mein uski graphic tafseel se zyada us pal par dhyaan hai jab bachche ko samajh aata hai ki woh is baatcheet ke niyam nahi jaanta.

Mask neeche hua toh maine aankhein hata lin. Mujhe bas uski muskurahat ka asaamaanya phailaav yaad raha. Maine na poori tarah dekha, na poori tarah bach saka. Usne phir sawaal poochha. Is baar awaaz mein gussa nahi tha. Intezaar tha. Jaise uske paas poori raat ho, aur mere paas ek hi jawaab.

### Sahi shabd ka intezaar
School mein bachche uske kisse sunaate the. Har kisi ke paas bachne ka ek tareeka tha, aur doosra bachcha turant kehta tha ki woh tareeka galat hai. Us waqt mujhe sab yaad aaya, aur kuch bhi kaam ka nahi laga. Maine socha ki main bhaagoon toh? Pair zameen par jam gaye. Main jhooth boloon toh? Pehla jawaab toh waise hi bina soche diya tha.

Mujhe sabse zyada darr is baat se laga ki main use naaraaz nahi karna chahta tha. Hum bachchon ko anjaanon se bachna sikhaate hain, lekin saath hi har bade ke sawaal ka tameez se jawaab dena bhi. Main dono baatein ek saath karne ki koshish mein wahin khada tha.

Door se cycle ki ghanti aayi. Maine us taraf dekha. Roshni ka ek tukda sadak par sarak raha tha. Maine uske paar dukaan ka khula darwaza dekha aur usi taraf chal diya. Pehle dheere, phir tez. Aurat ne mera naam nahi liya. Bas wahi sawaal mere peeche aaya, itna shaant ki usse meri jaldi aur ajeeb lagne lagi.

### Agle din wahi jagah
Dukaan mein log the. Koi sikke gin raha tha. Koi packet maang raha tha. Maine pehli baar kisi bheed ko itni rahat se dekha. Dukandaar ne paani diya, phir ghar phone kiya. Is kalpanik ant mein bachcha bach jaata hai; yeh kisi lokpriya “survival rule” ki pushti nahi hai.

Agle din us jagah koi nahi tha. Main doston ke saath nikla. Unhone poochha ki chehra kaisa tha. Maine kaha ki theek se nahi dekha. Unhe niraasha hui. Woh kahani ka sabse daraawna hissa sunna chahte the. Mujhe bas apne muh se bina soche nikli pehli “haan” yaad thi.

Kuchisake-onna mere liye uske baad ek chehre se zyada ek dabaav ban gayi—woh pal jab kisi aur ko santusht karne ke liye hum apni bechaini ko nazarandaaz kar dete hain. Kabhi-kabhi kahani padhte hue bhi main pehle sawaal par rukta hoon. Is baar jawaab dene ki jaldi nahi karta.

@@ blackshuck
Toofan shuru hua toh humne socha tha church ke andar surakshit rahenge. Darwaza band hua, geele kapdon ki gandh patthar ki thandak mein mil gayi. Bahar bijli chamakti toh khidki ek pal ke liye safed ho jaati. Phir andhera pehle se gehra lagta. Main bench par baitha apne haath ragad raha tha. Tab darwaze ki taraf sabke chehre ek saath mud gaye.

### Andar aaya andhera
Black Shuck ki riwaayat se juda 1577 ka Suffolk toofan aur uska puraana likhit bayaan is kahani ki prishthabhoomi hain. Neeche ka “main” ek kalpanik shrota-drashta hai. Woh aitihasik gawahi dene nahi, us bhay ki kalpana karne ke liye hai jo logon ne ek kaale kutte ka roop dekar sunaaya.

Darwaza hawa se hila ya kisi ne dhakela, mujhe is kisse mein bhi theek se nahi dikhta. Jo dikhta hai woh farsh ke paas ek kaali aakriti hai. Uska aakaar roshni ke saath badalta hai. Pehle lagta hai koi geela jaanwar ghus aaya. Phir woh beech ke raaste mein rukta hai, aur mujhe uski aankhein dikhne ka bharam hota hai. Koi mere paas prarthana ke shabd bol raha hai, baar-baar ek hi jagah se shuru karke.

Bijli ki kadak itni paas hoti hai ki main kaan dhak leta hoon. Kuch girta hai. Log hilte hain. Us ek pal mein main jaanwar ko kho deta hoon. Jab haath hataata hoon, mujhe apni saans ki awaaz bahut tez sunaai deti hai.

### Jo ek pal mein nahi dekha gaya
Baad mein sabke paas us raat ka ek alag hissa hai. Kisi ne aankhein dekhin, kisi ne panje. Kisi ko yaad hai ki woh darwaze tak gaya. Kisi ko lagta hai woh achanak gaayab hua. Main jab bolne lagta hoon toh mujhe apne hi bayaan mein khaali jagah milti hai. Kadak ke waqt maine aankhein band ki thin. Uske pehle bhi mujhe farsh saaf nahi dikh raha tha.

Lekin doosre log jab apni baat jodte hain, toh meri yaad bhi badalne lagti hai. Main sochta hoon, shayad maine bhi woh dekha tha. Yahin kahani dheere se meri aankhon se nikal kar sabki zubaan par chali jaati hai. Ab woh ek aadmi ki yaad nahi, poore toofan ki shakal hai.

### Darwaze par reh gaye nishaan
Black Shuck ke baare mein alag riwaayatein hain: kabhi vinaash ka sanket, kabhi sunsaan raaste ka saathi, kabhi door se dikhkar gaayab hone waala praani. Kisi puraane darwaze par nishaan ko uske panje keh dene se unka kaaran siddh nahi hota. Puraana likhit bayaan bhi humein batata hai ki kya likha gaya; har vyakhya ko apne aap sach nahi banaata.

Mere kalpanik drashta ke liye raat tab khatam hoti hai jab woh ghar ka darwaza khatkhatata hai. Andar se roshni aati hai. Uske ghar ka saadhaaran kutta uthkar uske paas aata hai, geele jooton ko soonghta hai aur phir apni jagah let jaata hai. Aadmi uske paas baith jaata hai. Kaafi der tak kuch nahi kehta.

Saalon baad bhi toofan aaye toh use church yaad aata hai. Lekin ab woh ek baat apni kahani mein zaroor rakhta hai: “Us waqt meri aankhein band thin.” Shayad isi chhoti imaandaari ki wajah se baaki kahani aur gehri lagti hai. Hum uske saath andhere mein khade hote hain, bina yeh daawa kiye ki humein sab dikh raha hai.
