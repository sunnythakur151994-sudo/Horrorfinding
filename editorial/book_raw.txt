Is kitaab ko kaise padhein
Kuch jagahen isliye daraati hain kyunki wahaan koi rehta nahi. Kuch isliye, kyunki
kahani kehti hai ki koi ab bhi wahaan hai. Yeh kitaab unhi jagahon, afwaahon aur
lok-kathaaon ka chhota sa safar hai.
Har kahani ko seedhe, bolchaal ke Roman Hindi andaaz mein likha gaya hai. Main
ghatna, ek yaad rehne waala daraawna kissa aur uske baad jaanch ka nateeja. Har
panna apne aap mein poora hai; kahin se bhi shuru kar sakte ho.
Yeh 60 verified supernatural incidents ki list nahi. Ismein lok-katha, urban legend,
literary ghost story, historical tragedy aur disputed paranormal case sab shaamil hain.
Har kahani ke neeche ka note bataata hai ki record kitni door tak saath deta hai.
Jahaan tumhe "socho", "kalpana karo" ya ek scene ke andar le jaati narration mile, woh
mahaul banaane ke liye rachaa gaya punarkathan hai. Use meri personal experience,
kisi naye witness ki gawahi ya documented dialogue na samjho.
Tasveeron mein asli jagahen, objects, historical artwork aur saaf label ki hui AI
illustrations hain. Koi building photo uske haunted hone ka proof nahi. Sources aur
image-credit links par click karke asal pages khol sakte ho.
Kuch kahaniyon mein maut, hatya aur beemaari ka zikr hai. Unmein real logon ka dard
bhi hai. Suspense ke saath unki izzat aur historical farq ko bhi jagah di gayi hai.
Ab pehla panna kholo. Bhangarh ki gali abhi khaali hai.

KAHANI 01 / 60
Bhangarh ka shraap
Alwar, Rajasthan, India
Asli jagah ki photo; yeh paranormal activity ka saboot nahi.
Bhangarh ke khandharon mein dopahar ki dhoop bhi darwazon ke andar poori nahi
utarti. Kabhi yahaan bazaar lagte the. Aaj unhi galiyon mein chalne par apne kadmon ki
aahat kuch zyada der tak saath rehti hai.
Sabse mashhoor kahani Rajkumari Ratnavati ki hai. Lok-katha ke mutaabik, ek tantrik
unhe paana chahta tha. Usne unke liye laaye ja rahe itr par jaadu kiya. Ratnavati ko shak
hua aur unhone itr ek patthar par phenk diya. Kahani mein wahi patthar tantrik ki maut
ka kaaran bana; marte hue usne nagar ke tabaah hone ka shraap diya.
Ek doosri riwayat Baba Balau Nath ki hai: unki tapasya ki jagah par mahal ki chhaaya
nahi padni chahiye thi. Shart tooti, aur nagar ujad gaya. Dono alag lok-kathaayein hain,
ek hi ghatna ka pakka itihaas nahi.
Log band kamron se awaaz aur kisi ke peeche chalne ka ehsaas sunaate hain. Lekin
Bhangarh ka sabse gehra darr shayad yahi hai: saamne poora shehar khada hai, aur
usmein rehne waala koi nahi.
JAANCH MEIN KYA MILA?
Paranormal investigator Gaurav Tiwari par prakashit ek report ke mutaabik, unki team ko Bhangarh haunted
hone ka saboot nahi mila. Raat ki entry par rok apne aap mein bhoot hone ka pramaan nahi. Itr aur shraap ki
kahaniyaan lok-parampara hain.

KAHANI 02 / 60
Kuldhara ki aakhri raat
Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, India
Asli jagah ki photo; yeh paranormal activity ka saboot nahi.
Kuldhara mein gharon ki deewaarein bachi hain, lekin ghar waali aawaazein nahi. Rasoi
kahaan thi, aangan kahaan tha, sab samajh aata hai. Bas yeh samajhna mushkil lagta hai
ki ek basti itni chup kaise ho gayi.
Mashhoor lok-katha kehti hai ki Jaisalmer ke taaqatvar deewan Salim Singh ki nazar
gaon ki ek ladki par padi. Uski maang ke saamne jhukne ke bajaay, Paliwal Brahmin
parivaaron ne gaon chhod dene ka faisla kiya. Riwayat mein chulhe bujhe, darwaaze
band hue aur log raat ke andhere mein nikal gaye.
Jaate hue unhone shraap diya: yahaan dobara koi bas nahi paayega. Iske baad ki
kahaniyon mein sunsaan galiyon se baatcheet sunaai deti hai aur khaali gharon mein kisi
ki maujoodgi mehsoos hoti hai.
Zara us aakhri ghar ki kalpana karo jiska darwaaza band hua hoga. Andar cheezein apni
jagah hongi; baahar poora parivaar safar ke liye khada. Kuldhara ka darr kisi dikhte
saaye mein nahi, us adhoori vidaai mein basa lagta hai.
JAANCH MEIN KYA MILA?
Gaon ka ujadhna haqeeqat hai; sabhi parivaaron ka ek hi raat jaana siddh nahi. Paani ki kami, badalta arthik
jeevan aur kar ka dabaav bhi itihaas ki charcha mein aate hain. Shraap ko historical conclusion na samjhein.

KAHANI 03 / 60
Shaniwar Wada ki pukaar
Pune, Maharashtra, India
Asli jagah ki photo; yeh paranormal activity ka saboot nahi.
Pune ke Shaniwar Wada ki deewaarein sirf shaahi zindagi ki gawah nahi. Unse ek aisi
pukaar bhi judi hai jo kahani sunaane waale aaj tak dheemi awaaz mein dohraate hain:
"Kaka, mala vachva!" - Kaka, mujhe bachao.
1773 mein yuva Peshwa Narayanrao ki mahal mein hatya hui. Is hatya ke aas-paas
saazish aur satta ki ladaai ka itihaas hai. Lokpriya riwayat mein pakadne ke hukm ko
maarne ke hukm mein badal diya gaya; us chhote se badlaav ne ek naujawan ko apne hi
ghar mein shikaar bana diya.
Kahani aage kehti hai ki Narayanrao bachne ke liye mahal ke andar daude, madad
maangi, lekin pahunchti hui talwaaron se nahi bach sake. Baad ki bhoot-kathaaon ne us
aakhri pukaar ko mahal mein qaid kar diya.
Kehte hain, kuch raaton mein wahi awaaz phir uthti hai. Koi dikhta nahi; bas madad
maangta ek shabd reh jaata hai. Socho, darwaaze ke us paar kisi ko tumhaari zaroorat
ho - aur tum jaante ho ki uski maut ko sadiyaan beet chuki hain.
JAANCH MEIN KYA MILA?
Narayanrao ki hatya itihaas mein darj hai. Hukm ke akshar badalne ki mashhoor kahani aur aaj bhi cheekh
sunaai dene ka daava alag star ke saboot rakhte hain. Ghost recording ya repeatable scientific confirmation
nahi mili.

Asli photo: Kurseong ka drishya; alleged headless boy ki tasveer nahi.
Kurseong ke Dow Hill mein dhund pedon ke beech is tarah tikti hai jaise raasta
jaan-boojhkar aadha chhupa diya gaya ho. Kuch door tak sab saaf; phir agla mod safed
parde ke andar.
Yahaan ki mashhoor kahani ek bina sir waale ladke ki hai. Jungle se guzarne waalon ke
kisse kehte hain ki woh achaanak raaste par chalta dikhta hai, phir pedon ke beech
gaayab ho jaata hai. Kisi ne use peeche mudte nahi dekha - kahani mein uska chehra hai
hi nahi.
Paas ke school se judi riwayaton mein chhuttiyon ke dauraan bhi corridor mein kadmon
ki aahat sunne ka zikr aata hai. Band imaarat, khaali kamre aur andar chalne ki awaaz:
itna sa khayaal jungle ke sannate ko aur bhaari bana deta hai.
Is kisse ko sunne ke baad asli mushkil kuch dekhna nahi, na dekh paana hai. Dhund
mein khada har patla tana kuch pal ke liye insaan lagta hai. Aur jab tum thehar kar
pehchaanne ki koshish karte ho, hawa uska roop phir badal deti hai.
JAANCH MEIN KYA MILA?
Headless boy aur school ki aahaton ke kisse sthaaniya folklore aur tourism reporting mein milte hain. Inhe
kisi pehchaane hue bachche ki pramaanit maut ya vaigyanik jaanch ka nateeja nahi maana ja sakta.

2001 ki garmiyon mein Delhi ke kuch ilaakon mein raat ko chhat par sona aam baat thi.

chamakti aankhein, kisi ne dhaatu jaise panje.
Kisse badhte gaye. Ek gali mein hamla hua bataya gaya, doosri mein kisi ne chhat se
chhalaang lagate dekha. Log pehra dene lage. Raat ki halki khatpat par padosi bhi uth
baithte. Jise kisi ne saaf nahi dekha tha, uska darr poore mohalle ko dikhaai dene laga.
Is kahani ka sabse bechain karne waala hissa yeh hai ki khatra sirf kahani ke praani se

pahuncha raha tha.

pair khud bhaagne lagte hain. Monkey Man ki raaton mein isi tarah anjaana darr,
afwaah aur asli chot ek doosre mein ulajh gaye.

Nishi Daak ki kahani mein tumhe daraane ke liye cheekh ki zaroorat nahi. Sirf tumhaara
naam kaafi hai - us awaaz mein, jis par tum sabse zyada bharosa karte ho.
Bengal ki riwayat kehti hai ki Nishi raat ko kisi apne ki awaaz banaakar bulaata hai.
Neend se utha insaan soche ki maa, bhai ya dost baahar hai, aur us awaaz ke peeche
chal de. Ghar chhoot jaata hai; andhera ghana hota jaata hai.
Kai versions mein naseehat milti hai ki raat ki pehli-do pukaar par jawaab mat do. Nishi
ki pukaaron ki ginti har jagah ek jaisi nahi, lekin kahani ka jaal wahi hai: pehchaan ko hi
dhokha bana dena.
Socho, tum aangan mein pahunch chuke ho. Ab awaaz gali se aa rahi hai. Gali tak jaao
toh kheton se. Har baar wahi apnapan, har baar thodi aur doori. Peeche ghar ka diya
chhota hota jaata hai. Phir ek pal aata hai jab tumhe yaad nahi rehta ki tum nikle kiske
liye the.
JAANCH MEIN KYA MILA?
